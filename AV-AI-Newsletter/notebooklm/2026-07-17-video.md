# AVS AI Dispatch — Video Edition Notes — Week of July 17, 2026

These are the sole production notes for this week’s AVS AI Dispatch video edition. Target length: five to seven minutes.

**Suggested episode title: “Built to Be Corrected.”**

## Audience, narration, and visual style

The audience is a team of audio/video production professionals inside the Audio/Video Services department of a faith-based organization.

- Never mention these notes or their instructions in narration.
- Keep narration family-friendly, professional, observational, and concise.
- Describe technology without mystical or supernatural metaphors.
- Label vendor, company, and paper-author figures clearly.
- Preserve exact dates, prices, specifications, sample counts, and product status.
- Put only short labels and important numbers on screen.
- Use flat 2D editorial vector illustration, warm parchment, one muted blue accent, clean sans-serif typography, and generous negative space.
- Avoid neon, glow effects, three-dimensional renders, and futuristic interface imagery.

Visually compare:

- Left: a flat pixel mask with limited correction
- Right: an editable spline with visible control points and locked artist keys

- **$599/year regular**
- **Separate Nuke license required**
- **90-day unrestricted trial**
- **Nuke 16.1+**
- **Recommended: more than 8GB VRAM**
- **Local inference**
- **Licensed training data**

### MultiRef-Compass

Key on-screen facts:

- **350 multi-reference samples**
- **8 audio-video systems**
- **4 dimensions**

Narration point: a polished frame can still be wrong when the references are not bound correctly.
