# Industry, Studies & Regulation Research — Week of July 20–26, 2026

Trade press, studies, benchmark data, regulation. Compiled 2026-07-27.

## Similarweb — 2026 Generative AI Landscape report (published during the week)

- **9.5B average monthly web visits** to generative AI platforms (Jun 2025–May 2026), **up 70%** from 5.6B; monthly uniques +57% to 655M.
- **ChatGPT share of standalone gen-AI web visits fell from >80% (late 2024) to just above 50% by May 2026.** Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, DeepSeek absorbed the difference (separate June 2026 snapshot: ChatGPT ~52–53%, Gemini ~27%, Claude ~9.2%). App-side MAU growth tells a different story (Meta AI +435%, Claude +349% YoY US).
- Audience overlap: 95% of ChatGPT users also used Google (measured Mar–May 2026; 494M ChatGPT users, 461M overlap).
- **AI Overviews at 43% of US searches** (volatile series; peaked ~40% Aug 2025, dipped ~27% Oct 2025, record May 2026). **Measurement conflict:** Adthena's June 2026 pulse recorded AIO in only 18% of US / 23% of UK searches. No industry-standard measurement exists; neither vendor has reconciled.
- **ChatGPT citation rate rose ~1.3% → 6.8% of answers** (Jun 2025 → May 2026). By industry: travel 22.6%, retail 13.5%, finance 8.0%, tech 6.6%. Cited-source mix: reviews/UGC 28.9%, news/publishers 26.0% of top 10k US desktop citations.
- **Citation vs referral disconnect:** 65% of cited URLs sit 2–3 folders deep, but 58.8% of AI referral traffic lands on homepages (post-May 7 ChatGPT search update: homepage referral share jumped ~25% → ~60%).
- **ChatGPT referral traffic converts at 7.1%** — second only to paid search (7.8%), ahead of organic, social, email, display (Similarweb clickstream, Apr–May 2026).
- **ChatGPT ad penetration: 26% of US desktop chats in June (14% in May); CTR 0.50%** (down from 0.68% in May — compression as inventory scales). Second measurement conflict: Adthena recorded **zero** ad placements across 169,560 UK ChatGPT scrapes in June while Similarweb shows UK penetration above 30%.
- AI recommendation → visits: recommended brand got **2–4x** the subsequent visits of the unrecommended competitor across six brand-pair tests (Jul–Dec 2025).
- Chatbot demographics flattening: 18–34 now ~50% of usage (was 61% in May 2024); 55+ grew 12% → 18%. Counter-current: noai.duckduckgo.com draws 33% of its US usage from 18–24s.
- Caveat: Similarweb's own disclaimer — estimations/extrapolations, not warranted for accuracy.
- Sources: PPC Land (https://ppc.land/chatgpt-loses-web-share-to-gemini-and-claude-as-ad-penetration-hits-26/); Similarweb report page (https://www.similarweb.com/corp/reports/2026-generative-ai-landscape/).

## WARC / TikTok / LIONS Advisory — "The New Creative Advantage" (published Jul 14; trade coverage Jul 22)

- 400 marketers (UK/US/AU/BR), fieldwork May 2026. **90% say AI is part of the creative toolkit; 88% report higher creative volume; only 45% report significant quality improvement.** 87% rate their own org's use effective (self-assessment bias noted).
- Briefing paradox: **67% use demographic data as the primary input when briefing generative AI**, while **59% agree demographic segmentation no longer works** (Marketer's Toolkit 2026). Only **17%** always incorporate community/audience insight beyond demographics.
- Named obstacles: generic visual styles 40%, unpredictable quality 36%, lacks originality 32%.
- Andy Yang (TikTok, Global Head of Creative & Brand Ads), foreword: the gap in AI-assisted creativity is **"not a technology gap, it is an intelligence gap."**
- Disclosure: TikTok co-commissioned; TikTok sells community/cultural signal.
- Related behavioral data (same week's coverage): Google ATLAS — AI touches 68% of jobs but only **21% of tasks at the median**; 86% of AI use is non-work. Adobe: workers estimate 32% of tasks could improve with creative AI, only 9% use it. MiQ (3,169 marketers, 16 countries): 72% planning expanded AI use vs 45% confident applying it.
- Source: PPC Land weekly (https://ppc.land/marketers-brief-ai-with-the-demographic-data-they-say-no-longer-works/).

## Fractl — AI search vocabulary study (released July 21)

- 343 US marketing decision-makers responsible for AI visibility. **81% still say "SEO"** when discussing AI search strategy internally; 19% use "GEO," 15% "LLM optimization," **10% "AEO."** 48% use "AI search optimization" — closest thing to consensus.
- Search behavior when hunting for help: 46% type "AI search optimization," 24% "SEO" (70% combined); **"GEO" 12%, "AEO" 3%.**
- **Heavy buzzword use is the #1 vendor red flag (36%)**, ahead of no case studies (21%), vague performance claims (20%), repackaged SEO with AI branding (16%). C-suite red flag is different: 33% cite "repackaged SEO with AI branding."
- Case studies outrank terminology fluency as a credibility signal ~4:1.
- **Budgets moving regardless of labels: average 24% of search/content budgets now routed to AI visibility**; 82% allocate something; 43% spend >20%; financial services leads at 39% average.
- Disclosure: Fractl sells content-marketing/AI-workflow services.
- Sources: Fractl (https://www.frac.tl/ai-search-optimization-language/); PPC Land (https://ppc.land/marketers-still-call-it-seo-as-81-reject-geo-pitch-fractl-finds/).

## Regulation — the AI-content labeling stack (deadline: August 2)

- **July 20:** European Commission published Article 50 implementation guidelines (C(2026) 5054 final) + finalized **Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content** (200-token watermarking threshold; deployers named liable). **Article 50 of the EU AI Act becomes applicable August 2, 2026.** Fines: up to **€15M or 3% of total worldwide annual turnover**, whichever is higher.
- **Advertising is NOT covered by the artistic carve-out** — Commission guidance explicitly lists an AI-manipulated video using synthetic humans to sell a product as not artistic. Assistive editing (retouching, color, cropping) exempt where semantics unchanged.
- **July 24:** Google announced it is signing the Code of Practice (two days after the July 22 signing date for the initial presumption-of-compliance cohort).
- Overlapping regimes: **New York** synthetic performer disclosure in force since June 9, 2026 ($1,000 first violation, $5,000 subsequent; audio exempt; expressive works exempt). **India** intermediary rules since Feb 20, 2026 (labels + provenance metadata; safe-harbour at stake; covers audio). **California** SB 942/AB 853 operative Aug 2, synced to EU. Staged EU deadlines follow: Dec 2, 2026 (marking conformity for existing systems), Feb 2, 2027 (watermark-detection interoperability).
- Platform plumbing shipped in pieces: Google AI label setting across 5 ads products (Jul 9), Ads API attestation structures (Jun 24), DV360 API attestation field (Jul 23), policy fix so AI labels don't violate text-overlay rules (Jul 22). Google's disclaimer: using the setting does not guarantee compliance. Liability sits with the advertiser in every regime.
- Audience context: IAB research — 39% of Gen Z report negative sentiment toward AI ads vs 20% of Millennials; consumer-executive perception gap widened 32 → 37 points (2024 → 2026). Adoption: 26% of surveyed orgs already use AI digital replicas, 22% synthetic talent.
- Sources: PPC Land (https://ppc.land/advertisers-face-5-000-new-york-fines-and-3-eu-penalties-over-ai-labels/ ; https://ppc.land/google-signs-eu-ai-code-as-advertisers-face-3-turnover-fines-august-2/).

## Measurement & other studies in the window

- **SE Ranking (via Search Engine Journal, July):** AI Mode returned a text ad on **29% of tested commercial keywords**; the advertiser's domain appeared among the answer's cited sources only **11%** of the time; exact URL 1.95%. Paid presence, citations, and organic rankings are effectively independent surfaces. (Caveat: keywords preselected to trigger ads; single-date sample; SE Ranking sells AI Mode tracking.) https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-ai-mode-shows-ads-on-1-in-3-commercial-keywords/582976/
- **AppsFlyer:** 58.6% of 157 US agency/brand leaders say they underinvest in channels AI systems cannot measure (social, CTV lead the blind spots).
- **LiveRamp simulations:** poor identity precision dropped measured ROI $1.50 → $0.43 (-70%); as little as **1% wrong data can reverse channel rankings**.
- **Semrush (519 US professionals):** brand recognition sways just **7% of B2B buyers inside AI answers** — precise use-case fit persuades instead; 84% use AI on deals ≥$1,000.
- **Semrush AI Visibility Index:** only **36 of 1,200+ tracked brands** achieve consistent visibility across every AI platform.
- **Time (via Digiday coverage of the week):** Time is producing text-based ad/content pages human readers never see, built for AI crawlers, and routes AI crawlers to a stripped-down copy of its site. COO Mark Howard frames it as serving two audiences. Cloudflare: bots >50% of web traffic; crawl-to-referral ratios from 118:1 up to ~50,000:1.
- **BCG (context, June 15, 2026):** survey of 300 CMOs — **90% agree GenAI is already reshaping how consumers discover and evaluate brands**; only 8% run campaigns where multiple agents operate autonomously; 42% use GenAI only as a task assistant; majority report setting up dedicated AEO/GEO teams. https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/making-the-agentic-marketing-transformation-a-reality
