## Google Ads
- **AI Max migration window opened Sept 1** and runs progressively through Sept 30: campaigns still using campaign-level broad match or standalone Automatically Created Assets convert **in place** (campaign IDs survive; AI Max is a setting, not a campaign type). Broad-match cohort gets search-term matching on; ACA cohort also gets text customization on (Google rewriting headlines/descriptions from site content). Brand inclusions/exclusions carry over. No opt-out of the migration itself; settings can be changed after conversion. Per-campaign timestamps via API v25.1 fields `campaign.aca_migration_date_time` / `campaign.broad_match_migration_date_time` (join with OR); flag `campaign.ai_max_setting.enable_ai_max`. Reporting: don't sum the classic search-term report with the AI Max search-term/landing-page view; check `expanded_final_url`. DSA phase moved to February 2027.
  - Independent evidence, both directions: smec measured a **median +13% conversion value with median ROAS difference of 0%** across 250+ retail campaigns; Lunio's IVT divergence (2.46%→5.28% vs 3.07%); Alphabet's paid clicks +13% in Q1 and Q2 2026 while CPC growth slowed to 3% (Choice OMG dossier, 66 sources).
  - Origins: [Google Ads Developer Blog mechanics via PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-blocks-new-broad-match-settings-before-september-1-ai-max-migration/) · [Improvado on reporting (Sept 2)](https://improvado.io/blog/google-ads-ai-max-september-2026-upgrade) · [Incisive Growth: "September 1 was not a soft deadline"](https://incisivegrowth.com/blogs/google-ai-max-migration-is-live-what-actually-changed/) · [Choice OMG verified dossier](https://choice.marketing/research/google-ai-max-auto-upgrade/) · [Digital Applied on API v25.1 fields](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/google-ads-api-v251-ai-max-migration-date-fields) · [SEL on the original email](https://searchengineland.com/google-to-auto-upgrade-some-search-campaigns-to-ai-max-484428).

- **Search Console generative AI performance reports and the Search generative AI opt-out control rolled out to all websites worldwide as of Aug 31** ("For real this time" — SER). Reports: impressions in AI Overviews, AI Mode, Discover AI by page/country/date (+device for Search); no clicks, no queries, no position; sites with too few AI impressions may not see it. Opt-out control: removes a site from AI features as links or grounding; "not a ranking signal" for core search; born from the UK CMA's June conduct requirement.
