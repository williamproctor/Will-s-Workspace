# Conversation Research (X / LinkedIn) — Week of July 27–August 2, 2026

Compiled 2026-08-18 (retroactive edition). Sources: PPC Land weekly synthesis (https://ppc.land/bots-overtake-humans/), Meta earnings coverage, Paid Media Collective Issue 224.

## 1. Meta's quarter: "paying off" vs. the sell-off

- Zuckerberg on the July 29 call: "On a dollar basis, our ads business is reporting faster year-over-year revenue growth than any other company's reported ad business — so these AI investments are paying off."
- The market disagreed on the framing: EPS $6.18 missed consensus (~$7.22) by ~14%; net income fell to $15.8B ($2.4B legal charge + $1.18B severance); operating margin compressed to 31% from an eight-quarter 40–48% range; capex $31.08B; free cash flow collapsed to $784M from $8.55B a year earlier. Shares fell roughly 8–10% after hours.
- MediaPost framed the investor question as "company diversification or distraction." Adweek noted Wall Street is no longer reflexively rewarding AI outlays, and quoted Zuckerberg extending agents toward outcomes-based pricing: "Effectively we will get paid when we deliver results for those businesses."
- Adweek also reported Meta ran its optimism-themed brand campaign (the July 23 "Future Is for Everyone" spot from our last edition) precisely because the company entered earnings week under pressure to justify 2026 AI spend.
- Discourse split: performance marketers celebrating the Advantage+/GEM numbers; finance/tech X dunking on the FCF line.

## 2. Musk confirms X stopped demoting links (public exchange with Paul Graham, July 30)

- Elon Musk stated X stopped demoting posts containing links "more than a year ago," replying to Paul Graham on X. Closes the era of burying links in replies — a workaround marketers built entire posting patterns around.
- Marketer discourse: whether to revert to links-in-post; skepticism about whether reach data supports the claim (Musk's statement is a platform-owner claim, not a measurement).
- Source: PPC Land weekly (July 30 item).

## 3. 77 companies sign letter against Anthropic's open-weights stance (July 28)

- Nvidia, Google, Meta, and OpenAI among 77 signatories accusing Anthropic of seeking an open-weights ban; Anthropic counters that its proposals target chip exports and pre-release testing.
- Relevance: model-lab politics is now brand positioning — the labs whose tools marketers use daily are fighting in public, and "which lab do you align with" is becoming a thought-leadership topic.
- Source: PPC Land weekly (July 28 item).

## 4. Smaller discourse items

- Jyll Saskin Gales (July 28, LinkedIn): found ChatGPT inside a client's Google Ads auction insights on exact-match keywords in niche financial software, present since May — first practitioner receipts that the two platforms bid in overlapping auctions.
- Reddit's Steve Huffman on the July 30 call: "AI Overviews has yet to make a similar level of positive impact," and direct app users are "worth multiples more than the search referral traffic." Stock fell 12.5% despite 64% ad growth — the traffic-quality debate in one earnings reaction.
