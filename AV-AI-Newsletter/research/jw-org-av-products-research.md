## 1. EBT (Essential Bible Teachings)

**Official Name:** Bible Videos — Essential Teachings
**URL:** https://www.jw.org/en/bible-teachings/bible-study-tools/bible-videos-essential-teachings/

### Content & Format
- Collection of short video lessons (approximately 3–5 minutes each) answering fundamental Bible questions
- Designed as educational tools for Bible study and outreach

### Known Episodes
- Was the Universe Created?
- Is God Real?
- Does God Have a Name?
- Who Is the Author of the Bible?
- How Can We Be Sure the Bible Is True?
- What Is the Purpose of Life?
- Why Did God Create the Earth?
- What Is the Condition of the Dead?
- Why Does God Allow Suffering?

### Production Style
- High-quality cinematic visuals with 3D animation and motion graphics
- Combines live-action footage, CGI environments, and animated sequences
- Short-form format optimized for digital distribution and Bible study sessions
- Produced at professional broadcast quality

### Target Audience
- General public / Bible students / anyone exploring Bible teachings
- Adults and older teens

### Translation & Distribution
- Available in multiple languages on JW.org
- Downloadable in various video formats (MP4, M4V, 3GP)
- Used as study aids alongside printed and digital Bible study tools

## 2. Dig for Treasures (Bible Book Introduction Videos)

**Official Name:** Bible Book Introduction Videos (likely the internal/working name "Dig for Treasures" relates to the midweek meeting section "Treasures From God's Word" / "Digging for Spiritual Gems")
**URL:** https://jw.org/en/library/videos/books-of-bible-intros

### Content & Format
- Short animated overview videos (approximately 5–6 minutes each) introducing individual books of the Bible
- Provide basic facts, key themes, authorship, time period, and how each book contributes to the Bible's central message
- Played during the midweek "Life and Ministry Meeting" as part of the "Treasures From God's Word" section

### Known Episodes
- Introduction to the Bible (overall)
- Introduction to Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy
- Introduction to Joshua, Judges, Ruth, Samuel, Kings
- Introduction to Jonah
- Introduction to Obadiah
- Introduction to Amos
- Introduction to James
- (Covers all 66 Bible books)

### Production Style
- Illustrated/painted art style with animated elements — uses rich, detailed digital paintings that are brought to life with subtle camera movements, parallax effects, and animated overlays
- Distinct from the 3D CGI used in EBT or the 2D character animation of Become Jehovah's Friend
- Combines narration with on-screen text, maps, timelines, and illustrated scenes from the Bible book being introduced
- Historically researched visual details (clothing, architecture, landscapes)

### Target Audience
- All ages — used in congregation meetings (midweek meeting program)
- Bible students and members preparing for weekly Bible reading

### Translation & Distribution
- Available in hundreds of languages
- Integrated into the Life and Ministry Meeting Workbook schedule
- Downloadable from JW.org

## 3. Become Jehovah's Friend (pk2D)

**Official Name:** Become Jehovah's Friend
**Internal Code:** pk2D (likely "pk" = publication kids, "2D" = 2D animation style)
**URLs:**
- Season 1: https://www.jw.org/en/library/videos/Become-Jehovahs-Friend/
- Season 2: https://www.jw.org/en/library/videos/become-jehovahs-friend-2/

### Content & Format
- Animated children's series teaching Bible principles and moral lessons
- Includes Video Lessons, Songs, and Activities
- Episodes average ~2 minutes runtime
- Season 1: 53 episodes (June 2012 – March 2021) + 51 specials
- Season 2: Ongoing with additional lessons

### Main Characters
- **Caleb** — young boy, primary character
- **Sophia** — young girl, Caleb's sister
- **Zoe** — additional character
- Parents and other family/community members

### Known Episode Titles
- Jehovah Is a Real Person
- Learn From Jehovah's Friends — Mephibosheth
- Caleb and Sophia Visit Bethel
- The Steps to Baptism
- Episodes covering: obedience, kindness, forgiveness, prayer, friendship, faith, pioneering, Jesus' sacrifice

### Production Style
- **2D animation** — character-driven animated series with a warm, child-friendly art style
- Colorful, simple character designs optimized for young children
- Includes both narrative episodes and musical/song segments
- Behind-the-scenes production content has been created showing the animation process
- Production involves scriptwriting, storyboarding, voice recording, and full animation pipeline

### Target Audience
- Children (primary: ages 3–10)
- Families

### Reception & Impact
- Translated into **130+ languages**
- Children worldwide reported memorizing entire episodes including songs and narration
- Parents and children have written extensively about positive reception
- Photos shared globally of children engaging with the content
- Described as touching "little hearts" — one of JW.org's most popular and recognizable series

### Translation & Distribution
- Available on JW.org in multiple formats: MP3 audio, MP4/M4V/3GP video
- Downloadable for offline viewing
- Used in family worship and personal study

## JW.org Video Production — Scale & Infrastructure

### Current Production Scale

| Metric | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Website Languages** | 1,000+ languages (including 100+ sign languages) |
| **Home Page Languages** | 821 languages (most translated website in the world) |
| **Video Translation** | Individual videos available in up to 569 languages |
| **Monthly Views** | 10+ million views across all video content monthly |
| **JW Broadcasting Views** | ~8.6 million views/downloads per monthly program release |
| **Global Reach** | Content viewed in 230+ countries |
| **Sign Languages** | ~30 sign language video publications |
| **Original Content** | 60+ hours of original programming produced |

### Production Facilities

#### Current: Patterson Bethel (Watchtower Educational Center), New York
- Houses the **Audio/Video Services Department** (under Teaching Committee oversight)
- Full production facility: recording studios, film crews, editing suites
- Produces: audio Bible recordings, convention dramas, music, video programs, sign language content
- International filming capability (crews deployed to locations like Denmark, Australia)

#### Current: Brooklyn / Warwick Headquarters
- JW Broadcasting main studio (originally at 30 Columbia Heights, Brooklyn)
- Studio built in ~2 months (industry estimate: 18 months)
- Monthly broadcast production
- Coordination hub for global translation work

#### Under Construction: Ramapo Audio/Video Production Center (Sterling Mine Road)
- **249-acre property** in Ramapo, New York
- Purpose: Central hub for all audio/video content production
- **Key features:**
  - Flexible film studios with **virtual production sets** (capable of recreating any global location without travel)
  - **Art and Animation Studios** (dedicated spaces for animation production)
  - Creative workshops for photography, art, and set design
  - Music production facilities
  - Central energy plant
  - 600 residential units for volunteer staff (up to 850 additional volunteers in later phases)
  - Multi-use event facility (1,200 capacity, converts between dining hall and auditorium)
  - Offices, maintenance workshops, recreation/wellness facilities, clinic
- **Timeline:**
  - Phase 1 (Planning Approval): Completed June 2023
  - Phase 2 (Land Development): In progress as of early 2025 (est. 18 months)
  - Phase 3 (Building Construction): Est. 38 months
  - Foundation pouring began: November 2025 (parking garage), December 2025 (first residence building)

#### Regional: Australia Film Sets
- "The Good News According to Jesus" — 18-episode dramatization of Jesus' life
- 500+ volunteers built 7,000+ sq meters (75,347 sq ft) of historically accurate film sets
- Estimated 1,000-minute epic — filming began May 2022 after 3 years of planning
- Includes dedicated support facilities: kitchen, dining, offices, hair/makeup studios, animal pens

### Production Pipeline & Workflow

#### Video Production
1. Script development (based on Bible accounts, reviewed for accuracy)
2. Storyboarding with rough drawings for sequence and design
3. Casting, costume design, color scheme planning
4. Filming (studio and/or on-location)
5. Post-production (editing, VFX, sound design)
6. Quality review

#### Animation Production
1. Script and concept development
2. Storyboarding
3. Character design and modeling (2D or 3D depending on series)
4. Animation
5. Voice recording
6. Compositing and final render
7. Quality review

#### Translation & Dubbing Pipeline
1. Script translation by local translation teams (collaborative, meaning-focused)
2. Native speaker selection for voice work
3. Audio recording (at branch offices, Kingdom Halls, or portable studios)
4. Video editing with translated audio
5. On-screen text processing (translated titles, captions)
6. Final assembly and upload to JW.org
- Portable recording systems (microphone + laptop with audio software) deployed to remote locations
- This approach enabled production in **3x as many languages** compared to previous methods
- Central America example: 2,500 volunteers, 20+ recording locations, 5 countries, 38 indigenous languages + Spanish

### Volunteer Workforce
- All production staff are **unpaid volunteers** (Bethel members)
- Volunteers serve full-time, averaging 44+ hours/week
- Live on-site at Bethel facilities
- International teams assembled for major productions
- The Ramapo facility will house hundreds of additional media production volunteers

## AI Usage in JW.org Production

### Current Evidence
- **No public evidence** of generative AI being used in video/animation production
- The organization's documented approach emphasizes **human translators, native speakers, and volunteer craftspeople**
- Translation work specifically prioritizes human expertise: teams work collaboratively to ensure material reads naturally rather than like a machine translation
- JW.org has published educational content about AI from a biblical perspective ("Artificial Intelligence — A Blessing or a Curse?")

### Third-Party AI Tools
- **JW.org Content Tools** exist on platforms like Pickaxe for workflow automation (fetching video captions, accessing workbook content, retrieving study materials) — these appear to be third-party integrations, not official JW.org AI production tools

### Future Potential
- The Ramapo facility's **virtual production sets** suggest adoption of modern production technology (LED volumes, real-time rendering)
- The scale of translation (1,000+ languages) and content volume creates strong potential use cases for AI-assisted workflows:
  - Automated lip-sync for dubbed content
  - AI-assisted translation drafts (with human review)
  - Automated subtitle generation
  - AI-enhanced animation workflows
- However, no official statements confirm AI adoption in production pipelines
