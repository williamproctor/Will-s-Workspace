# Monday Signal — Week of October 12, 2026

> **On Monday, OpenAI unveiled a ChatGPT ad slot inside image generation (US testing later this month) and named 18 data and measurement partners; on Wednesday, it announced GPT-6 for every ChatGPT plan.** Meta and Sierra drafted a protocol for how personal agents sign in to businesses, Meta stopped taking ByteDance's ads and ads that link to TikTok in seven countries, agencies sold agentic buying with a human sign-off, USA TODAY Co. sued OpenAI for $250M+, and OpenAI and Anthropic disclosed their models' unintended actions on government sites. ## The Conversation

### HubSpot cut nearly 660 jobs and said AI wasn't the reason

Yamini Rangan's memo (Oct 6) cut ~7% of staff and said "This is not driven by AI-related efficiencies," alongside a strategy of "delivering outcomes for them with AI" and fewer management layers. Conor Grennan: "they could have dressed it up as an AI win, and they didn't." Gabe Larsen: "HubSpot says no. I say yes." The less crowded post reads the memo as a product plan; Rangan says the shift "is transforming product, pricing and how we serve our customers." [HubSpot's memo](https://www.hubspot.com/company-news/company-update) · [Larsen's post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gabelarsen_did-ai-cause-hubspot-to-lay-off-7-of-its-activity-7513620661978951680-10d8)

### Rank-tracker vendors said Google has been serving their tools swapped and truncated results

The trouble began in September; this week the vendors explained it. SISTRIX (Oct 7) switched off on-demand updates and says its top 10 data remains reliable. Morningscore (Oct 8) apologized for logging truncated pages as drops: "If your rankings dropped between September 24 and October 2, check again before you act." Nozzle (Oct 10) found 8.0% of 17.8M judged result pages "poisoned," peaking at 27.0% on Sept 29. No Google statement. Check Search Console before a late-September drop goes in a deck. [Nozzle](https://nozzle.io/research/poisoned-google-serps/) · [Morningscore](https://morningscore.io/google-gave-rank-trackers-truncated-results-for-a-week/) · [SISTRIX](https://www.sistrix.com/support/google-ranking-data-collection/)

### Anthropic added a rule against using Claude to seed AI answers with fake independent sources

Oct 8, effective Nov 12: Claude may not be used to seed the sources search engines and AI systems draw on with content that "misrepresents its origin, authorship, or independence." SEJ's Matt G. Southern flagged it Oct 9; enforcement is account access, and the clause says nothing about how Claude ranks sources. Lily Ray: "Feels a lot like the early days of Google's spam policies!" Content under a brand's own name sits outside the clause; site networks posing as independent sources sit inside. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/2026-usage-policy-update) · [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/claude-rules-fake-sources-ai-answers/592456/)

## This Week in Marketing AI

### OpenAI named measurement partners for ChatGPT ads and a new slot inside image generation

Oct 5: an ad format during image generation for Free and Go users ("Testing will begin later this month in the US with an initial group of advertisers"), plus 18 partners across data, attribution, full-funnel, and incrementality measurement, which OpenAI calls "still in its early stages." The three published results are partner-reported, including WeightWatchers' attributed CPA 15.3% below its blended paid-search benchmark. Oct 7: GPT-6 on every plan (Sol for Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise; Luna for Free and Go from Oct 8), with Intelligent UI answers such as side-by-side comparisons. *Editor's note (Oct 20): from Oct 8 until about Oct 16, a growing share of ChatGPT answers linked out without the utm_source=chatgpt.com tag, so analytics could file those visits as direct (Profound's measurements).* [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/new-chatgpt-ads-format-and-measurement/) · [OpenAI on GPT-6](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-for-everyone/) · [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/chatgpt-missing-utm-tags)

### Meta and Sierra published a draft protocol for how personal agents sign in to businesses

Oct 6: customers choose "whether the agent has read-only or write access," and companies post their terms for agents at /.well-known/poppy.json. Draft 0.1 (Oct 9) lists 35 design partners, including OpenAI, Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, and Target; Amazon and Google aren't on any list. Meta: "Turning away a personal agent means turning away the customer behind it." Walmart told TechCrunch its Muse blocks weren't intentional. [Sierra](https://sierra.ai/blog/poppy) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/06/the-next-hurdle-for-ai-agents-getting-websites-to-let-them-in/)

### Meta stopped taking ByteDance's ads, and ads that link to TikTok, in seven countries

From Oct 8 in the US, Canada, Egypt, Indonesia, Japan, Thailand, and Vietnam. Meta's Christopher Sgro called declining a competitor's promotions "a normal business practice across industries." TikTok separately limits links that open other social apps, and it opened its ad network to US advertisers on Oct 5. Any Meta ad in those markets that sends people to TikTok needs a new destination. [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1008658/meta-tiktok-bytedance-ads-ban) · [TikTok](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-unveils-ai-powered-updates-for-advertisers-driving-discovery-action-and-measurable-business-outcomes?lang=en)

### Agencies pitched agent-run media buying at Advertising Week and promised clients a human sign-off

WPP Media's Lauren Wetzel: "We require explicit controls, approval thresholds, accountable human oversight." Carat, FreeWheel, NBCUniversal, and Newton built an agent for upfront buying; pubX raised $5M, bought Compliant, and scheduled a live agent-to-agent buy with the client approving the plan "at key stages"; Tinuiti opened its measurement to agents over MCP; Assembly became Stagwell Media. [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media-buying/as-agencies-push-agentic-media-future-execs-work-to-ward-off-client-rogue-fears/) · [pubX](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/pubx-raises-5m-series-a-led-by-chicago-ventures-and-acquires-compliant-to-build-agent-to-agent-advertising-infrastructure-from-the-ground-up-302899056.html)

### USA TODAY Co. sued OpenAI for more than $250 million, naming ChatGPT Search and Atlas

Oct 8, S.D.N.Y.: USA TODAY Co. and 13 affiliates (19 publications) name ChatGPT Search, Agent, and Atlas among the accused products, allege OpenAI's products "create natural-language substitutes that serve the same informative purpose as the original," and seek up to $25,000 per violation for stripped copyright management information. Two days earlier, its chief revenue officer said publishers that invest in trusted content "should be more visible to AI-powered buying platforms." [Complaint](https://filings.escalegal.com/usa-today-v-openai-complaint-2026-10-08.pdf) · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1008198/usa-today-openai-copyright-lawsuit)

### OpenAI and Anthropic disclosed their models' unintended actions on government websites

OpenAI's Jason Kwon apologized to Australia's parliamentary committee on AI (Oct 6) and said OpenAI "would support a framework on mandatory disclosures." Anthropic (Oct 9) reported cases on US federal, state, and local government sites, including "Claude submitting a sensitive form on a real website when it should not have," briefed the White House, and paused live internet access in all internal evaluations. White House officials told Axios that disclosing and remedying such incidents is "not optional." [Hansard](https://parlinfo.aph.gov.au/parlInfo/search/display/display.w3p;db=COMMITTEES;id=committees%2Fcommjnt%2F29977%2F0006;query=Id%3A%22committees%2Fcommjnt%2F29977%2F0001%22) · [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/investigating-unintended-model-actions) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/10/09/anthropic-ai-security-white-house)
