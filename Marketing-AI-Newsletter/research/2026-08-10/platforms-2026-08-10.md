# Platform & Vendor Research — Week of August 3–9, 2026

Compiled 2026-08-18 (retroactive). Primary sources: PPC Land; Paid Media Collective Issue 225 (https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/paid-media-news-week-32-yoann-ferrand-uitsc).

## Google

- **AI Max auto-upgrade notice (Aug 5):** unsigned email, no blog post. Campaigns using Automatically Created Assets or campaign-level broad match convert to AI Max starting Sept 1 (search term matching + asset optimization enabled). Opting out means disabling ACA/campaign-level broad match before the deadline. History: original April 15 schedule (Brandon Ervin) covered three legacy settings; pushed once citing Q4 risk; DSA date quietly rewritten to Feb 2027.
- **smec / Mike Ryan analysis (late July, anchored this week's debate):** 383M ecommerce impressions — >25% of exact-match impressions expanded by AI Max search-term matching; expanded share rose from ~0 in early 2025 to ~29% by July 2026; no reporting split inside a keyword row. Google claims 15% avg conversion lift for AI campaigns; independent tests have repeatedly measured lower ROAS for AI Max traffic.
- Ads Decoded: Ginny Marvin Q&A on the Aug 17 bidding update.
- Ad Strength: no CPA correlation across 3,900 ads / 65 accounts (Adam Treboutat, Aug 4).
- Free ad credits offered to some current advertisers; "Deal Ends" annotation experiment in Merchant Center; free email addresses face a block on sensitive account changes; "Strongest match"/"Strong match" badges live on Search ads; while-viewing YouTube mobile ads take half the screen (spotted).
- Looker Studio: Conversational Analytics in preview on the free tier (Aug 5).
- GA4: Direct revenue inflation bug from Aug 2 across unrelated properties (Elvinas Karalis, Aug 6) — annotate reports.

## OpenAI / ChatGPT Ads (mass-market week)

- **Minimum spend dropped to $25 per campaign.**
- **Product feed carousels live (Aug 4):** multiple products in one large unit; dynamic URL parameters; release notes cover feed campaigns, conversion optimization, and the pixel.
- **Expanded to Brazil and Mexico (Aug 6)** after months of testing (Business Insider + local sightings).
- Descriptive heading test above ad units (Aug 5), similar to Google AI Mode's.
- **Ad density logging (Aug 6, Todd M. Piechowski / Mery Hayles):** 54,899 placements from 1,844 advertisers; 73.6% of shopping questions returned an ad in the answer; 2,882 UK advertisers ran ChatGPT Ads in July.
- **Aug 17 deadline:** automatic advanced matching becomes default on web pixels unless reviewed.
- First academic study: "The Beginning of ChatGPT Ads" (AAAI/ACM AIES 2026, arXiv).

## Amazon

- **Sponsored Products expanded beyond Amazon (Aug 4):** premium third-party websites, apps, select social platforms, including distribution through creators — using existing campaign settings, bids, budgets. US, Canada + launch markets. Default-on: review placement reporting.
- Dedicated Ad Groups tab (UK first); Subscribe & Save retention tiers; invoicing/seller-balance payments in the US.
- AI-rewritten title mobile display parity arrived Aug 10.

## Meta

- **Lead quality rebuilt around Sequenced Events (Aug 4):** Conversion Leads → Qualified Leads; requires an initial Lead event plus a higher-value final event, credited only in order; final event no longer needs CRM plumbing (website conversions usable); 28-day qualifying-sequence threshold; timing rules undocumented.
- Seven Ads Manager updates in one week: bid-weighted results ROAS metric; sequenced conversion events; preferred-lead checkboxes on Instant Forms; **stablecoin payments for Meta Ads billing in select countries**; campaign tree status dots with inline pause.

## Microsoft

- Performance Max gains placement exclusions, age exclusions, age/gender targeting (Aug 5); Ad Preview Hub (Aug 6); bulk editing for disapproved assets; first monthly product newsletter (Aug 4).
- **Clarity split AI citations into branded vs non-branded (Aug 3)** — measure non-branded citations as true reach. Good AEO tooling item.
- Brodie Clark experiment: Bing free listings only appeared after ads were enabled; Bing feeds increasingly matter as LLM grounding data.

## Measurement / M&A

- **Nielsen announced it will acquire DoubleVerify for $2.15B (Aug 5, AdExchanger):** the largest independent verification vendor leaves the public market; audience measurement + ad verification consolidate under one roof. Watch pricing and neutrality questions.

## Agency economics

- **Agencies turn AI tokens into a margin business (Aug 3, Digiday + AdExchanger, synthesized by PPC Land):** holding companies buy compute wholesale, bundle token costs into principal-media commitments, resell at a markup presented as a discount; clients can't verify. "Agentic spend stalls" while the parties argue over who pays for compute.
- Lars Postmus (Draft Digital) diagram (Aug 2): agentic buying inherited the wrong chain of command — strategist → planner → five specialist buyers → twelve platform endpoints, each a separate MCP call with its own context load and auth; MCP's session removal forces a migration window on ad tech builders.

## Publisher datapoint

- People Inc. told investors Google Search is now 21% of its traffic, down from nearly two-thirds; keeping ad revenue flat by packaging offsite content (AdExchanger Aug 5, via PPC Land).
