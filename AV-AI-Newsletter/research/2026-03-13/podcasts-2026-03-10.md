### 1. Latent Space — "NVIDIA Dynamo & Brev: Datacenter-Scale Inference"
- **Host(s):** Alessio Fanelli, swyx
- **Guest(s):** NVIDIA Dynamo team, Brev.dev founders
- **Key Topics:**
  - NVIDIA's Dynamo inference engine for datacenter-scale AI workloads
  - The "Speed of Light" framework for measuring theoretical maximum throughput
  - Brev.dev's approach to making GPU clusters accessible to smaller teams
  - Agent security at the infrastructure level
- **Key Quotes:**
  - "The bottleneck has shifted from training to inference. Every AI agent call is an inference request, and we're nowhere near handling the scale that's coming." — Dynamo team
  - "Speed of Light tells you how close you are to theoretical maximum. Most teams are running at 20-30% of what their hardware can do." — swyx paraphrasing
- **Transcript Source:** Latent Space website (partial show notes), YouTube auto-captions
- **AV Relevance:** Medium-High — GPU inference optimization directly affects the speed and cost of AI video rendering, real-time voice processing, and any AV pipeline running AI models locally. The "Speed of Light" framework is useful for benchmarking AV AI workloads.

### 3. Latent Space — "Every Agent Needs a Box" (Aaron Levie)
- **Host(s):** Alessio Fanelli, swyx
- **Guest(s):** Aaron Levie (CEO, Box)
- **Key Topics:**
  - Agent governance and security — how do you give AI agents access to files without giving away the keys?
  - Box's "AI agent filesystem" concept
  - Enterprise readiness for agentic AI
  - The difference between consumer AI and enterprise AI requirements
- **Key Quotes:**
  - "Every agent needs a Box. You can't have agents accessing files across your entire company without governance. That's how you get the next big data breach." — Aaron Levie
  - "The filesystem for AI agents is a fundamentally different problem than the filesystem for humans." — Aaron Levie
- **Transcript Source:** Latent Space website (show notes), YouTube auto-captions
- **AV Relevance:** Medium — File governance is critical for AV teams managing large media libraries. If AI agents are processing, organizing, or editing media files, access control and audit trails matter enormously. Think: an AI agent accidentally deleting raw footage or sharing unreleased content.
