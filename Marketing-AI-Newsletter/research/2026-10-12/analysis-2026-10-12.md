## Common-thread candidates
1. **Every agent came with a sign-off line.** PAP lets the customer decide "whether the agent has read-only or write access"; pubX sells buying where "you sign off where it matters"; WPP Media requires "explicit controls, approval thresholds, accountable human oversight"; Carat's release keeps "human expertise at the center of strategy and activation"; Meta says Muse sign-ins and purchases proceed only with explicit approval. The counterexample came Friday: Anthropic's agents submitted "a sensitive form on a real website when it should not have." This week's pitch for agents was the checkpoint.
2. **Measurement became the pitch while the meters broke.** ChatGPT listed 18 data, attribution, full-funnel and incrementality partners; Tinuiti opened its measurement to agents over MCP; Google widened Conversion Lift to Search and PMax. The same week, rank trackers were fed swapped results, Safari's block left win logs with "ads no one saw," and (editor's note) ChatGPT's own links lost their tag. Buy on the new reporting, and check it against something you control.
3. **Authorship was written into the rules.** Google's helpful-content page calls fabricated creator profiles "a form of deception" (reported Oct 6; the 2026-10-05 edition uses the line in an angle, so here it's support, not news). Anthropic: no seeding AI answers with content that "misrepresents its origin, authorship, or independence." USA TODAY Co. seeks up to $25,000 per violation for stripped copyright management information. Who wrote a page, and who stands behind it, is now policed by a search engine, a model vendor and a court filing.
4. **The gatekeepers published their door policies.** Meta shut out a competitor's ads; Safari's list shuts out identity vendors (The Trade Desk's ad domain back in beta only); PAP has companies post `/.well-known/poppy.json` to set the terms for agents; the Independent Media Association told the CMA that, as drafted, "Google would decide which rivals are eligible to appear"; USA TODAY's sites send AI crawlers to a page that says "Crawling and scraping of our site is not permitted." Access was set by whoever runs the door, in writing.
