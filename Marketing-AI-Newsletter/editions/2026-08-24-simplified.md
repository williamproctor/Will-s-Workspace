## This Week in Marketing AI

### Manual price controls narrowed on three platforms

Google's recalibration went live August 17 (budget-capped campaigns now spend toward stated targets; one-way correction). Microsoft retires Max CPC for new non-portfolio campaigns October 1. ChatGPT Ads preselected "Maximize results," whose own docs say it "does not guarantee delivery against a specific CPA, CPC, ROAS, or other cost-efficiency target at this time"; budgets are already 7-day averages, and new view-through metrics sit outside bidding entirely. One week before the September 1 AI Max conversion. [PPC Land — Google](https://ppc.land/google-today-forces-overperforming-cpas-up-across-5-campaign-types/) · [PPC Land — Microsoft/OpenAI](https://ppc.land/microsoft-retires-max-cpc-as-chatgpt-ads-defaults-to-automated-bidding/)

### ChatGPT ads hit 31 European markets today

Announced August 18, live August 24: EU27 plus Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland. Free and Go plans only. No personalization in the EEA/Switzerland at launch; targeting is conversation, language, coarse location, device. Self-serve Ads Manager still "coming soon"; bookings via sales team and agencies. Six months from US pilot to 40 markets. If contextual-only performs, it resets the AI-ads targeting debate everywhere. [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/openais-ads-business-hits-europe-at-the-six-month-mark/) · [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/ChatGPT-ads-hit-Europe-on-Monday-but-not-the-personalized-kind.1375456.0.html)

### Apple Maps ads went live (with a rebate)

Delivery on since Friday, US and Canada: Suggested Places (pre-query) and search results. 15% monthly statement credit for up to a year, capped at $1,000/month, book by October 11. Location and time targeting, no identity profiles. Apple-reported: 1B+ business searches a month, half leading to action. [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/apple-maps-ads-go-live-with-15-back-capped-at-1-000-usd-a-month/) · [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/14/apple-maps-opens-ad-booking-for-businesses-promotional-offer-available/)

### Walmart: Connect +43%, Sparky users spend +40%

Q2 FY27 (August 20): global ads +38%, Walmart Connect +43% ex-Vizio. Furner: Sparky users up 70% YoY and spending 40% more per order (retailer-reported; 35% last quarter). Vibe acquisition now a ~20bps operating-income headwind; stock fell ~9% premarket despite raised guidance. First retailer CEO to put hard order-value numbers on an in-house shopping agent. [Transcript](https://corporate.walmart.com/content/dam/corporate/documents/newsroom/2026/08/20/walmart-releases-q2-fy27-earnings/q2-fy27-earnings-call-transcript.pdf) · [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/walmart-ad-business-gains-38-as-walmart-connect-hits-43-in-q2/)

### Uber fined €825M for automation without human review

Dutch DPA (with CNIL), August 21: fully automated driver deactivations 2018–2022, "complete absence of human intervention," inadequate disclosure. Second-largest GDPR fine ever, at the 4%-of-turnover maximum under Article 22. Uber is appealing. The reach: any automated decision that significantly affects someone, which includes ad-account suspensions and monetization cutoffs. [The regulator](https://www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/actueel/uber-krijgt-boete-van-bijna-825-miljoen-euro-voor-geautomatiseerd-blokkeren-van-chauffeurs) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/dutch-regulator-fines-uber-966-million-automating-driver-suspensions-document-2026-08-21/)

### Two numbers priced AI trust: 31.5% and 6%

IAB's disclosure framework v2 (August 18) leans on NYU Stern data: an AI label cuts ad CTR 31.5%, hence materiality-based labeling (synthetic people yes, color correction no; ✨ or "AI-generated" at WCAG contrast; C2PA underneath). StackAdapt (August 19, 500 pros via NewtonX): 91% use AI, 88% see gains, 6% almost always act on in-platform recommendations, comfort halves when AI acts autonomously. Platforms defaulted to automation the same week. [IAB](https://www.iab.com/guidelines/ai-transparency-disclosure-standards-v2/) · [StackAdapt PDF](https://s3.amazonaws.com/media.mediapost.com/uploads/StackAdapt_AI-Delegation-Gap-Report_2026.pdf)

## Common Threads

### Controls left, accountability arrived

Platforms removed price ceilings; a regulator priced unaccountable automation at €825M; a class action turned creative automation into evidence. Automation is being maximized on one side of the market and litigated on the other.

### The audience got buttons

A million slop flags, a fandom revolt, a 31.5% label tax. Anti-AI sentiment now has interfaces, and they feed classifiers and court filings.

### Identity-free launched as a tier

Apple Maps (location + time, no profiles) and ChatGPT Europe (conversation context only) both went live identity-free in the same week, unapologetically.

### Shoppers delegate before marketers do

Sparky users spend 40% more per order; 6% of marketers act on platform AI recommendations. The delegation gap is on the buy side, not the consumer side.
