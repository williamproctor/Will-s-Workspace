## The Conversation

### A sportsbook deepfaked a fan two days after ESPN made her famous

Sara Smith went viral off a Sunday broadcast; by Tuesday offshore sportsbook BetUS had posted an AI ad on X falsely showing her pulling her top down to distract the defense. X turned on it unanimously: "A real company posting a sexualized deepfake of a fan based on footage from a TV broadcast. We can't let this be the future." BetUS holds no US license, so there's none to lose; the disgust lands on everyone adjacent. Deepfake ads are a brand-safety category now. [Whiskey Riff](https://www.whiskeyriff.com/2026/09/09/sportsbook-under-fire-for-ai-generated-ad-falsely-depicting-viral-ole-miss-fan-pulling-her-top-down/)

### Google revoked ad credits after the money was spent

David Melamed: two client accounts had $3,200 promotional credits marked "Invalidated" more than a month after spending to earn them; no appeal, no refund; one because his MCC billing profile set the account up. Credit-holders also bid like spend is half price, raising CPCs for everyone. Ginny Marvin: "I've passed this along to the team." Treat credits as provisional until they survive two billing cycles. [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/advertisers-report-google-ads-credits-being-revoked-after-spending-488330)

### The AI industry's leaders asked it to slow down

Dario Amodei's essay (Sept 12): "We must slow the pace at which we improve the capabilities of AI models." Musk: "Dario is right." Altman: "I agree with Dario that we need to pace the frontier." Six weeks of AI-marketing backlash ran on public unease; the industry's leaders just said it's warranted. Handle "AI-powered" with care in consumer copy. [The essay](https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier)

## This Week in Marketing AI

### Google shipped its "largest reduction in quality" to Europe

Sept 8, 30 markets, DMA compliance after a €460M fine: an aggregator unit (one comparison service on top, expanded by default) and a stripped supplier unit for direct businesses (no dates, live prices, or tags); vacation rentals gone; extends into AI Overviews and AI Mode. Nick Fox: the changes "degrade the user experience for Europeans." Google's supporting data (30% direct-booking loss, user dissatisfaction) is Google's alone. First look at regulator-shaped AI search. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/google-warns-lower-quality-it-revamps-europe-search-results-avoid-eu-fines-2026-09-08/) · [Skift](https://skift.com/2026/09/10/google-update-europe-hotel-flight-search-results/)

### People Also Ask is 97% AI Overviews

AlsoAsked's 19.2M-PAA series: 12% fourteen months ago, 11% in April, 86% in August, 97% in the first week of September; Allintitle reads it as 100%. The question box answers itself and your page becomes a citation under it, if at all. Report PAA as a visibility surface, not a traffic source. [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/ppa-ai-overviews-google-42047.html) · [Monthly table](https://seohandbook.co.uk/seo-news-updates/people-also-ask-ai-generated-97-percent/)

### Amazon's DSP sells ChatGPT ads; OpenAI shut out rival image and voice tools

Sept 10: select US advertisers (Delta Vacations first) buy ChatGPT inventory via Amazon DSP, managed service, CPC/CPM, product-feed ads; OpenAI controls placement. OpenAI says ads pass $1B in the next 12 months; Sensor Tower: ads per user-hour +163% Apr→Aug. Sept 9 (The Information): OpenAI stopped approving ads for standalone image/audio-generation tools; Adobe's Firefly out, video "still open…for now"; Images 2.5 shipped the same week. Check your category against a rulebook that moves privately. [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media-buying/amazon-brings-its-dsp-to-openais-chatgpt-ads-extending-its-supply-chasing-streak/) · [SEL on the policy](https://searchengineland.com/openai-reportedly-blocks-rival-ai-tools-from-advertising-in-chatgpt-488019)

### Your Shopping CTR went up. Check the denominator.

Ryan, 175B impressions: median impressions 1.85M→1.4M, median CTR 1.20%→1.55%, clicks flat; Optmyzr CTR +17% YoY. Hypothesis: Google routes low-click-probability queries to AI Overviews and keeps Shopping where clicks are likely (unproven; Google's docs don't confirm). A Q4 board pack will read the CTR as a win. Put impressions, clicks, and traffic next to it; rebaseline 2025 benchmarks. Search Engine Watch: a headphones query in AI Mode showed only ads in the product block. [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-shopping-ads-imp-ctr-42021.html) · [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/ai-overviews-may-be-affecting-shopping-ad-ctr-and-impressions-487265)

### What AI answers do to demand: two datasets

Exploding Topics (2,300+ US consumers): 57.5% of AI users were talked out of a purchase by a chatbot; two-thirds replaced some product searches with AI; four in ten dislike chatbot ads. Fix the sources the model reads. SE Ranking's ChatGPT Ads test: 97,131 impressions, 1,263 clicks, 1.30% CTR. ChatGPT Ads expanded to Europe, India, MENA with a fuller performance stack. Infrastructure ahead of incrementality evidence. [Anicca roundup](https://anicca.co.uk/blog/weekly-update-search-marketing-11-09-2026/) · [SER on the updates](https://www.seroundtable.com/openai-chatgpt-ads-updates-42017.html)

### Blogging confidence at a twelve-year low, AI adoption at 92%

Orbit Media: 14% say their blog delivers strong results (26% in 2022); AI adoption 92.4% with no relationship to results; decline tracks abandoned practices (keyword research, original research, human editing, promotion). Posts take 3h20m instead of 4h+; ~50 hours saved a year while results slid. Speed replaced the parts that made the output good. [Via Anicca](https://anicca.co.uk/blog/weekly-update-search-marketing-11-09-2026/)

## Common Threads

### The referee also plays
Google grades its own EU remedy, labels its own PAA answers, defines "explicit intent" for AI Mode ads, and invalidates credits with no appeal. Every judgment this week came from the party with the incentive.

### Your supplier is your competitor
OpenAI sold ads to Adobe's category, then closed it the week Images 2.5 shipped. Amazon buys a channel it doesn't control. Google routes low-click queries to its own answers.

### Denominators are lying
Shopping CTR up because impressions fell; PAA visibility replaced clicks; blogs faster while results slid. Read the second number.

### The person in the ad didn't sign
A deepfaked fan, an unauthorized singer, an influencer briefed against her client's channel, and the industry's leaders asking for caution. Consent is the brief.
