> **Two ad auctions, one week, opposite momentum: Monday the FTC and 22 states accused Amazon of rigging its ad auction with an invented bidder for seven years ($20B+); Wednesday a judge let Google keep its ad-tech stack with behavioral remedies only.** Also: the AI Max migration went live as a month-long window, Gemini 3.8 Flash stopped citing sources in AI Mode for two days, Search Console's AI reports went global without clicks, and Xero's influencer told small businesses to replace their accountants with Claude.

## The Conversation

### Xero's influencer told SMBs to fire their accountants

A Xero-sponsored Instagram story: £120K spent on accountants since 2020, now Xero plus Claude does the £800-a-month management accounts. Accountants are Xero's sales channel; LinkedIn revolted (Sept 3–5); Xero pulled it and UK MD Kate Hayward said it "should never have run." Rumana Jeffreys: the comparison "reframes the accountant's fee as the cost of producing a spreadsheet, rather than the cost of judgement." The unclaimed angle: the brief leaked the channel strategy. ### Suno pulled its Mary J. Blige ad

An 85-second studio spot spread over the weekend as an endorsement; Suno then told Variety it had dealt with "someone who presented themselves as Ms. Blige's official representative" and killed the campaign when she objected. Talent-rights chain of custody is the whole story in AI-company campaigns. [Consequence](https://consequence.net/2026/09/suno-pulls-mary-j-blige-ad-says-she-never-authorized-campaign/)

### OpenAI's Astra film vs the Blender community

The launch ad shows a designer alone with a screen, Blender doing the work; Blender's community objected to its free, teach-yourself tool being used to sell the idea that the craft is optional. Fourth AI-lab marketing misfire since August, plus Samsung and Suno in a fortnight: the audience with the most to lose saw it first. [Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/ai/even-just-the-advert-for-chatgpt-6-astra-is-creating-controversy)

## This Week in Marketing AI

### FTC: Amazon rigged its ad auction with an invented bidder

Filed Aug 31 (W.D. Wash.) with 22 states: since 2019 a hidden "soft reserve price" via an "invented auction participant" turned a marketed second-price auction into first-price; Sponsored Products advertisers paid their full bid ~80% of the time; surcharges rose on Prime Day; $20B+ extracted from ~1.2M customers. Amazon: "misguided," CPC flat 2019–2024. Your bid strategy assumed second-price. [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/08/ftc-states-sue-amazon-over-secret-ad-surcharge-scheme) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/ftc-file-lawsuit-alleging-amazon-deceived-advertisers-wsj-reports-2026-08-31/)

### Google keeps AdX and DFP

Judge Brinkema (Sept 2) rejected divestiture and open-sourcing DFP's auction logic, accepted "most" behavioral remedies "as modified"; opinion sealed 14 days, joint Final Judgment due in 30. On the table: real-time bid data for rival ad servers, no Unified Pricing Rules, no first/last look. Second breakup avoided in a year; publisher damages suits continue. [The order](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.vaed.533508/gov.uscourts.vaed.533508.1857.0_1.pdf) · [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/media/google-wont-be-forced-to-break-up-adtech-biz-even-after-monopoly-ruling/)

### AI Max migration: a window, not a switch

From Sept 1 through Sept 30, campaign-level broad match and ACA campaigns convert in place; ACA cohort also gets text customization (Google rewriting your copy) unless switched off after. API v25.1 exposes per-campaign timestamps (join with OR) and `enable_ai_max`. Evidence both ways: smec median +13% conversion value at 0% ROAS delta (250+ campaigns); Lunio's IVT divergence; Alphabet clicks +13%, CPC +3%. Don't sum classic and AI Max search-term reports; verify expanded final URLs. [Improvado](https://improvado.io/blog/google-ads-ai-max-september-2026-upgrade) · [Choice OMG dossier](https://choice.marketing/research/google-ai-max-auto-upgrade/)

### Gemini 3.8 Flash forgot to cite

In AI Mode Sept 2 (Pro/Ultra); by Sept 3 Gagan Ghotra and Glenn Gabe showed top-of-funnel answers with zero links; Stein: "not working as intended," fixed by Sept 4. Your AI visibility is downstream of model defaults you don't control, and the fix came from a tweet. [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ai-mode-gemini-38-no-links-42011.html) · [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/google-to-fix-citation-bug-with-gemini-3-8-flash-in-ai-mode-486892)

### Search Console AI reports and opt-out went global (no clicks)

"As of August 31, 2026, we've rolled out this control to all websites worldwide." Impressions in AIOs, AI Mode, Discover AI by page/country/date/device; no clicks, queries, or positions; minimum-impressions threshold. Opt-out toggle removes a site from AI features as link or grounding, not a ranking signal. Arrived the day before AI Max started converting campaigns. [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-search-console-generative-ai-tools-live-41984.html) · [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/google-search-console-ai-performance-reports-and-search-generative-ai-control-rolling-out-globally-486269)
