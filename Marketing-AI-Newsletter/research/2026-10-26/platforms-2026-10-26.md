## Google Search
- **Rank trackers, week three (Oct 19–22; follow-up to the 2026-10-12 conversation item and the 2026-10-19 watch item):** Nozzle's new study (Derek Perkins, Thu Oct 22; data through Oct 21; 248M SERPs) finds 13.9% of SERPs judged since Sept 16 ignored part of the query, against 0.06% before, peaking at 39.9% on Sept 28: "Google has been playing dumb with rank trackers: it serves a normal page, the full query in its title, with results about one word of it." Nozzle's poisoning tracker (updated Oct 21) puts poisoned SERPs at 22.6% since Sept 13 (page 1 or 2), peaking at 43.5% on Sept 30, easing to 4–6% on Oct 14–15, then 25.9% on Oct 19 and 35.7% on Oct 21, "the worst day since October 8": "A refused request tells a scraper to try again; a poisoned one gets stored as real." Ahrefs (Thu Oct 22): "Depending on the data provider, we're seeing between 5% and 20% of search results affected in this way." and "We're also cleaning up any past results that may have been affected." AccuRanker (Peter Emil Tybirk, Mon Oct 19): 30.2% desktop and 25.7% mobile on Sept 25, 1.47% over Oct 1–9, under 1% after mid-October, 1.1–1.7% across 22 markets: "Google tightened its detection again on October 15, mostly on mobile. This time our checks held most of it back, and by October 17 both devices were under 1% again. Google keeps adjusting, and so do we." SerpApi's bug is still open. Google hasn't commented. Origins: [Nozzle, "Google Plays Dumb"](https://nozzle.io/research/google-plays-dumb/) · [Nozzle, "Poisoned Google SERPs"](https://nozzle.io/research/poisoned-google-serps/) · [Ahrefs, Oct 22](https://ahrefs.com/blog/serp-data-update/) · [AccuRanker, Oct 19](https://www.accuranker.com/blog/poisoned-serps/) · [SerpApi roadmap issue](https://github.com/serpapi/public-roadmap/issues/3781)
  - Conflict to hold: the vendors measure different things. Nozzle's earlier 8.0% average and 27.0% peak counted page 1 only; the new 22.6% counts pages 1–2. On Sept 29 Nozzle measured 27% where AccuRanker measured 1.4%. Attribute each figure to its vendor and method; all are vendor data.

Origins: [SER, Oct 21, 11:35 am](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-php2shell-42353.html) · [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-reminds-sites-to-serve-503s-during-server-downtime/593873/) · [Google crawling docs](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/troubleshooting/http-status-codes) · [CERT/CC VU#763488, Oct 22](https://www.kb.cert.org/vuls/id/763488) · [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cmly0lg3w5x2o) · [Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/cyber-agency-warns-websites-may-go-dark-as-php-prepares-critical-fix-4961963)
