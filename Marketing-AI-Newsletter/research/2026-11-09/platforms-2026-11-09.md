## Google Ads
- **Visible AI labels on ads now cover California, South Korea and Vietnam, and labels Google applies itself "cannot be overwritten" (Google Ads Help, "Use AI content label settings and disclosures"; SER, Thu Nov 5, 7:51 am; AuditSocials detected the change Nov 5 against an Oct 2 snapshot)** — the first alternate story. The AI label setting launched in July with visible overlays for the European Union, India and New York (Relevant Audience); the help page now lists six locations. "In some geographies, AI labels will also show on the ad itself. For campaigns that target the following locations, ads with assets that you designate as AI created or edited will also include visible overlays on the ads": the European Union, India, New York, California, South Korea and Vietnam. Elsewhere the disclosure sits in the "How this ad was made" section of My Ad Center, which "will be accessible globally". Google may apply labels itself "in regions where Google itself is legally required to do so, or when we get signals to do so from other platforms", and "When you use Google's fully-automated features to create and serve creatives, assets may be labeled as AI created or edited on your behalf to assist with your compliance." "In any of these cases, labels cannot be overwritten." And: "Use of the AI label setting in Google's advertising products doesn't guarantee your compliance with specific regulations." Images and videos made in Google's own tools carry SynthID watermarks and C2PA markup. The Merchant Center version lists the same six places and warns: "Labeling with this feature might impact whether this content appears on some experiences."
