## The Conversation

### ChatGPT's traffic drop was a missing tag

From Oct 8, inline links in ChatGPT answers lost `utm_source=chatgpt.com` and passed no referrer, so the visits landed in Direct. Cedric Huesler of Adobe (LinkedIn, Oct 13): "ChatGPT didn't stop sending visitors. It stopped telling you it sent them". Graphite's Ethan Smith put the GA4 drop at about 45%. Huesler says OpenAI confirmed "a known bug" to him (OpenAI hasn't said so publicly); tags returned Oct 15 by his telemetry and Oct 16 by Profound's, and Profound says the fix "does not automatically recover source information that was missing from earlier visits." All three companies sell AI-visibility products. Mark Oct 8–16 and report AI referrals as a floor. [Huesler's post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cedrichuesler_chatgpt-didnt-stop-sending-visitors-it-activity-7515970265852588033-KwRS) · [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/chatgpt-missing-utm-tags)

### The National Trust paused its TikTok ads after AI ads ran under its name

An AI-generated National Trust membership ad ran on TikTok Oct 10–11, and Alex Pollard posted it Oct 12 ("I knew it was AI before I even saw the badge in the corner."). The Trust: "This was an automated feature from TikTok Ads which produced AI generated adverts in error," and it paused all its TikTok ads. Practitioners pointed to the "Auto-add newly generated assets during delivery" setting; TikTok's help page calls its creative AI features "optional," and the outlets that asked TikTok for comment carry no reply. Check auto-generated creative settings before Q4 flights. [Civil Society](https://www.civilsociety.co.uk/news/national-trust-pauses-tiktok-ads-after-ai-content-error.html) · [TikTok's help page](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-smart-plus-campaign)

### Nine LinkedIn creators posted the same "go back to Canva" complaint

Navin Watumull's Oct 13 collage of nine near-identical posts, all tagging Canva, ran under the caption "Nothing brings LinkedIn creators together like having the exact same original thought" and had 2,026 reactions when indexed; the earliest version found dates from Aug 8. Commenters split between "100% an ad campaign" and a copycat trend. BuzzInContent, which identifies Watumull as an Adobe marketing manager, "could not independently verify whether this is a seeded campaign by Canva," and Canva hasn't responded. If money changed hands, each post would need a paid-partnership label. [Watumull's post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/navinwatumull_nothing-brings-linkedin-creators-together-activity-7515789051933921280-jewi) · [BuzzInContent](https://www.buzzincontent.com/influencer-marketing/organic-opinion-or-paid-promotion-nine-creators-spark-debate-with-similar-canva-vs-chatgpt-posts-12678979)

## This Week in Marketing AI

### Meta's October 30 terms let it crawl business users' sites; the US text lost "train" on Friday

On Oct 15, Nieman Lab reported that Meta's new terms let it crawl sites "associated with your products or services," with test credentials, and that the only alternative is to "stop all access or use of our Products for business or commercial purposes." On Friday, after publishers objected, Meta removed "train" from the US-English text and kept the crawl grant and its scope. SEJ notes that advertisers already allowed crawling for "research and training" under the ad terms; from Oct 30 any business use triggers the grant, even a Facebook Page alone. *Editor's note (Oct 20): Relevant Audience found "train" gone from the UK-English previews on Oct 20, a day after SEJ still saw it there.* [Nieman Lab](https://www.niemanlab.org/2026/10/metas-new-commercial-terms-will-allow-the-scraping-of-news-sites-legal-experts-say/) · [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/metas-new-terms-expand-crawling-rights-beyond-advertisers/593217/) · [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/meta/meta-commercial-terms-removes-train-keeps-crawling-clause/)

### Google's Universal Cart went live; Meta adds product browsing to existing ads Oct 28

Universal Cart rolled out Oct 15 in US Search and the Gemini app, with Google Pay checkout at many stores or a one-way transfer to the merchant, which will "remain the seller of record"; SEJ found that "None of these pages list a metric for products saved to a cart." Meta told advertisers, "Starting October 28, 2026, we'll add product browsing to your existing ads," citing "3% more conversions" in its own test; opt out before Oct 28, or per ad after. [Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/universal-cart-launch/) · [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-universal-cart-store-handoff/593145/) · [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/meta/meta-product-browsing-existing-ads-october-28/)

### Google's AI Mode ad tests reached drug brands and Personal Intelligence users

Google told Adweek on Oct 12 that its ad test in AI Mode and AI Overviews was "recently expanded to include certain pharmaceutical products." On Oct 16, SEL reported ads for Personal Intelligence users; in March Google had said there were no ads for people who connect their apps, and "That isn't changing right now." Google is also moving inline links "higher up in responses more frequently" and told SER, "we'll introduce additional metrics over time." [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/media/google-opens-ai-mode-to-prescription-drug-ads/) · [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/google-begins-testing-ads-in-ai-mode-for-users-with-personal-intelligence-enabled-496990) · [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-updates-links-in-ai-mode-and-ai-overviews-again-42308.html)

### Google Ads added channel priorities; Display on Search changes in February 2027

Oct 14: "Say goodbye to all-or-nothing channel targeting." Advertisers mark channels High or Low priority and can cap the Low ones. From February 2027, Search campaigns with Display Expansion "will automatically migrate," and Google says to set the Display Network to "Low priority" with a 50% cap "to maintain your existing configuration." The help page says API and Editor support is "coming soon"; Ginny Marvin posted, "Supported via Google Ads API at launch." [Google](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/channel-prioritization-budget-allocation/) · [Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/18385152?hl=en) · [Marvin's post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ginnymarvin_googleads-ppc-activity-7516161009955442688-Jb_6)

### DataDome says Muse doesn't identify itself; Amex wants intent logged before agents buy

DataDome, a bot-management vendor, said Oct 15 that AI-agent requests on its network rose 41.5% from Q2 to nearly 25 billion in Q3, 49.7% of them from Meta, and that "Muse does not identify itself as an AI agent." Amex's framework (Oct 13) records a shopper's limits before an agent buys; Digital Commerce 360 reports no live integrations yet. Deloitte: "39% say gen AI is their primary tool for at least one holiday shopping task, but only 16% would trust it to complete a purchase with their permission." [DataDome](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261015226683/en/DataDome-Report-Agentic-Browser-Traffic-Surged-Nearly-5x-in-a-Month-as-Metas-Muse-Arrived-Unannounced-From-the-Cloud) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/13/amex-introduces-a-framework-to-prevent-ai-agents-from-making-unauthorized-purchases/) · [Deloitte](https://www.deloitte.com/content/dam/insights/articles/2026/amer189387_cic_holiday-retail-survey/pdf/DI_2026-Holiday-Retail-Survey.pdf)

### ChatGPT's fan-outs favor brand sites, Ahrefs found; its Reddit citations fell 88%

Ahrefs, an SEO and AI-visibility tool vendor, found brand sites behind 75.9% of the most common `site:` fan-outs in GPT-5.6 Luna answers (August–September, before GPT-6 reached free users), and "ChatGPT's Reddit citations dropped 88% in six months." Semrush ("an Adobe company" that owns SEL) ranked Tesco 25th and Asda 606th among ChatGPT's most-cited UK domains in July, though both were top-100 mentioned brands. Bing's Topics Portfolio (beta) lists queries where other sites appear and yours doesn't. [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/query-fan-out-chatgpt/) · [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/news/474244-semrush-releases-2026-ai-visibility-index-revealing-its-first-uk-benchmark/) · [Bing](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/10/introducing-topics-portfolio-beta-in-ai-performance/)
