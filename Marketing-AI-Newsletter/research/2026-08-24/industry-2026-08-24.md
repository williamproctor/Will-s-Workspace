## Uber fined €824,990,000 under GDPR Article 22 (Aug 21) — automation without human review, priced

- Dutch DPA (AP), cooperating with France's CNIL, fined Uber for **fully automated driver deactivations** (fraud suspicion → temporary; low ratings → temporary/permanent) between 2018–2022 with "complete absence of human intervention," plus failure to inform drivers. Second-largest GDPR fine ever; calculated at the 4%-of-global-turnover maximum. Decision dated Aug 17 (Reuters exclusive), announced Aug 21. Uber appealing: "We strongly disagree with this decision and disproportionate fine." Fourth AP fine against Uber (€600K in 2018, €10M in 2023, €290M in 2024). Complaint origin: 170+ French drivers via La Ligue des droits de l'Homme, 2020.
- AP deputy chair Monique Verdier: deactivations happened "without warning or human involvement" and "have a significant impact on drivers' lives."
- Marketing relevance: the same Article 22 logic reaches ad-account suspensions, automated policy enforcement, and any platform decisioning that cuts off a business's revenue without human review — and the fine arrives the same week platforms removed manual bid controls.

## IAB AI Transparency & Disclosure Framework V2 (Aug 18) — the 31.5% label tax

- Voluntary, materiality-based standard for when AI use in creative needs a consumer-facing label. Leans on an **NYU Stern finding: disclosing generative-AI involvement cut an ad's CTR by 31.5%** — the framework's argument against blanket labeling (plus the implied-truth effect: unlabeled content reads as more credible once labels exist).
- Disclosure triggers: AI-generated images/video from prompts, synthetic voices of deceased persons (authorized or not), synthetic voices of living persons in fabricated scenarios, photorealistic AI influencers, digital twins (deceased; living in events that didn't happen), conversational agents ("AI-powered" label). Exempt: color correction, routine post, clearly stylized/fantastical imagery.
- Implementation specified to the glyph: Unicode sparkle U+2728 or "AI-generated" text at ≥4.5:1 contrast (WCAG AA); video labels in first frame; audio labels before the segment; C2PA metadata with com.iab.threshold and com.iab.disclosure assertions. 24-month roadmap; AI Disclosure Lead within 60 days; months 6–12 pilot the performance impact (where 31.5% gets confirmed or contradicted at scale).
- Regulatory backdrop: EU AI Act Article 50 applicable Aug 2 (~190 signatories to the EC transparency Code by end of July); California SB 942; New York. Cadbury reportedly generated 130,000 unique AI videos for one campaign; 83% of ad executives report AI deployment (up from 60% in 2024).
- Origins: [IAB framework page](https://www.iab.com/guidelines/ai-transparency-disclosure-standards-v2/) · [framework PDF](https://www.iab.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IAB_AI_Trasparency_Disclosure_Framework_August_2026.pdf) · [Marketing Dive](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/iab-revisits-ai-disclosure-in-ads-as-legal-requirements-multiply/828182/) · [PPC Land detail](https://ppc.land/ai-ad-labels-cut-click-through-31-5-iab-framework-cites-nyu-study/).

## StackAdapt "The AI Delegation Gap" (Aug 19) — 91% use AI, 6% act on its recommendations

- NewtonX survey of 500 marketing/advertising professionals (May 19–June 8, 2026, mid-size + enterprise, NA/EMEA/APAC) plus 187 StackAdapt clients. **91% of organizations use AI tools** (91% NA / 90% EMEA / 92% APAC); 86% use them regularly; 88% report performance improvement. But **only 6% "almost always" act on in-platform AI recommendations**; 42% ignore recommendations as generic/irrelevant; comfort falls from 90% (AI recommends) to 89% (AI prepares for approval) to 78% (AI acts within rules) to **50% (autonomous AI, even with proven performance)**; 22% very confident evaluating AI decisions; 19% have AI fully integrated; 17% report no clear accountability for bad AI decisions. Top delegation barriers: brand risk 63%, data quality 56%, transparency 36%.
- The tension: platforms made automated bidding the default this same week while the buy side reports it acts on AI recommendations 6% of the time.
- Origins: [PPC Land report](https://ppc.land/only-6-of-marketers-act-on-in-platform-ai-recommendations-stackadapt-finds/) · [report PDF (via MediaPost S3)](https://s3.amazonaws.com/media.mediapost.com/uploads/StackAdapt_AI-Delegation-Gap-Report_2026.pdf) · [MarTech Cube](https://www.martechcube.com/stackadapt-report-reveals-ai-decision-making-shift-in-advertising/) · [Performance Marketing World](https://www.performancemarketingworld.com/article/1968020/90-marketers-use-ai-yet-half-trust-autonomous-tools-%E2%80%93-report).
