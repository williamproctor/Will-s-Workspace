## 1. ChatGPT's "traffic drop" was a missing tag, and OpenAI fixed it Thursday

**Timing note:** straddle. ChatGPT stopped tagging inline links on Thursday Oct 8 (about 2pm PT per Cedric Huesler; Profound's first dip is 18:30 UTC that day), the day GPT-6 and Intelligent UI reached Free and Go users. The 2026-10-12 research covered that rollout as news, not the tracking break. In window: the diagnosis, the argument and the fix (posts from Tuesday evening PT Oct 13 through Friday Oct 16; fix rolling out from Thursday Oct 15).

**What happened:** Inline links in GPT-6 answers dropped `utm_source=chatgpt.com` (OpenAI added the tag in June, per SER). The links open with `noopener,noreferrer`, so there was no referrer either, and GA4 and Adobe Analytics filed the visits as Direct. Huesler (Adobe; his LinkedIn headline reads "Product at Adobe for Brand Visibility solutions") posted Adobe telemetry showing ChatGPT visits falling and Direct rising by nearly as much on the same pages. On Wednesday Ethan Smith (CEO, Graphite) posted a fall of about 45% in ChatGPT-attributed GA4 sessions across sites, and Search Engine Roundtable wrote it up. Huesler says OpenAI confirmed a known bug to him Wednesday morning PT. The fix began rolling out early Thursday; Profound's answer sampling shows tagged links back near 99% from 06:30 UTC Friday. We found no public OpenAI statement, and the ChatGPT release notes have no entry between Oct 9 and Oct 20.

## 2. National Trust paused all its TikTok ads after TikTok's AI made some for it

**Timing note:** the ads ran over the weekend of Oct 10–11 (Pollard saw one on Sunday Oct 11; Anstee says "over the weekend"). Posts and coverage ran Monday Oct 12 to Wednesday Oct 14. The 2026-10-12 research flagged it as a carry-forward.

**What happened:** An AI-generated National Trust membership ad ("more than 500 remarkable places") ran on TikTok. UK marketers on LinkedIn called it slop from a charity with one of the country's best photo libraries, and one asked how it squared with the Trust's climate campaigning. The Trust replied on LinkedIn that an automated TikTok Ads feature had produced the ads "in error", and it paused all its TikTok ads. Practitioners then posted the setting to check: "Auto-add newly generated assets during delivery". TikTok hasn't commented: Civil Society, Third Sector and Arts Professional all say they contacted it, and none carries a statement.
