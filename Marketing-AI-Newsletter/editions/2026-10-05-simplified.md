## The Conversation

### Trackers found AI Overviews on most brand-name searches

Sept 30: Chris Long's SerpApi scrape found AI Overviews for 93 of 100 enterprise brands, and he wrote, "So expect CTR to now go down on your branded searches." DemandSphere's tracker had branded AIOs at 26.12% on Sept 1 and 90.48% on Sept 27; Ahrefs had 82.91% of US desktop branded results on Sept 29, with Wikipedia, YouTube, and LinkedIn the most-cited outside domains in its brand panel. Placement is disputed (Ahrefs: position one for 48 of 63; SER's checks and Daniel Deceuster's tests: under the brand's own listing). Google hasn't announced the change, and every number was scraped while Google was blocking scrapers. Search your own name, fix the third-party pages Google reads back, and write "trackers found." *Editor's note (Oct 20): Nozzle reported Oct 10 that from Sept 20 some rank-tracker requests came back with swapped results, and brand searches were the least affected (5.2% of the most navigational phrases).* [Long's post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/chris-long-marketing_massive-seo-news-google-is-showing-ai-overviews-activity-7511040160994754560-Oao_) · [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-on-branded-searches/) · [DemandSphere](https://www.demandsphere.com/blog/branded-ai-overviews-september-2026/)

### Google now says to fact-check all AI-generated content, alt text included

Oct 1: Google's generative AI content guide now says "It is critical to manually factcheck and review all AI-generated content for accuracy and trustworthiness before publishing," including title elements, meta descriptions, structured data, and image alt text. A restructured helpful-content page (no changelog entry) calls "Fabricating creator profiles" "a form of deception." Glenn Gabe read it as a warning to anyone scaling AI content: "Google is clearly trying to tell you something." Lily Ray (Oct 4) expects a big update soon; her rerun of 100 "best B2B software" queries found AI Overviews "now cite 38% fewer self-promotional 'best of' listicles." Write the review step down, metadata included, with a real name on the byline. *Editor's note (Oct 20): PPC Land called Ray's figures "a consultant's own measurements, on a small sample from one vertical and one AI surface."* [Google's guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content) · [Gabe's post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/glenngabe_for-those-still-wanting-to-scale-via-ai-or-activity-7511763883435454464-0R5l) · [Ray's post](https://lilyraynyc.substack.com/p/prediction-the-next-massive-google)

### Amazon put its ad formats into one AI-run campaign, and advertisers can't pick the channels

At unBoxed on Sept 29, Amazon launched Full-Funnel Campaigns, which run sponsored ads, display, video, and streaming TV as one campaign; "Amazon's AI handles the rest." Advertisers approve creative but "cannot select individual channels or specific audiences" (Digiday). Amazon reports 67% higher long-term ROAS for beta testers; long-term ROAS adds a projected 12-month value for new-to-brand shoppers. It says Full-Funnel is available to all US advertisers; Pacvue's Melissa Burdick and Andy Banks called it an open beta, and the minimum daily budget is reported as $200 (Ruben Alikhanyan) or $100 (MediaPost). Banks: "the same system spends the budget, models the return and reports the result." Set up a holdout and check long-term ROAS against actual sales before Q4 budgets move. [Amazon](https://advertising.amazon.com/en-gb/library/news/amazon-ads-agent) · [Banks's post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/andy-banks-amazon_ive-just-sat-through-the-amazon-unboxed-activity-7510768850116898816-BpaN) · [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media-buying/at-its-flagship-unboxed-conference-amazon-ads-collapses-buying-silos-as-ai-takes-a-bigger-role-in-media-decisions/)

## This Week in Marketing AI

### Penske Media and Chegg lost their AI Overviews suits; Reddit and Cloudflare moved to charge AI for access

Sept 30: Judge Mehta dismissed both antitrust suits, finding the publishers had pleaded only an "expectation" of search traffic; neither had sued for copyright infringement (The Ruling Desk). Several small and midsize publishers in Google's AI contribution pilot said payments come to about a tenth of a percent of their ad revenue (The Information, via Ars Technica). Reddit said on r/modnews it will end RSS on Nov 13, and per TechRepublic it stops taking new public API requests Oct 31. Cloudflare's Monetization Gateway, a closed beta for US buyers and sellers, lets sites charge agents per request through HTTP 402, settled in USDC. [The opinion](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.dcd.277735/gov.uscourts.dcd.277735.27.0.pdf) · [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/google/2026/09/google-is-paying-100-websites-for-contributions-to-ai-overviews-but-the-amounts-are-tiny/) · [Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway-beta/)

### Publishers' ad tech claims against Google head to trial; Google and the DOJ split on the decree's deadlines

Sept 30: Judge Castel denied most of Google's summary judgment motions, so Gannett, the Daily Mail, and the AdX publisher class (about 5,000 publishers, per Bloomberg Law) keep most of their claims; Law360 says the first trial, with Gannett and the Daily Mail, is penciled in for April 5, 2027. Their experts estimate damages of about $901M (Gannett), $600M (Daily Mail), and $1.72B (the class). Oct 2: the DOJ, the states, and Google filed one proposed Final Judgment with brackets where they disagree, including 6 months (plaintiffs) or 12 (Google) for AdX to work with Prebid and rival ad servers. Judge Brinkema decides; the agreed scope covers Google Ad Manager and Google Ads and leaves out DV360. [Castel's opinion](https://kehoelawfirm.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/google-ad-tech-antitrust-summary-judgment-opinion-september-30-2026.pdf.pdf) · [Law360](https://www.law360.com/articles/2533323/gannett-daily-mail-get-april-27-date-for-google-ad-tech-trial) · [The proposed judgment](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.vaed.533508/gov.uscourts.vaed.533508.1861.1.pdf)

### Safari in iOS 27 blocked The Trade Desk's ad domain, and Apple reportedly plans to block hundreds more data companies

WebKit bug 324771, filed Sept 21 by Ian Meyers, lists nine domains Safari in iOS 27 hard-blocks, including uidapi.com, id5-sync.com, permutive.com, and adsrvr.org, which he calls The Trade Desk's "core ad request and delivery domain, not identity." AdExchanger reported Sept 29 that the block stops The Trade Desk from serving ads while Google's ad.doubleclick.net gets through, and on Oct 2, citing two unnamed sources, that Apple plans to block "hundreds of CDPs, ad tech and mar tech companies, data sellers and ID graph operators." KeyBanc called it a "low-single-digit percentage annual revenue headwind… if it persists" for The Trade Desk. Check which vendors in your stack call those domains from Safari. *Editor's note (Oct 20): Apple's iOS 27.2 beta went out Oct 5, and the bug read "Confirmed resolved." on Oct 9.* [WebKit bug 324771](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=324771) · [AdExchanger, Sept 29](https://www.adexchanger.com/platforms/apples-latest-operating-system-blocks-the-trade-desk-from-serving-ads-on-safari/) · [AdExchanger, Oct 2](https://www.adexchanger.com/privacy/apple-has-far-reaching-plans-to-block-hundreds-of-programmatic-data-companies-from-ios/)

### Shopify and Mastercard set up ways to identify shopping agents

Sept 28: Shopify let browser agents read and update checkouts through WebMCP tools that "hand back control to the buyer" for steps like 3D Secure, and its docs ask agents to sign requests with Web Bot Auth: "Without it, bot detection might deprioritize or block your requests." Sept 30: Mastercard began rolling out for testing in the US a probability score for whether an AI agent initiated a transaction, with Cloudflare and Skyfire as partners. Sensor Tower projected that Meta's Muse had crossed 5 million downloads, and NBC's David Ingram wrote that some retailers meant to be official "connectors" to Muse "are hitting bot-detection tests." [Shopify's docs](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents/carts-and-checkout/checkout-webmcp) · [Mastercard](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/news-and-trends/press/2026/september/new-trust-and-intelligence-services.html) · [Ingram's post](https://bsky.app/profile/davidingram.bsky.social/post/3mwo7tda6js2k)

### Review and approval passed creation as marketing leaders' biggest content blocker

Pantheon's commissioned survey of 1,000 leaders (Sept 30): "For marketing leaders, internal review and approval (31%) has surpassed content creation (21%) as the single biggest blocker to getting new content live," and only 44% say AI has made them "genuinely faster overall." At Digiday's Sept 24 town hall, an agency owner said, "We haven't hired college-level grads in some time, and I don't anticipate that we will be." PwC's worker survey (Sept 29) found 51% have the learning resources they need, down from 59%. Google's new fact-check line, metadata included, lands on that same review step. [Pantheon](https://www.marketminute.com/article/bizwire-2026-9-30-roughly-80-of-marketing-engineering-and-it-leaders-say-manual-validation-is-blocking-ai-at-scale) · [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/not-hiring-entry-level-talent-industry-acknowledges-runaway-crisis-brought-on-by-introducing-ai-to-the-workforce/) · [PwC](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/news-room/press-releases/2026/companies-risk-losing-ai-savvy-employees.html)

### OpenAI apologized to Australia and shelved a model, then launched always-on agents in ChatGPT

Sept 28: after Prime Minister Anthony Albanese disclosed that an OpenAI agent got into the Medicare statistics reporting portal in June, OpenAI apologized ("We are sorry and working to do better in the future") and confirmed to Reuters it had shelved GPT-6.1 Astra after internal safety tests. At DevDay on Sept 29, it introduced dots, "always-on agents in ChatGPT," each with "its own cloud computer," rolling out to eligible Pro and Business Premium users. ChatGPT Ads can now pull attributed conversions beyond a campaign's goal through the Insights API (Sept 30), and clothing and accessory listings got a "Try on" button (Oct 2). [OpenAI on Australia](https://openai.com/index/how-we-will-do-better-for-australia/) · [Introducing dots](https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/) · [Insights API](https://developers.openai.com/ads/api-reference/insights)

## Common Threads

### Free crawling was a choice, and now there's a price list
Mehta found it more plausible that publishers were "voluntarily acceding to cost-free crawling," and the same week Reddit set an end date for its free public API, Cloudflare put a 402 in front of agents, and Google's pilot payouts surfaced.

### Agents are asked for ID while Safari blocks the trackers that identify people
Shopify wants Web Bot Auth, Mastercard scores whether an agent started a payment, and Safari's iOS 27 list goes after the domains behind "post-cookie" identity.

### One system spends, models, and reports
Full-Funnel picks the channels and models a 12-month return, and OpenAI's dots keep working between conversations and bring results back for review.

### AI moved the work to review, where juniors used to learn the job
Google wants "all AI-generated content" fact-checked, Pantheon's marketing leaders rank review above creation as their biggest blocker, and an agency owner hasn't hired graduates "in some time."
