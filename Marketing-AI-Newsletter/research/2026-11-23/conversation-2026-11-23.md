**What happened:** Yeşilyurt asked ChatGPT for hosting coupon codes and got cards instead of links: "A card for each brand", "An end date ("Ends December 7")", "The code itself, with a Copy button" and "A "Get deal" button". "The whole coupon site experience, inside the answer." His reading: "This is bad news for coupon and affiliate sites. - **What brands and affiliates should do.** Yeşilyurt: "Push exclusive codes that only work through my own link. Public codes are now free content for AI." And for brands: "put the real, current code on your official promo page. Otherwise AI will pick whatever it finds on Reddit." In his comments, Kristof George pointed out that "several affiliate networks can credit a sale to whoever owns the coupon code even with no click or cookie, so an exclusive code that ChatGPT surfaces can still pay out." Muhammad Taseen: "The card is built from whoever publishes the code first. Stale codes become the source."
- **What isn't known.** "I am not sure yet if this is a test or a rollout." "I also do not know where "Get deal" sends you: the brand, an affiliate link, or somewhere else." Gabe found "major differences in the paid versus free versions of ChatGPT": "The paid version didn't show the cards, but did still provide codes."

**Conflicts to hold:**
- It may be a test. Yeşilyurt saw it "on several hosting coupon prompts", and Gabe saw cards in the free version only. We found no statement from OpenAI or Google.
- The 3% to 16% figure comes from Yeşilyurt's own prompt tracking. Hold it as "in the Black Friday prompts I track", not as a market share.
- Who gets paid is unknown. "Get deal" may go to the brand, an affiliate or elsewhere (Yeşilyurt). George's point about code-based crediting is one commenter's description of how some networks work, not a finding about these cards.
- That codes like BLACKFRIDAYREDDIT come from community and affiliate pages is Yeşilyurt's inference from the code names and the source pill.
- AI Overviews and AI Mode show codes too (Gabe; Yeşilyurt; SER), but as text or cards with source links. Gabe's AI Overviews post and his ChatGPT post are two different posts on the same day.
- The help-page edit happened between Nov 4 and Nov 15 (partly prior window). The removal alone doesn't show that partnerships influence results.
- SER's "Google has been doing this in their ads for a decade" refers to merchant-supplied promotions in Google's own products, not to ChatGPT.

**Comment window:** open through Cyber Monday (Nov 30), and Yeşilyurt will report again after it. The take not made in the posts we found: the click that disappears also disappears from your analytics. If a shopper copies a code from a ChatGPT card and then types your URL, GA4 records direct or organic traffic, and the coupon site records nothing. The only place the AI answer's effect shows up is the redemption log for that code. So, for Cyber Week, give the code on your official promo page (Yeşilyurt's advice) a name used nowhere else, then compare its redemptions with your affiliate and email codes. The redemption count measures what the answer did, and referral traffic will undercount it. Affiliates should ask their network now whether a code redeemed with no click still pays, which is the case George describes.
