# Platforms track — week of August 17–23, 2026

Primary-source announcements and platform changes. Origin links listed first; discovery/synthesis noted where used.

## OpenAI / ChatGPT Ads

- **ChatGPT Ads expands to 31 European markets — ads go live Monday, August 24 (publication day).** Announced Aug 18. The 27 EU states plus Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland. Free and Go plans only; Plus/Pro/Enterprise stay ad-free. **No personalized ads in the EEA/Switzerland at launch** — targeting is contextual (current conversation, language, coarse location, device); earlier chats, saved memories, and ad history stay out. Bookings via OpenAI's Ads Solutions team and partner agencies; self-serve Ads Manager "later this quarter" (country table still shows Coming Soon for all 31). Six months into the ads business: started as US pilot in February, then Canada, Australia, NZ, UK, Mexico, Brazil, Japan, South Korea. VP of Ads Dave Dugan (statement to Digiday): "There's no such thing as a one-size-fits-all European audience — and that's precisely what makes the region so dynamic… expectations around relevance, quality and trust are rightly high. We look forward to meeting that bar." Groundwork: EU privacy policy posted Aug 14; privacy terms updated June 4. Origins: [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/openais-ads-business-hits-europe-at-the-six-month-mark/) (original reporting, Dugan quotes) · [Notebookcheck detail on the no-personalization nuance](https://www.notebookcheck.net/ChatGPT-ads-hit-Europe-on-Monday-but-not-the-personalized-kind.1375456.0.html) · practitioner receipt: [Nathan M. LinkedIn post, Aug 20](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nathan-m-b4611bab_chatgpt-ads-expand-to-31-eu-markets-what-activity-7496094950254665728-09yF) ("tens of thousands of marketers have already run ads on the platform," per OpenAI).
- **Maximize results became the preselected bidding default for eligible new ChatGPT ad groups** (help page updated ~Aug 12; product email week of Aug 17). OpenAI's own doc caveat: the strategy "prioritizes result volume and does not guarantee delivery against a specific CPA, CPC, ROAS, or other cost-efficiency target at this time." Only route to a price ceiling: Manual: Max bid, chosen per ad group (no account-level preference). Context stack: fixed daily budgets became 7-day averages in July (no opt-out); **view-through conversions (VTA, fixed 1-day window) arrived Aug 18–19** — visible in reporting, excluded from bidding/billing/CPA; **Automatic Advanced Matching became default Aug 17** with 10-day opt-out. Origin: PPC Land reporting ([Microsoft retires Max CPC as ChatGPT Ads defaults to automated bidding](https://ppc.land/microsoft-retires-max-cpc-as-chatgpt-ads-defaults-to-automated-bidding/)); OpenAI help pages are the underlying documents.
- SER's Friday recap also flagged the 31-market expansion: https://www.seroundtable.com/video-08-21-2026-41915.html

## Google

- **Bidding recalibration went live Monday, Aug 17** — budget-constrained campaigns on tCPA/tROAS (incl. ad-group targets) now optimize toward the stated target rather than the lower CPA the budget cap produced; one-way correction, five campaign types, across Google Ads, SA360, DV360, Editor, API. Disclosed June 15; Q&A Aug 5; Marvin interview Aug 12. First reliable post-rollout reads: mid-September to mid-October. Origin: [PPC Land day-of report](https://ppc.land/google-today-forces-overperforming-cpas-up-across-5-campaign-types/) (their original), building on the SEL/Marvin record from our Aug 17 edition.
- **AI Max experimentation and planning tools announced Aug 20** on Google's own blog (Brandon Ervin, Director of PM, Search Ads): multi-campaign A/B tests of budgets/ROI targets (rolling out in September), experiments that respect brand and location controls, Performance Planner now simulates bidding/budget-target changes with one-click apply. Origin: [blog.google](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/ai-max-testing-planning-tools/).
- **August 2026 spam update:** started Tue Aug 18 ~12:30pm ET, completed Fri Aug 21 ~4:50am ET. "Normal spam update," global, all languages; does not target link spam or site-reputation abuse. Origin: [Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-august-2026-spam-update-done-41906.html).

We're bringing our most intelligent workhorse model to Search for even more helpful responses. Rolling out globally today in AI Mode for Google AI Pro & Ultra subscribers in English." (X post URL not located; quoted by SEL/SEJ/SER.) ## Apple

## YouTube

- **Copyright menu became "Claims" (Aug 19):** Content ID copyright and AI likeness privacy complaints merged into one dashboard; four dispute categories for likeness claims (explicit consent, parody/satire/public interest, not altered/AI-made, content doesn't appear). Two consequential lines in the docs: **altered-content disclosures don't stop claims, and privacy settings don't stop claims** (private/unlisted videos are claimable — reaches into brand pre-launch approval workflows). 1–3 minute Shorts with an active claim are blocked outright. Origin: PPC Land reporting via [weekly synthesis](https://ppc.land/bid-controls-shrink-as-ad-measurement-breaks-week-of-august-17/); YouTube help docs underlying.

## Similarweb (measurement vendor, platform-adjacent)

- **AI Ads launched Aug 17** (Business Wire, 8:00am ET): competitive ad intelligence for ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, and AI Overviews — the surfaces with no ad library. Headline stats from the release: **26% of ad-eligible ChatGPT replies carry a sponsored ad** (July 2026, US desktop); AI Overviews trigger on a large share of queries and often include ads. Ad gallery + panel-based (real conversations, not synthetic prompts). Vendor motive noted: Similarweb sells the visibility product for the gap it documents. Origin: [PPC Land report on the release](https://ppc.land/similarweb-opens-ai-ad-data-as-26-of-chatgpt-replies-carry-sponsored-ads/) (Business Wire release underlying).
