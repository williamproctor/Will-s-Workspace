## Reddit
- **RSS and the public Data API are ending (r/modnews, Wed Sept 30, 17:41 UTC).** "Continuing our infrastructure updates: What's changing in the coming months" (u/judy-funnie): "Because RSS is now a common surface for large-scale scraping and automated abuse, we'll stop supporting RSS feeds on November 13, 2026 while preserving moderation workflows that depend on it through supported alternatives." And: "If you use RSS for feeds outside of a community you moderate, there is no replacement. RSS has been a beloved part of the open web for a long time, and we're grateful to everyone who used it to stay connected to Reddit." Apps and bots move to the Developer Platform: "Over 14,000 apps and bots have registered"; register "before January 12, 2027 to avoid disruption." Old Reddit will be limited to logged-in users who used it in the last six months (mods exempt; TechCrunch notes Reddit changed this from 90 days as the news went out). The four dates, per TechRepublic's read of the Sept 30 update: Oct 31, 2026, no new requests for public API access; Nov 13, RSS ends; Jan 12, 2027, removal begins for apps and users that haven't registered; March 2027, remaining public Data API access ends. TechCrunch (Sarah Perez, 10:45 AM PDT) on who loses access: tools that used the API "including social listening products, those used by researchers, and AI products, such as AI assistants. Today, those assistants can use Reddit to answer questions, but after closing public API access, they will need commercial deals with Reddit for its data." Context from TechCrunch: Reddit's "other revenue" (which includes data licensing) grew 24% year over year to $43M in Q2. MediaNama: a "$1 million App Migration Program pays $1,000 to each eligible app that migrates" (Reddit's r/redditdev post not fetched).
