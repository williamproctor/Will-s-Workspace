# Monday Signal — Week of November 16, 2026

> **Google began its November core update on Nov 10, made Gemini 4 Hydrogen AI Mode's default model on Nov 12 (per Robby Stein), and under-logged Search Console's AI impressions for Nov 9–11; by SEJ's count, the first full week after the rollout includes Thanksgiving, Black Friday and Cyber Monday.** Ads reached AI Mode's follow-ups, Bing's AI Mode and ChatGPT image generation, and Salesforce projected $75.8 billion in AI-driven Cyber Week spending. On the feed, NoGood counted 1 Business Hub click against 1,020 GA4 sessions, and George Prodanov told ChatGPT advertisers, "Your cap becomes an average".

## The Conversation

### NoGood's ChatGPT Business Hub showed 1 click while GA4 counted 1,020 sessions

OpenAI's Business Hub (beta, Nov 10) shows verified businesses 28 days of mentions and website clicks, and a mention counts only "when ChatGPT references the business's website." NoGood's Mostafa ElBermawy counted 1 click against 1,020 sessions and called it "a business profile and not a search console"; Previsible counted 12 clicks against 32 sessions. [OpenAI Help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt) · [NoGood](https://nogood.io/blog/chatgpt-business-hub-vs-ga4/) · [Previsible](https://previsible.com/ai-search/chatgpt-business-hub/)

### Split rank trackers and an under-logged AI report made the core update hard to read

On Nov 11 Glenn Gabe posted that "it might be hard to gauge visibility drops/surges accurately," shared Ahrefs and Semrush charts that disagreed about the same sites, and called Search Console data "the source of truth (for now)" (as AEO Sherpa quotes him). Lily Ray reported more manual-action inquiries "than any time I can remember"; Google hasn't linked them to the update. [Gabe on X](https://x.com/glenngabe/status/2120517778813641075) · [AEO Sherpa](https://aeosherpa.com/news/ahrefs-and-semrush-split-on-core-update-day-one) · [Ray's post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lily-ray-44755615_google-search-status-dashboard-activity-7525993341948203008-YYa1)

### ChatGPT Ads' target bidding went live Nov 9, and one advertiser said OpenAI "silently rejected" 3,930 pixel events

New conversion and click campaigns now bid to Target CPA and Target CPC, and the caps retire Jan 1, 2027; Prodanov's summary was "Your cap becomes an average". Ian McGavin wrote that "OpenAI silently rejected 3,930 of our pixel events," one account's experience that OpenAI hasn't addressed, and its help page warns, "Setup alone does not establish that usable conversion signals are being received." Reconcile accepted events against your orders first. [OpenAI Help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001634-bidding-strategies-and-how-pricing-works-in-chatgpt-ads) · [Prodanov's post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/georgeprodanov_chatgptads-openai-ppc-activity-7525507375391215616--cDK) · [McGavin's post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ian-mcgavin_chatgpt-ads-got-target-cpa-today-you-tell-activity-7526005412345716736-5PCy)

## This Week in Marketing AI

### Google started a core update, put Hydrogen in AI Mode and under-logged AI impressions

The core update began Nov 10 and "may take up to 2 weeks to complete." On Nov 12 Hydrogen reached Search, "now the new default model in AI Mode" per Stein, and Search Console Help disclosed a logging error that under-counted Generative AI report impressions for Nov 9–11. *Editor's note (Nov 16): When we checked, the Search Status Dashboard still showed no completion for the update.* [Google Search Status Dashboard](https://status.search.google.com/incidents/WLxcJyC6jMWMfryxsKMc) · [Search Console Help](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/6211453?hl=en)

### Ads appeared in AI Mode follow-ups, atop Bing's AI Mode and while ChatGPT draws images

SER reported Google's multiturn ads live in AI Mode on Nov 9, showing "on responses to follow-up user queries," and ads at the top of Bing's AI Mode with labels Barry Schwartz called "so muted"; Microsoft hasn't announced it. ChatGPT showed Juozas Kaziukėnas an ad while drawing an image, and businesses can now sponsor their ChatGPT agent. [SER on multiturn ads](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ai-mode-multiturn-ads-42478.html) · [SER on Bing](https://www.seroundtable.com/bing-ads-top-ai-mode-results-42479.html) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)

### Salesforce projects $75.8 billion in AI-driven Cyber Week spending

Salesforce, which sells commerce software, says "AI is projected to drive $75.8 billion in Cyber Week spending," without naming the markets or defining "AI-driven," and 73% of shoppers it surveyed "aren't comfortable giving a personal AI agent their payment information." [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/ai-first-holiday/)

### The Trade Desk beat its own guide by 13%, and PubMatic, Zeta and Liftoff grew

The Trade Desk's $736 million in revenue (Nov 9) fell 0.5% from a year earlier but topped its "at least" $650 million guide by 13% by our arithmetic, including $15 million from a partner it doesn't expect to recur. PubMatic grew 17%, Zeta 46% (30% excluding Marigold and political candidate revenue) and Liftoff 32%. [The Trade Desk's release](https://investors.thetradedesk.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2026/The-Trade-Desk-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx) · [The Trade Desk's 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1671933/000167193326000116/ttd-20260930.htm)

### Yahoo Scout became a publishers' collective's first licensee, and Google lost again to SerpApi

The nonprofit Internet Licensing Collective signed Yahoo Scout as its first commercial customer on Nov 12; Digiday reports the fee wasn't disclosed. On Nov 9 a federal judge dismissed Google's amended complaint against SerpApi with leave to amend, the second dismissal; we found the order on a third-party host, and dismissal isn't a ruling that scraping is lawful. [The ILC's release (GlobeNewswire)](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/12/3401676/0/en/the-internet-licensing-collective-ilc-launches-with-yahoo-scout-as-first-commercial-partner.html) · [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/yahoo-becomes-first-licensee-of-spotify-like-collective-ai-content-licensing-model/) · [The order (PDF, third-party host)](https://filings.escalegal.com/google-serpapi-dismissal-order-2026-11-09.pdf)

### Google Ads says disclosed AI impersonation is still banned and shows invalid-traffic credits

Google's Nov 11 policy update says ads that "impersonate public figures using AI are not allowed, even if they disclose that they were generated or edited using AI," and a new card shows "Estimated savings" and "Credited cost" from invalid traffic. Anthropic's ban on using Claude to seed AI answers' sources with misleading content took effect Nov 12 (our Oct 12 edition). [Google Ads policy update](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/18891933?hl=en) · [Google](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/invalid-traffic-overview-google-ads/) · [Anthropic's Usage Policy (PDF)](https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/files/4zrzovbb/website/6ae2f5bb8675bcd9fac006125817ba1393b396e4.pdf)

OpenAI counts a mention only when ChatGPT cites your site, and Google's AI report has a Nov 9–11 gap.

### Ads moved into follow-up answers and the wait for an image

The core update may run to about Nov 24, Hydrogen arrived Nov 12, and Black Friday and Cyber Monday fall on Nov 27 and 30.

## Angles for the Week

- **Calibrate ChatGPT's Business Hub against GA4 every 28 days.** Log Hub clicks against ChatGPT-referred sessions; NoGood's 1 to 1,020 and Previsible's 12 to 32 show the spread. Window: monthly.
- **Read the core update against last year's holiday week.** Annotate the logging gap, the update and Hydrogen, and compare clicks with the same week of 2025. Window: through December.
