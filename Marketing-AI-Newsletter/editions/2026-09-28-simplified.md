## The Conversation

### Kalshi's AI ad copied a YouTuber's video and swapped his face

Elliot Choy (X, Sept 23): "i guess kalshi saw this and thought they should steal my video, turn me into a white dude, and run it as an ad." YouTube served it to him; NPR's Bobby Allyn confirmed it. Kalshi: about four months old, made by an unnamed agency, no more AI-generated people, "reviewing our relationship with" the agency. Creator Pushpek Sidhu reported the same thing in June. On Sept 9, Kalshi's growth chief had posted that the brand went "all-in on unmistakably human creativity." The agency excuse left the blame with Kalshi; licensing a creator's video costs about a quarter of a new one (The Now, via BI). [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/kalshi-in-hot-water-over-race-swapping-ai-ads-2026-9) · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1000309/youtuber-elliot-choy-says-an-ai-kalshi-ad-stole-one-of-his-videos-and-changed-his-race)

### Google turned on AI Mode checkout for Shopify stores; GA4 won't see the orders

Sept 22 emails: "Eligible products are automatically included. There's nothing for you to set up." Menachem Ani to Mike Ryan on X: "No action on our end." US merchants selling to US customers; opt out under Sales channels > Agentic. Shopify's help page: "Google Analytics and custom pixels won't fire" in that checkout; only server-to-server started/completed events do. Audit which AI channels have direct checkout on and reconcile revenue in Shopify. [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-native-checkout-emails-42140.html) · [Shopify help](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts/google)

### AI Overviews got more links; some open AI Mode

Sept 21: Peec AI's Malte Landwehr charted AI Overviews with an external link in the answer text jumping from near zero to 26.2% after Sept 11; Gagan Ghotra recorded underlined AIO text that opened AI Mode instead. Search Console counts nothing for query-refinement links. A link count is inventory, not traffic; check where links go. [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-ai-overviews-have-more-links-but-not-all-reach-the-web/590762/) · [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ai-overviews-more-external-links-42135.html)

## This Week in Marketing AI

### Amazon locked Muse out; Shopify, PayPal, and Expedia plugged it in

Muse (launched Sept 8) topped the US free iPhone chart Sept 18 with 2.5M+ downloads by Sept 21 (Sensor Tower via CNBC). Sunday night, Sept 20, Amazon's popup: "Continued access by an unauthorized AI agent violates Amazon's Conditions of Use." Its complaints: no notice, no self-identification, stored credentials. Shopify (Mon), PayPal and Expedia (Tue) signed on; six banks published agent-payment principles (Tue). Wednesday, Amazon let sellers run their stores from Claude with per-action approval. Reach through agents now depends on where you sell. *Editor's note (Sept 30): NBC News's Sept 29 tests found Muse blocked at several big retailers; its headline names Walmart, a Meta-announced partner.* [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/amazon-blocks-metas-muse-ai-assistant-in-new-standoff-over-agentic-shopping/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/21/meta-muse-personal-ai-agent-downloads.html) · [GeekWire on Accelerate](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/amazon-opens-its-seller-tools-to-outside-ai-agents-starting-with-anthropics-claude/)

### ChatGPT's share of AI prompts: 70% to 50% in six months

Comscore (Sept 22), January to June: ChatGPT 70% to 50%, Gemini 17% to 30%, Claude 2% to 11%. Google desktop searches with an AI Overview: 25.8% (July 2025) to 39.4%. Sponsored ads in hotel prompts with source links: 6% in March, 24% by May. AEO trackers weighted to ChatGPT now see about half the prompts. [Comscore](https://www.comscore.com/Insights/Press-Releases/2026/9/Comscores-Q2-2026-AI-Intelligence-Report) · [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/comscore-data-shows-how-ai-discovery-is-splintering-beyond-chatgpt/)

### Made on YouTube: brand deals in Studio, likeness alerts on phones

Sept 23: Creator Partnerships plus Ask Studio drafting replies to brand briefs; affiliate program in 35 countries by year-end; video A/B tests of up to three cuts coming soon; prompt-built home feeds; Ask YouTube as a product guide; likeness detection on mobile, voice detection later this year. 72% of US video posters ages 14–44 have used AI to create or edit. [Mohan's post](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/innovation-youtube-era-made-on-viewers-creators/) · [Pappu's post](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/made-on-youtube-new-tools-power-creation-journey/)

### Google promised an AI Max report; Microsoft's is in its API

Google (Sept 23): one report showing search term, creative assets, and landing page, "later this year"; AI Brief in seven more languages. Microsoft's Search Term Landing Page report and PMax device exclusions shipped in August (covered Sept 24–25). Both answer the Q4 question of what the automation did. [Google](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/ai-max-language-reporting-features/) · [TechWyse](https://www.techwyse.com/news/platform-updates/microsoft-ads-api-search-reporting-pmax-device-exclusions)

### The UK wants AI assistants on Google's choice screens

CMA (Sept 23): a choice screen at first use on Android and Chrome, then a yearly prompt; qualifying AI assistants included; publisher attribution required. Comments close Oct 9. [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/cma-strengthens-proposals-allowing-people-choice-over-their-search-service)

### Temu pulled partnership ads from likely fake creators

Fortune (Sept 25): after its Aug 31 story (up to $962M on Meta partnership ads; about 73 of the top 100 creator accounts probably fake, per Online Risk Labs), 90 of those accounts showed no Temu ads by Sept 4, and weekly partnership ads fell from 115,114 to 64,008. Vet creator accounts before boosting. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/25/temu-axed-its-1-billion-network-of-fake-accounts-on-meta/)

## Common Threads

### The storefront picked its agents
Amazon blocked a shopper's agent and invited sellers' agents; Shopify, PayPal, and the banks set terms for Muse.

### Default-on is the launch plan
Native checkout "automatically included," agentic storefronts on by default, AI Max absorbing DSA and broad match. The decision sits at the opt-out.

### The creator is the new attack surface
Kalshi's face swap, Temu's likely fake creators, YouTube's likeness detection on phones.

### Dashboards are missing the new traffic
Checkout orders GA4 can't see, AI Mode links Search Console may not count, half of prompts outside ChatGPT, a fifth of News UK's traffic not human.
