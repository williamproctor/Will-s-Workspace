# Industry track — week of November 16–22, 2026

## Agents went shopping before Black Friday: Salesforce put $75.8B of a $357B global Cyber Week on "AI and agents", Muse reached two more countries and skipped Amazon, and Klarna's assistant started buying for users

**Timing note:** in window. Google's Black Friday shopping post is dated Mon Nov 16. On Tue Nov 17, Muse launched in Australia (Meta), GeekWire reported Muse's "Do not search Amazon" instruction, Andy Jassy spoke at a Semafor event (reported by BI on Nov 18), YouTube opened Shopping affiliates in Australia with Amazon.com.au (Google Australia), and Klarna announced its AI assistant. On Wed Nov 18, Loblaw joined Muse in Canada and ShopBack announced its Muse connector, and Google's Demand Gen Drop added checkout links. Salesforce's Cyber Week forecast followed on Thu Nov 19. It follows up its Nov 12 release, which was the 2026-11-16 edition's story 3. Kaziukėnas spotted Muse's Amazon change on Fri Nov 13 (prior window, per GeekWire). ChatGPT Wallet launched Nov 13 (prior window; context only). Adobe published nothing new in window; its forecast is dated Sept 28. Cyber Week itself (Nov 24–30) is post-window.

- **The forecast (Salesforce, Thu Nov 19):** "Salesforce today predicts that the 2026 Cyber Week (Tuesday, November 24, through Monday, November 30) will drive a record $357 billion in global online sales, and $75.8 billion of that figure will be influenced by AI and agents". That's "$75.8 billion, or 21% of all global sales". The US forecast is $84B, up 6%. "Twenty percent of holiday shopping traffic will be nonhuman", and 8% of shoppers say an agent has already bought something for them. Origins: [Salesforce, Nov 19](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/cyber-week-ai-sales-2026/) · the Nov 12 release it follows: [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/ai-first-holiday/)
- **The agents and who they let in:**
  - Muse arrived in Australia, where Meta's Will Easton said "With the festive season coming up, the timing couldn't be better", with early partners including Kogan, MECCA, Officeworks and ShopBack, whose connector lets users "compare prices and earn cashback" (Meta, Nov 17; ShopBack, Nov 18).
  - Loblaw became Muse's first Canadian retail connector: "Starting today, shoppers can plan meals, build their basket and check out with participating Loblaw banners, all in Muse." (Meta, Nov 18)
  - GeekWire reports that Muse's shopping skill now tells it to "leave out Amazon unless the user asks for it" and "Do not search Amazon", after Kaziukėnas's "Amazon blocked Muse, so now Muse blocked Amazon".
  - Jassy, asked about outside agents: "Well, I don't think that we've said we're not going to open it up," and "It's just that it has to be a good customer experience, which it hasn't been to date, and it has to be a fair exchange of value." (Semafor, via BI)
  - The same day Muse reached Australia without Amazon, Amazon.com.au joined YouTube Shopping's new Australian affiliate program as a partner (Google Australia, Nov 17).
  - Origins: [Meta, Australia](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/11/muse-is-now-available-in-australia/) · [Meta, Loblaw](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/11/meta-welcomes-loblaw-as-first-canadian-retail-partner-to-muse/) · [ShopBack](https://corporate.shopback.com/press/shopback-connects-to-muse-from-meta-in-time-for-black-friday) · [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/do-not-search-amazon-metas-muse-now-shops-elsewhere-by-default/) · [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/amazon-ai-shopping-agents-muse-instinct-dots-2026-11) · [Google Australia](https://blog.google/intl/en-au/products/connect-communicate/youtube-shopping-affiliate-program-in-australia/)
- **An assistant that buys (Klarna, Tue Nov 17):** Klarna introduced "the Klarna AI-assistant, a personal assistant that handles shopping, returns, bookings, and everyday admin", reachable over WhatsApp and iMessage. "Currently live with a small group of early users", and the "Waitlist opens this week." Klarna also says that "Klarna customers have spent more than $34 billion through the app's built-in browser since 2020". Origins: [Klarna (Business Wire, via Klarna IR)](https://investors.klarna.com/News--Events/news/news-details/2026/From-returns-to-restaurant-bookings-Klarnas-AI-assistant-finds-the-best-product--price-buys-it-for-you-and-you-dont-pay-until-it-arrives-2026-AsJ__j3bx3/default.aspx)

It also shows you merchant promo codes and offers", and shoppers can "check out securely right in the cart using Google Pay" (Molly Shaheen, Nov 16). Demand Gen ads can now send shoppers to "checkout links or Universal Commerce Protocol-enabled checkout" (Nov 18). Origins: [Google, Nov 16](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/black-friday-cyber-monday-shopping-tips-2026/) · [Google, Nov 18](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/demand-gen-drop-november-2026/)

**Claims to hold precisely:**
- Salesforce's verb changed. On Nov 12, "AI is projected to drive $75.8 billion in Cyber Week spending"; on Nov 19, the same $75.8B is "influenced by AI and agents". The Nov 19 release makes the scope global ("of all global sales"), which answers last week's open question, but it still doesn't define "influenced". Print "influenced by", not "driven by", and call it global, not US.
- Salesforce's Cyber Week is seven days (Tue Nov 24–Mon Nov 30). Adobe's forecast counts the five days from Thanksgiving through Cyber Monday. Don't compare their totals.
- "Twenty percent of holiday shopping traffic will be nonhuman" is a projection, and the release doesn't say whether it counts bots and scrapers or only shopping agents.
- Klarna's $34 billion is spending "through the app's built-in browser since 2020", not through the new assistant. eMarketer's write-up says "through the assistant"; that's wrong. The assistant is live for "a small group of early users".
- Muse's Amazon instruction is GeekWire's quotation of the skill text. Amazon blocking Muse first is Kaziukėnas's account (via GeekWire). Jassy's lines come from Semafor's stage via BI.
- Meta says Muse "checks with you before sensitive actions such as sending an email or making a purchase", and local connectors roll out "progressively". Don't say every partner was live at launch.
- ChatGPT Wallet (Nov 13) is prior window; mention it only as context.
