## Audience, narration, and visual style

The audience is audio/video production professionals inside the Audio/Video Services department of a faith-based organization.

- Never mention these notes or their instructions in narration.
- Keep narration family-friendly, professional, observational, and concise.
- Describe technology without mystical or supernatural metaphors.
- Label vendor, author, customer, and independent evidence.
- Preserve exact product status, dates, numbers, and unknowns.
- Use short labels and key facts on screen rather than dense paragraphs.
- Visual style: flat 2D editorial vector illustration, warm parchment background, one muted blue accent, clean sans-serif typography, generous negative space.
- Avoid neon, glow effects, three-dimensional renders, and futuristic interface imagery.
