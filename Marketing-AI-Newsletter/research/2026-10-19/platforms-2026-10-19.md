## Google Ads
- **Channel prioritization and budget allocation (blog.google, Wed Oct 14, Tal Akabas; rolling out from Thu Oct 15):** "Say goodbye to all-or-nothing channel targeting." Advertisers rank channels High or Low priority, and "campaigns will fully fund high-priority channels (within their target when using Target CPA or Target ROAS) before allocating the remaining budget to lower-priority channels." Low-priority channels can take a percentage cap, applied "On a best-effort basis". Performance Max and Demand Gen group Search, YouTube, Maps, Gmail and Discover as "Google channels". The instruction for Search campaigns: "If you currently use the Google Display Network, set it to "Low priority" with a 50% budget cap by February 2027 to maintain your existing configuration." Help page: "Starting on October 15, 2026 … progressively"; budget allocation isn't supported in Demand Gen; API and Editor support "coming soon"; "Until February 2027, Search campaigns with the Display Network turned on … continue to use current Display Expansion behavior". Origins: [Google](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/channel-prioritization-budget-allocation/) · [Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/18385152?hl=en) · [SEJ, Oct 14](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-ads-introduces-channel-prioritization-and-budget-allocation/592833/)
  - **Podcast:** *Ads Decoded* Ep. 7, "How to guide campaign spend with Google Ads budget and bidding controls" (host Ginny Marvin, Google's ads product liaison; guest Tal Akabas, Google Ads product manager), published Oct 15, 21:00 UTC. Akabas: "These controls are just additional guardrails to help you feel more comfortable with how your budget is being spent across campaigns and enable to steer the AI". On Search plus Display today: "there's actually a backend mechanism that is effectively doing channel prioritization in the backend." "the automated backend logic will be going away as we roll this out."
