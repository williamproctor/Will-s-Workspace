## Common-thread candidates
1. **The answer is the ad slot now.** Sponsored Agents, sponsored text links inside AI Mode answers, Business Agent inside YouTube ads, and a Google program that pays for what shapes the answer. Every surface that used to separate the answer from the ad blurred this week.
2. **Money is moving toward the answer layer.** Google pays publishers for generation-phase contribution; Profound valued at $1.8B for measuring AI visibility; Europe's remedy moves shelf space from Google's own units to comparison services. Whoever shapes the answer is getting paid, and the price lists are private.
3. **Supervision, not surgery.** Brinkema chose behavioral remedies and a monitor over divestiture; Judge Wu kept TikTok's 2019 decree; the DMA remedy is Google implementing what it calls a worse product under watch. Courts and regulators are choosing to sit inside the companies instead of breaking them up.
4. **Attention is measured; outcomes are pending.** Novig's 911% mention spike with 4% about the app; Ryan's "one to two conversion cycles"; AI contribution earnings with no methodology; AI performance insights with no click metric. Everyone has a number for exposure and nobody has one for effect yet.
