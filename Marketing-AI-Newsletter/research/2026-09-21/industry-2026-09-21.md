# Industry track — week of September 14–20, 2026

## US v. Google (ad tech): the remedies opinion, unsealed Tuesday Sept 16

- Judge Leonie Brinkema's 106-page memorandum opinion (filed under seal Sept 2; neither side proposed redactions within the 14-day window, so it was released as written). Verified against the opinion text ([CourtListener PDF, Doc. 1858](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.vaed.533508/gov.uscourts.vaed.533508.1858.0.pdf)):
  - "structural remedies are neither realistic nor needed" — no AdX divestiture, no open-sourcing DFP's final auction logic, no contingent DFP divestiture. Reasoning (per National Law Review's reading): the Microsoft "significant causal connection" standard for structural relief wasn't met; the auction logic wasn't part of the violation; one DFP function depends on a daily computation running ten hours across 4,000 machines.
  - **Prebid interoperability:** AdX and DFP must integrate with Prebid, operating "in a functionally equivalent manner and on materially identical terms as DFP currently interoperates with and solicits bids from AdX." AdX must submit real-time bids to rival publisher ad servers on the same terms as DFP gets them.
  - **Data sharing / transparency:** technical documentation on how DFP selects a winner (instead of open-sourcing), real-time bid data shared with publishers (win/loss per AI Weekly / AdExchanger).
  - **Anti-discrimination:** no re-implementing First Look / Last Look or tying DFP to AdX.
  - **Buy side:** "buy-side behavioral remedies are appropriate only as to AdWords, and not as to DV360." AdWords barred from bidding directly into DFP (the opinion cites Google Partner Bidding/"gBid" as the circumvention risk), from favoring Google's own tools, and from first-party-data advantages.
  - **Term and oversight:** "Google's proposal for a 6-year term is appropriate" (plaintiffs sought 15 years); a Monitor and Technical Committee (full access to employees, systems, source code per AdExchanger); the Final Judgment to "take effect 60 days after it is entered," with monitor setup starting sooner. Joint proposed Final Judgment was due within 30 days of the Sept 2 order (~Oct 2). Remedies apply globally (BI).
