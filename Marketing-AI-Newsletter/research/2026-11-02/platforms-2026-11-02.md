## Google Search
- **User-generated spam guide: keep share links out of Search by default (Google Search Central, updated Mon Oct 26; SER, 3:15 pm; SEL):** a new section, "Only index content meant for Search": "If your service lets users create shareable pages, generated outputs, or interactive tools, consider whether that content is meant to be found through search engines or only accessed directly by people with a link." "When pages are created for direct sharing, collaboration, or utility rather than public search discovery, apply the noindex rule (using a robots meta tag or X-Robots-Tag HTTP response header) or block crawling in robots.txt by default." The changelog entry, via SER: "Updated the user-generated spam prevention guide with guidance on blocking indexing of link-shared content and monitoring abuse in Search Console." Origins: [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/prevent-abuse) · [SER, Oct 26](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-user-generated-spam-guidance-tools-42378.html) · [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/google-search-says-you-should-block-indexing-of-link-shared-content-499040)
