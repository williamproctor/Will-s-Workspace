David: Man of War

A concept trailer reimagining the life of King David with the epic, brutal, and imposing scale of Denis Villeneuve's Dune. An unflinching look at the burden of the crown and the cost of blood.

01 — Script Structure

The Arc

Mapping the explicit structure of the Dune Part 3 Teaser to the life and biblical accounts of King David.

1. THE NEW EMPIRE
A sweeping, high-angle wide shot of the capital city. The united armies of Israel in rigid formation. Establishing the massive scale of David's kingdom. (2 Samuel 5:6-10)
10s
2. THE KING'S BURDEN
David on his throne in a dimly lit, vast room. He looks older, tired, and heavily burdened by his rule and the endless bloodshed. (1 Chronicles 22:8)
15s
3. GHOST OF THE PAST
A solitary figure in the wilderness. Michal looking betrayed, turning away into the harsh desert winds. The human cost of the protagonist's choices. (2 Samuel 6:20-23)
12s
4. THE SCHEMER
An eerie, intimate close-up of Absalom, looking dangerously handsome, calculating in the shadows of the palace. An inner-circle threat. (2 Samuel 15:1-6)
10s
5. VISIONS OF WARFARE
Rapid montage. Chariots burning, hand-to-hand combat against the Philistines. Violent bursts of bronze, dust, and blood. (2 Samuel 8)
25s
6. THE TRAGIC PATH
Slow-motion, wide tracking shot on the palace roof at sunset. David looking out over the city. Bathsheba in the distance. The inevitable fall. (2 Samuel 11:2)
12s
7. TITLE CARD
Pitch-black screen. Thundering sound effect. The words fade in glowing with a fiery orange aura before fading to black.
6s
