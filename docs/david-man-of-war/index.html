David: Man of War

A concept trailer reimagining the life of King David with the epic, brutal, and imposing scale of Denis Villeneuve's Dune. An unflinching look at the burden of the crown and the cost of blood.

01 — Script Structure

The Arc

Mapping the explicit structure of the Dune Part 3 Teaser to the life and biblical accounts of King David.

1. THE WEIGHT OF THE CROWN
Older, battle-weary King David alone on the brutalist throne. The crushing reality of rule, interspersed with visceral flashes of his innocent past and first taste of blood.
15s
2. THE GIANT & THE MAD KING
A young, ruddy David steps forward against impossible, brutalist scale. Paranoia breeds in King Saul, an exceptionally tall and handsome ruler driven to madness by jealousy.
20s
3. THE WILDERNESS & THE WARS
Fleeing through towering, claustrophobic slot canyons, finding refuge among broken men. Then, the absolute chaos and brutal dust-choked reality of leading armies to war.
25s
4. THE SINS OF THE FATHER
A tragic path sealed on a sunset roof. A loyal soldier abandoned. The breathtaking rise of Absalom, flawlessly handsome with heavy hair, sparking a shadow rebellion that shatters the kingdom.
20s
5. EPIC MONTAGE & FINALE
Rapid cuts of profound grief, apocalyptic consequence, and the passing of a blood-soaked sword to the next generation. The King's final breath.
10s
