## What leads

**News lead:** Google changed its ranking system and its answer model in the same week, and the instruments for reading both went unreliable at the same time. On Tuesday Nov 10, the Search Status Dashboard logged "the November 2026 core update, which applies globally and to all languages. The rollout may take up to 2 weeks to complete." It's the third core update of 2026 (SEJ). On Thursday, Gemini 4 Hydrogen arrived in Search. Robby Stein: "It's now the new default model in AI Mode & being used for your hardest questions in AI Overviews." Google's post called it "the biggest model upgrade to Search yet" and promised "a broader range of helpful links than before" and "more images, structured responses, comparison tables, and custom tools built for you"; SEJ: "Neither the post nor Stein's thread provide counts for links or sites." Gemini 4 Carbon had reached AI Mode for Google AI Ultra subscribers on Tuesday. Meanwhile, Search Console Help says "A logging error caused a decrease in impressions on the Generative AI performance report in Search for data from November 9 - November 11, 2026." Glenn Gabe, who "ran the numbers for ~1000 sites across a few verticals", posted Ahrefs and Semrush charts that disagreed, writing: "With Google poisoning the SERPs for AI scrapers and now SEO tools, it might be hard to gauge visibility drops/surges accurately." Google also tested AI Overview source icons at the bottom of the answer (SERP Alert), definitions fully expanded by default (Gabe) and a results page with no AI Mode tab (Gabe, via SER). Google also published a reading tool of its own: a Search Central page (Nov 12) on finding Search traffic in your own analytics through the HTTP Referer header. That header "doesn't include the search query", and the page doesn't say how to separate AI Mode or AI Overview clicks. Two things are new for marketers. First, Google's usual comparison window, a full week after the rollout ends, now falls on "U.S. Thanksgiving Day, Black Friday and Cyber Monday" (SEJ, counting from Google's two-week estimate). Second, no single tool can read this update this month. (Post-window, editor's note only: the dashboard had no completion entry when read Nov 16.)

## Angle candidates
1. "Claim your ChatGPT business listing, then calibrate it against GA4." (Business Hub reports mentions and website clicks for the past 28 days. A mention counts only when ChatGPT "references the business's website", and a blank metric "doesn't necessarily mean zero activity". Pull Hub clicks and ChatGPT-referred GA4 sessions for the same 28 days and log the ratio monthly, as the instrument's calibration rather than proof that either system is wrong: NoGood found 1 click against 1,020 sessions, and Previsible 12 against 32. Big brands should also check whether ChatGPT cites their own pages, since naming the brand without the site doesn't count. Window: now. The Hub shows a rolling 28 days, so log it on the same day each month. Business Agents are a US-only beta for ecommerce and online service businesses.)
2. "Read the core update in your own logs, not in one tool, and not against Black Friday." (Annotate Nov 9–11, when the Generative AI report under-logged impressions; Nov 10, the core update; Nov 12, Hydrogen in AI Mode and AI Overviews; and the rollout's end, still unposted when read on Nov 16 (post-window). Use Search Console clicks rather than the Generative AI report's impressions, and check them against your own analytics with the Referer values Google documented Nov 12. Search uses the origin referrer policy, so the header carries only the scheme and host, and it "doesn't include the search query". Retailers should compare against the same holiday week of 2025, because the first full week after rollout includes "U.S. Thanksgiving Day, Black Friday and Cyber Monday" (SEJ). Advertisers whose CTR moves after Nov 12 should check broad match, AI Max and PMax first (Aristide D.). Window: through the rollout, about Nov 24, and the December comparisons.)
3. "Open Microsoft's new AI Max report and see which pages and headlines the AI chose." (The search term landing page report is "scoped to AI Max-enabled Search campaigns", and Microsoft Advertising's November newsletter lists it as live. For each search term, it shows the landing page and whether it was "Advertiser Provided" or "AI Expanded from URL", and whether "At least one headline asset in the row was AI-generated". Pull it for your highest-spending AI Max campaigns and check the AI-expanded URLs and AI-generated headlines against your offers and brand rules. "AIGenerated" doesn't mean every headline asset in the row was AI-made. Window: now, before Black Friday (Nov 27).)
4. "Ask your AI-visibility vendors, in writing, how they place content." (Anthropic's Usage Policy, in force since Nov 12, bars using Claude to "Manipulate the sources from which search engines or AI systems draw answers by seeding them with content that misrepresents its origin, authorship, or independence", and to "Market, sell, or solicit funding for any of the above as a product or service". Google Ads now bans AI impersonation of public figures even when it's disclosed. Ask each agency or tool that promises AI visibility whether it publishes third-party sites, reviews or posts that don't say who's behind them, and what it uses to make them. Window: in force now. The policy targets deception, not AI-visibility work in general. Not legal advice. GrowthX competes with AEO/GEO vendors, so keep this neutral and name no vendor.)
5. "Ask your category's follow-up questions in AI Mode, Bing and ChatGPT, and see who advertises there." (Google's multiturn ads appear "on responses to follow-up user queries", and SER saw them on the third and fourth follow-ups. Bing puts search and shopping ads at the top of its AI Mode, with muted labels, and ChatGPT shows ads while images render and sells sponsored Business Agents. Run your top three buying journeys to the fourth question on each, screenshot who appears and how the ad is labeled, and decide whether your brand needs coverage there. Window: now through Cyber Monday (Nov 30). These are sightings; neither Google nor Microsoft has published coverage numbers.)

The 2026-11-09 angle 1 ("Set ChatGPT's Target CPC from your own revenue per session") covered the bidding change, so use this only framed as the pixel step. Window: before Jan 1, 2027.)
