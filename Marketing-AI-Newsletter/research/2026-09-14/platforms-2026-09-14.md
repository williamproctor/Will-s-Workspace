## Google Search
- **EU results redesigned Sept 8 under the DMA; Google calls it its largest quality reduction ever.** Reuters exclusive (Foo Yun Chee): "The changes mark the largest reduction in quality of service at the world's most popular internet search engine in its 29-year search history" (unnamed Google official). Context: €460M DMA fine in July for self-preferencing in shopping/hotels/transport/sports; 60 days to comply or periodic penalties up to 5% of worldwide daily turnover. New EEA-only result units documented on Search Central the same day: an **aggregator unit** (one Vertical Search Service block, top-ranked provider expanded by default) and a **supplier unit** for direct providers (no date filters, live prices, or descriptive tags). Applies across 30 European markets; extends to AI Overviews and AI Mode; vacation-rentals unit removed. Nick Fox (SVP knowledge & information): changes "degrade the user experience for Europeans" by "boosting online intermediaries at the expense of local businesses"; "users outside the EU will not be impacted." Google's claims (earlier DMA changes cut free direct-booking traffic 30%; user tests showed high dissatisfaction) are unverifiable — the data is Google's. Travel-tech groups counter that Google chose a worse layout. Origins: [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/google-warns-lower-quality-it-revamps-europe-search-results-avoid-eu-fines-2026-09-08/) · [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/google-says-dma-changes-in-eu-resulted-in-worse-degradation-of-search-quality-ever-487340) · [TNW analysis](https://thenextweb.com/news/google-eu-search-dma-remedy-degraded-quality-nick-fox) · [Skift on the travel layout](https://skift.com/2026/09/10/google-update-europe-hotel-flight-search-results/) · [Relevant Audience on the Search Central docs](https://www.relevantaudience.com/seo/google-eea-aggregator-supplier-search-units/).

Monthly series (Mark Williams-Cook, LinkedIn Sept 8): Jan 22%, Feb 24%, Mar 14%, Apr 11%, May 40%, Jun 64%, Jul 61%, Aug 86%, Sep 97%. "It seems highly likely we are on track for 100% shortly." ## Google Ads
- **AI Mode match-type experiment:** exact and phrase match keywords in regular Search campaigns can serve text ads in AI Mode "where there is explicit and direct user intent" (Ginny Marvin, Sept 4, replying to Anthony Higman's Sept 3 LinkedIn post; "a small experiment that recently kicked off"). AI Max/PMax remain the route for conversational intent and Highlighted Answers. Origins: [Higman's post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/anthony-higman-a3656a46_breaking-google-is-now-serving-regular-activity-7501374557593845760-EjIf) · [SER Sept 4](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ads-serving-restrictive-match-types-ai-mode-42024.html) · [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/google-tests-traditional-search-campaigns-in-ai-mode-487199) · [SEW Sept 8](https://searchenginewatch.com/google-now-serving-regular-search-campaigns-with-restrictive-match-types-in-ai-mode/).
- **Promotional credits invalidated after qualifying spend** (Melamed; Marvin escalated) — see conversation track. [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ads-credit-revoked-42049.html) · [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/advertisers-report-google-ads-credits-being-revoked-after-spending-488330).
- **Shopping ads' rising CTR is a shrinking denominator** — Mike Ryan (Smarter Ecommerce), LinkedIn ~Sept 6–7, via SER Sept 7 and SEL: across 175B impressions (thousands of Shopping/PMax campaigns, hundreds of accounts), median impressions fell ~24% (1.85M→1.4M) mid-2025 to mid-2026 while median CTR rose 1.20%→~1.55%; clicks flat or slightly down; Optmyzr separately found CTR +17% YoY. Hypothesis (verbatim): "Google checks the predicted CTR of a given query and serves AIOs preferentially for lower-probability queries in order to preserve revenue. Thus CTR climbs partly due to a shrinking denominator (fewer impressions) and partly due to selection/pruning (higher click propensity)." A "bridging strategy" toward "both/and" experiences with Shopping ads inside AIOs. Not proven; Google's docs don't confirm predicted-CTR routing. Origins: [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-shopping-ads-imp-ctr-42021.html) · [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/ai-overviews-may-be-affecting-shopping-ad-ctr-and-impressions-487265) · [TechWyse (European data: Shopping impressions −17%, clicks −6.5%)](https://www.techwyse.com/news/reports-data/rising-shopping-ad-ctr-is-masking-a-drop-in-visibility).
- **AI Mode product block all ads:** Search Engine Watch tested a headphones query and found every product inside the AI Mode answer was an ad, no organic listings (previously organic products in-answer, ads below). Single observation; monitor. (Via Anicca.)
- **Target CPA now literal:** post-Aug 17, a $10 target seeks ~$10 rather than beating it; Reva Minkoff (SMX Now) notes that's how tCPA was originally described in 2015–16. Reset targets to the number you want. (Via Anicca.)
- SER Sept 11 recap: AI dashboard rolling out to some advertisers; budget/bidding-target beta for Search; local customer optimization and store sales in Data Manager; loyalty-program segmentation; video campaign group beta; Merchant Center AI Performance report adds AI search intent, terms, attribution; Shopping "sold last week"/"X days left" labels; a "very early experiment" showing a new attribute in sponsored results; product videos in Merchant Center. ## OpenAI / ChatGPT Ads
- **Amazon DSP pilots ChatGPT Ads (Sept 10):** select US advertisers (Delta Vacations first named); managed service; CPC and CPM; product-feed ads auto-built from catalogs; text/image units beneath responses with sponsored labels; **OpenAI keeps control of delivery and placement**; aggregated metrics (impressions, clicks, CPC, CPM, cost per result). Digiday: Amazon's supply-chasing streak (Netflix, Roku, Spotify, SiriusXM, Disney/Hulu/ESPN); OpenAI reportedly targeting $100B ad revenue by 2030 (>200% CAGR). Adweek: OpenAI says the ads business is on track for $1B+ over the next 12 months; **Sensor Tower: ads served per user per hour in the US up 163% (August vs April)**. Origins: [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media-buying/amazon-brings-its-dsp-to-openais-chatgpt-ads-extending-its-supply-chasing-streak/) · [Marketing Dive](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/amazon-pilots-ad-services-in-chatgpt-what-marketers-need-to-know/829945/) · [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/commerce/amazon-and-openai-team-up-to-target-chatgpts-us-users/) · [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/amazon-pilots-chatgpt-ads-through-its-dsp-488026).
- **OpenAI reportedly stopped approving ads for standalone image- and audio-generation products** (The Information, Sept 9; communicated privately to partners). Adobe affected (Firefly promoted in the February pilot alongside Acrobat Studio, Albertsons, Target, Williams-Sonoma). Video's still open…for now." Same week: ChatGPT Images 2.5 shipped; ChatGPT Live voice launched in July; Adgully: outbound referral links restricted for some third-party image editor queries. OpenAI hasn't publicly confirmed; its policy reserves the right to refuse ads "for any reason." Origins: [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/openai-reportedly-blocks-rival-ai-tools-from-advertising-in-chatgpt-488019) · [PYMNTS (Wyatt quote)](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/openai-cuts-off-rivals-ads-chatgpt/) · [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/advertising/openai-tightens-ad-rules-bars-rival-ai-image-audio-services-ws-l-110339.htm).

## Cloudflare (deadline Sept 15 — just after the window)
- In-window state (Anicca Sept 11): Cloudflare's July plan would remove the exemption keeping Googlebot, Bingbot, Applebot outside AI-training blocks on Sept 15; sites with training restrictions could lose search crawling. **What shipped Sept 15 (post-window, for the editor's note):** a new "Disallow AI Training" setting plus an "Accountable" crawler designation — Google, Apple, Microsoft keep crawling for search under it; existing training blocks migrated to it automatically; only explicit **Block** now stops the three crawlers entirely (search included). "Block AI Bots" deprecated. Bing's robots.txt no-training support targeted early 2027. Origins: [Cloudflare blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/accountable-mixed-use-ai-crawlers/) · [press release](https://www.cloudflare.com/press/press-releases/2026/cloudflare-helps-end-the-search-or-ai-training-tradeoff/) · [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/cloudflare-lets-sites-disallow-ai-training-without-blocking-googlebot/589559/).
