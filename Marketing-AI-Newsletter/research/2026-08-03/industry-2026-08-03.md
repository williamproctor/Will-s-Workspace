# Industry, Studies & Regulation Research — Week of July 27–August 2, 2026

Compiled 2026-08-18 (retroactive). Primary synthesis: PPC Land weekly (https://ppc.land/bots-overtake-humans/).

## TAG / ANA / Fiducia: AI slop grades premium (published Tue July 28)

- First statistically rigorous sizing of AI-generated junk in programmatic: **slop = 1.3–2.4% of open-web programmatic spend** — and it scores better than clean supply on the quality signals buyers use.
- Slop IVT rate 0.05% vs 0.32% for clean supply; viewability 77.2% vs 74.9%; graded premium **>70% of the time**; **TrueCPM $7.08 for slop vs $6.15 for clean** — buyers paid more for fabricated supply.
- Method: Scott Cunningham (Cunningham.tech), DeepSee domain classification, Mobian page evaluation; $33.97M matched spend, 233M URLs, 4.45B impressions. 11,552 slop domains (parenting, travel, recipes, personal finance). Templated-site rate 30.0% vs 1.2% clean.
- 88% overlap with MFA (existing suppression catches most); the residual 12% escapes current frameworks and costs more than clean supply. Known publishers: effectively zero slop. Exposure varied 0.11%–13.84% of spend by advertiser; ~1 in 27 impressions on unknown domains is slop.
- Social flagged as fastest-growing environment: one vendor estimates 25–40% of social video inventory misaligned; consistent with earlier finding that ~1/3 of YouTube Shorts feed is AI-generated.
- Same benchmark: TrueAdSpend rebounded to 43.3% from 36.3%; bottom-half advertisers pay $19.04 per quality impression vs $7.46 top half.
- Counterpoint same week: DoubleVerify (Jul 30) — fraud down 41% NA / 45% EMEA on protected campaigns; unprotected campaigns see AI bots generate 10x more clicks than humans. HUMAN Security (Jul 30): complete AI scam operation costs ~$5,000 setup + $2,970/month. Confiant (Aug 1): SourTrade malvertising assembles malware in-browser, ran through Google/Meta/X ad infrastructure.

## IAB Australia: the bot majority (guidance published Tue July 28, Sydney summit)

- Cloudflare data cited: **automated requests = 57.5% of web traffic by June**; majority of crawler activity is AI systems, not search engines.
- Composition: ~52% of AI crawler requests feed model training; **only ~2.6% are real-time fetches triggered by a human question**.
- Guidance sorts crawlers into 5 categories (search indexers, AI training, live agents, ad ops, unverified) with 4 verdicts (allow / conditional / license / block). Training crawlers: block-or-license. Live agents: conditional, restricted on ad-funded pages.
- DataDome: AI-agent requests +45% QoQ to 17.7B in Q2; 80–88% of AI referral traffic originates from ChatGPT even as ChatGPT crawl volume declined; MCP traffic peaked near 500K requests/day.
- **Cloudflare default-blocks training/agent crawlers on ad-bearing pages for new domains from September 15, 2026** (July 1 policy also moved from charging AI firms per crawl to paying publishers per answer).
- robots.txt warning: Google and OpenAI user-triggered fetchers generally ignore it; Anthropic honors it. Enforcement belongs at the network edge. llms.txt: adoption grew 8.8x to ~39,000 sites; 97% of the files received zero AI requests.
- Publisher reality checks: All About Berlin lost 75% of traffic (zero-click at 68%); publisher ad supply fell 40% in Q2 while Google Search ads grew 17%. **Time started serving ads to AI agents** (Digiday, Jul 30) using TollBit to convert pages to agent-readable markdown; open question quoted from a publishing exec: can a bot be influenced by ads, and does an agent visit count as valid traffic under MRC terms?

## AI search saturation & commerce compression

- AI Overviews now appear on close to half of Google queries: **43% (Similarweb) / 48% (Semrush)** (Search Engine Roundtable, Jul 30); informational queries higher.
- Productrise (Jul 29): **AI Mode surfaces Shopping listings on 23% of shopping queries vs 88% in standard search** — a 95% cut in listing exposure (100K searches, 2M listings dataset).
- Chemist Warehouse: +220% AI Overview appearances in a quarter, partly on +46% retailer brand mentions across ChatGPT and Google AI Mode; no framework links citations to sales yet.
- Yelp survey (Jul 30, n=1,323): only 43% of Gen Z trusts AI summaries for restaurant decisions; written reviews beat influencer recommendations for dining out 78% vs 59%.
- SISTRIX (Jul 31): 2,112 B2B documents — ChatGPT text scored 37.7 readability vs Claude 47.7; prompt construction swings quality more than model choice.

## Regulation

- **Article 50 of the EU AI Act became applicable Sunday, August 2** (transparency for AI interactions + synthetic content marking; fines up to 3% of global turnover).
- **Meta signed the EU code of practice on AI content marking Tue July 28** — reversing its 2025 refusal (VP Markus Reinisch), five days before penalties attach. Open question: which party (platform/agency/advertiser) carries the visible labeling duty per ad.
- Brazil: banned paid ads containing deepfakes in the final 72 hours before elections (Jul 31); ANPD documented a deepfake ad fraud ring using Gisele Bündchen's image that moved ~R$20M before arrests.
- Italy: Garante fined Lusha €2M and banned it from Italian contact data outright (public Jul 27). Austria: noyb complaint over a 1,741-partner consent banner (~172 hours to read the policies). Poland: IAB Polska political-ads duty map under EU Reg 2024/900.
- New York synthetic-performer law still stacking with EU exposure (in force since June 9).
