## The Conversation

### Meta vs Anthropic: the AI ad wars

Meta launched "The Future Is for Everyone" July 23 — optimism, butterflies, "call us whatever the hell you want… we're betting on people." The internet noticed the soundtrack is Bowie's "Five Years" (a song about extinction, verses trimmed) and that the ad shows almost no actual AI. Six days before Meta's earnings. It reads as the answer to Anthropic's "hard questions" ad (July 9, ~3.3M X views), which Sam Altman mocked as looking like satire. Sharpest detail: the same Anthropic ad was **praised on LinkedIn and shredded on X** — same creative, opposite receptions by network. **Comment window: open through Wednesday's Meta earnings.** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/meta-launched-a-new-ai-optimism-ad-set-to-a-song-about-human-extinction/) · [Reception split](https://bretkerr.substack.com/p/the-messenger-paradox-how-anthropics)

### X deletes 42,000 AI reply bots

Nikita Bier, July 24: AI engaging users "without a human in the loop runs counter to our mission." Part of a 1.7M-bot purge since early 2026 — this wave hit tools real growth teams use. LinkedIn is reportedly suppressing generic AI comments too. Automated engagement is being reclassified from tactic to violation. **Comment window: open now** — a grounded "what survives (draft-with-review) vs what dies (autopilot)" post lands all week. [Bier's post](https://digg.com/tech/1ygslyhm) · [Analysis](https://startupfortune.com/x-removes-42000-ai-reply-bot-accounts-and-the-warning-to-growth-marketers-is-impossible-to-ignore/)

### Google's AI invented a bakery closure

An AI Overview told customers Anna Mae's Bakery closes July 31. It doesn't. Google blamed thin web data. Perfect miniature of the AI-visibility problem: the machine speaks for your brand whether or not you publish. ## This Week in Marketing AI

### Alphabet Q2: AI-search ads are revenue now — and paid ≠ cited

Search up 17% to $63.3B (July 22). AI Mode (1B+ users) carries ads: contextual sitelinks, Direct Offers, Highlighted Answers. AI Max out of beta, 500K+ advertisers, claimed 15% lift (Google-measured; independent tests disagreed on cost). SE Ranking: on keywords showing AI Mode ads, the advertiser's domain was among cited sources **11% of the time**. Paid placement, citations, and rankings are three separate surfaces. [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-search-ads-gain-17-to-63-3-billion-while-network-drops-1/) · [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-ai-mode-shows-ads-on-1-in-3-commercial-keywords/582976/)

### August 2: AI-content labels get 3%-of-turnover fines

EU AI Act Article 50 binds August 2 (guidelines published July 20; Google signed the Code July 24). Advertising is **excluded from the artistic carve-out** — synthetic spokespeople need the full label; assistive editing is exempt. New York live since June 9; California syncs to August 2. Liability sits with the advertiser everywhere. [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-signs-eu-ai-code-as-advertisers-face-3-turnover-fines-august-2/)

### ChatGPT Ads: conversion bidding on a dark auction

New: oCPC conversions objective, geo exclusions, AppsFlyer/Adjust, advanced matching, bulk API, product cards. Adthena tracks 7,378 ChatGPT advertisers (up 97x, small base) — but the Ads Manager shows only your own numbers, and UK costs swung 39–278% unexplained. Modern-scale spend, two-decades-ago reporting. [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/chatgpt-ads-budget-api-ad-formats-more-41756.html) · [PPC Land weekly](https://ppc.land/marketers-brief-ai-with-the-demographic-data-they-say-no-longer-works/)

### Similarweb: the assistant market split three ways

Gen-AI platforms: 9.5B monthly visits (+70% YoY). ChatGPT fell from >80% share to ~50%; June snapshots: Gemini ~27%, Claude ~9%. Citations up to 6.8% of ChatGPT answers; cited pages are deep pages while referral traffic lands on homepages; AI referrals convert at 7.1% (second only to paid search). Ads in 26% of US desktop chats — ~74% of exposure is still earned. Caveat: Similarweb and Adthena publish incompatible numbers; no standard exists. [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/chatgpt-loses-web-share-to-gemini-and-claude-as-ad-penetration-hits-26/)

### WARC: the brief is the bottleneck

88% more creative volume with AI; only 45% better quality. 67% brief models with demographics; 59% of the same marketers say demographics don't work; 17% add community insight. TikTok's Andy Yang: "not a technology gap, it is an intelligence gap." (TikTok co-commissioned.) [PPC Land weekly](https://ppc.land/marketers-brief-ai-with-the-demographic-data-they-say-no-longer-works/)

### Fractl: buyers still say "SEO"

81% call AI-search work "SEO" internally; "AEO" gets 3% of help-seeking searches. Buzzword pitches = #1 vendor red flag (36%); case studies beat acronyms 4:1. Budgets moved anyway: 24% of search/content spend now goes to AI visibility. [Fractl](https://www.frac.tl/ai-search-optimization-language/) · July 21

## Common Threads

### Authenticity is becoming platform policy
X purges AI replies, LinkedIn demotes AI comments, Meta ships human-verification badges. Genuinely human commentary appreciates as fake commentary gets purged.

### Paid ≠ cited
AI search now sells placements; citations stay earned (11% overlap; ~74% of chats ad-free). Two budgets, two disciplines.

### Production outran verification
88% more creative, 45% better; agents ship time savings with no accuracy rates; attestation fields nobody validates.

### Measurement is unsettled
Similarweb vs Adthena: 43% vs 18% AI Overview frequency. Single-vendor panels are hypotheses, not ground truth — say so in decks.
