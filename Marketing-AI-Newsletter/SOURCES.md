# Research Source List

Curated go-to sources for the weekly research pass. This list is a starting map, not a boundary — follow stories wherever they lead, but always end on a primary source. (The AV newsletter never committed a source list; this one does so any agent can run the research pass consistently.)

## The conversation layer (check first — this is the newsletter's priority track)

What marketing/AI X and LinkedIn are arguing about right now:

- **X**: posts from platform executives (Sam Altman, Mark Zuckerberg, Nikita Bier, Sundar Pichai, ad-platform leads), marketing/growth practitioners, and whatever campaign or dunk is circulating. Capture exact quotes, timestamps, and view counts where visible. If the XAgent X/Twitter MCP is configured with an API key (see `.cursor/mcp.json` in the workspace), use it for live trend/thread pulls; otherwise reconstruct via web search and coverage.
- **LinkedIn**: CMO/brand-lead posts around launches (companies increasingly announce there), the reception gap between LinkedIn and X on the same creative (often a story in itself).
- **Trade coverage of viral moments**: TechCrunch, The Verge, Business Insider, Adweek, Ad Age, B&T, Storyboard18, Engadget — fastest at writing up campaign backlashes and executive spats with quotes verified.
- **YouTube comments / community reception** on major brand campaigns (top comments get quoted in coverage and become part of the story).
- What to log per moment: who started it, the key posts (verbatim, attributed), when it started, how big (views/likes if reported), the split by platform, and whether the comment window is still open.

## Primary sources (check every week)

### Ad platforms & search
- Google: blog.google (Ads, Search, AI), Google Ads Help release notes, Search Central blog
- Meta: about.fb.com/news, Meta for Business updates
- Amazon Ads: advertising.amazon.com/blog
- TikTok: newsroom.tiktok.com, TikTok for Business
- LinkedIn: LinkedIn Ads blog / news.linkedin.com
- Microsoft Advertising blog; Reddit for Business; Pinterest Business; Snap for Business
- X/Twitter business blog

### AI vendors (marketing-relevant releases)
- OpenAI: openai.com/news
- Anthropic: anthropic.com/news
- Google DeepMind / Gemini: blog.google
- Perplexity: perplexity.ai/hub
- Adobe (GenStudio/Firefly), Canva, HubSpot, Salesforce (Marketing Cloud/Agentforce), Klaviyo, Semrush, Ahrefs product blogs

## Trade press

- Search Engine Land / Search Engine Roundtable (SEO + AI search shifts)
- Adweek, Ad Age, Marketing Brew, The Drum (advertising industry)
- MarTech.org, Marketing Dive (martech + industry data)
- Digiday, The Information, TechCrunch, The Verge (platforms, adtech, AI business)
- eMarketer/Insider Intelligence (forecasts and benchmark data)

## Studies, data, benchmarks

- Pew Research, Gartner, Forrester press releases
- Similarweb, Datos, BrightEdge, seoClarity (AI search traffic data)
- Artificial Analysis (model benchmarks) — for when model quality claims need an independent check
- Company earnings reports (Alphabet, Meta, Amazon quarterly ad revenue — seasonal)

## Voices (attribute to the person, not the show)

- Marketing/growth operators active on X and LinkedIn (rotate; verify quotes against the original post or transcript)
- Podcast transcripts: marketing and AI shows — quote the speaker by name per the source policy
- Conference keynotes and earnings-call quotes from platform executives

## Research pass structure

Three parallel tracks, saved to `research/YYYY-MM-DD/`:

1. **platforms** — primary-source sweep of the platform/vendor list above
2. **industry** — trade press + studies/funding/M&A
3. **voices** — attributable practitioner and executive commentary

Then a fourth synthesis artifact (**analysis**) chooses the lead story, maps common threads, and drafts candidate angles before writing begins.
