# Monday Signal — Week of August 31, 2026

> **AI agents got write access to live ad accounts (X Ads MCP's ten write tools, The Trade Desk opening its AI layer to Claude) while humans lost levers (Meta's placement exclusions gone, Google's Show more button gone, search clicks routed through encrypted redirects).** Meta will pay up to $18B and cap teen use at two hours a day. ## The Conversation

### Samsung's all-AI ad for a filmmakers' drive

The Portable SSD P9 launch film ran a "created using generative AI" disclosure for its whole runtime, selling 12K storage with footage nobody shot, to editors. Honor mocked it; r/videography dismantled it. Tom Goodwin's thread carried the lesson: "once an audience senses the work isn't real, the doubt doesn't stay with the ad. It transfers to the brand… Efficiency is a line item. Trust is the balance sheet." [Goodwin's post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/tomfgoodwin_that-samsung-made-an-entirely-ai-ad-is-astonishing-activity-7499387859070586880-ETJr)

### "Holy moly SEOs": AI Overviews expand on their own

Chris Long (Aug 27): Google is "revealing the AI Mode prompt box BY DEFAULT for some queries." Google confirmed: full answers, open Ask-anything box, organic pushed down, triggered "where our systems determine it's most useful." Pair with Brainlabs via Digiday: sessions down 10.5% across 54 clients, 46 of them losing traffic. [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-is-dynamically-expanding-ai-overviews-for-some-queries-486200) · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/986364/google-search-ai-overviews-auto-expand)

### Meta's placement exclusions vanished

From Aug 25, the placements control was gone from Ads Manager; value rules with a −90% floor replaced it. Exclusion became suppression, the last step in a sequence running since January 2025. Same day, AdExchanger documented Meta's ad engineers dispersing to OpenAI, TikTok, Amazon, and Roku. [PPC Land weekly](https://ppc.land/the-week-ai-agents-got-the-ad-account-and-meta-got-a-two-hour-clock/)

## This Week in Marketing AI

### AI agents got the ad account

X Ads MCP (Aug 24): 23 tools for any MCP client, ten of them writes; everything created paused, nothing spends until a human activates. The Trade Desk's Zuma (Aug 27) opens its AI layer to Claude; the 32% CPA claim compares new model to old, not to a control. IAB: a framework for measuring ads served to agents, due Nov 12. Agents can spend; counting comes later. [PPC Land on X Ads MCP](https://ppc.land/x-ads-mcp-gives-grok-and-claude-code-23-tools-to-run-ad-campaigns/)

### Meta: up to $18B and a two-hour teen clock

Settled Aug 26 with 47 states, DC, three territories, eight days into trial: $12.1B guaranteed, up to ~$5B contingent on rivals adopting matching limits, $10B charge this quarter, no admission. Product terms: two hours a day on Instagram and Facebook for ages 13–17, nighttime blocks, parental tools, "within months." Capped teen time is capped teen inventory. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/meta-reaches-18-billion-settlements-over-childrens-social-media-addiction-2026-08-26/) · [AP](https://apnews.com/article/meta-trial-instagram-settlement-97d342f2a33d835eda2356c5e1af9e37)

### Google keeps the transaction and encrypts the exit

AI Mode books hotels via Google Pay (Aug 27; Booking.com, Expedia, Hilton, Marriott, Wyndham). AI Overviews auto-expand (Aug 28). Search clicks route through encoded google.com/goto redirects (confirmed Aug 26; anti-scraping, but rank trackers now need hundreds of requests per SERP). More journey inside Google, fewer observable exits. [SER recap](https://www.seroundtable.com/video-08-28-2026-41963.html) · [Marketing O'Clock EP 448](https://marketingoclock.com/episodes/well-goto-a-world-with-google-searchs-goto-tracking-parameters/)

### AI search audited from three sides

Brainlabs/Digiday: sessions −10.5% across 54 clients. Bali study (arXiv 2608.07069): 85.6% of 4,776 venues never recommended by ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Perplexity; a website lifts odds 1.92x. Empirank: 1 in 9 OpenAI recommendations wrong. Adthena: ads on 4.47% of US ChatGPT queries, retail 39% of them. Distribution is being built faster than it's checked. [PPC Land weekly](https://ppc.land/the-week-ai-agents-got-the-ad-account-and-meta-got-a-two-hour-clock/)

### Nobody owns the audit

Sony v. Kroger: 392 unlicensed uses across dozens of banners, exposure ~$58.8M. A Twitch creator's class action says Amazon trained Nova Reel on streams without consent, pointing to same-day ToS and privacy amendments. Liquid Death sued over "zero sugar" cans listing allulose. Decentralized teams, machine-scale output, no owner for legal. [PPC Land weekly](https://ppc.land/the-week-ai-agents-got-the-ad-account-and-meta-got-a-two-hour-clock/)

### Microsoft AI Max everywhere, with a nuanced Max CPC answer

AI Max reached all Microsoft Advertising accounts. Navah Hopkins clarified: campaign-level setting, ad-group settings survive, and Max CPC stays for eCPC, Target Impression Share, and portfolio bidding because "not every brand can hit the conversion thresholds." Bing is testing "AI Overview" as the name for its Copilot Search box. [EP 447](https://poddtoppen.se/podcast/1401725029/marketing-oclock-digital-marketing-news/microsofts-ai-max-is-going-all-around-the-globe) · [Hopkins](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/marketing-o-clock_digitalmarketingnews-digitalmarketingpodcast-activity-7497639027114872832-ppHQ)

## Common Threads

### Hands off the wheel, by design
Agents got write access; Meta removed the placement lever; Google removed Show more and the direct link. Nobody announced the philosophy; together it's one.

### Trust is the balance sheet
Samsung saved on production and paid in brand; Meta books $10B for old design choices; Kroger's teams optimized output and skipped clearance.

### Distribution moved inside the answer
Hotel bookings in AI Mode, auto-expanding AIOs, Brainlabs' missing sessions. The page is becoming raw material.

### The audit gap
85.6% of businesses never recommended; 1 in 9 recommendations wrong; the IAB's agent-ads framework is due Nov 12.
