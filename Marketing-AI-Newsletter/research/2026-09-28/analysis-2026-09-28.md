## Common-thread candidates
1. **The storefront picked its agents.** Amazon blocks a shopper's agent and invites a seller's agent; Shopify and PayPal open the door to Muse; Google turns checkout on for merchants who didn't ask; banks write the terms they want. Access is being decided merchant by merchant, platform by platform, this quarter.
2. **Default-on is the launch strategy.** Native checkout in AI Mode "automatically included"; Shopify's agentic storefronts "active by default for eligible stores"; AI Max auto-upgrades DSA, ACA, and broad-match campaigns this month. The opt-out is where the decision now sits.
3. **The creator is the attack surface.** Kalshi's agency copied a creator's video and changed his face; Temu's partnership budget flowed to accounts ORL judged mostly fake; YouTube put likeness detection on phones. The trust signal in creator ads is the person, and this week showed how easily it's faked or replaced.
4. **Your dashboard is missing the new traffic.** AI Mode checkout orders never fire GA4; an AI Overview link to AI Mode may count as nothing in Search Console; Comscore says half of AI prompts now go somewhere other than ChatGPT; News UK says a fifth of its traffic wasn't human. Perry's line fits: measure the gaps.
