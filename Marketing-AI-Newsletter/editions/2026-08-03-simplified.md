## This Week in Marketing AI

### Earnings: the auction is a conversation between models

Meta ads $59.4B (+27%; prices +12%, US +20%); Advantage+ passed a $75B run-rate; the new Generative Recommender drove +8.3% clicks and +15.7% conversions on Facebook (all Meta-measured). Amazon ads $19.8B (+26%); Alexa+ prompt-clickers convert 48% more (seller-reported). Microsoft search ads +10% and decelerating while its agent registry added ~40M agents in two months. Reddit ads +64%, stock -12.5% on weak user growth; Huffman: AI Overviews "has yet to make a similar level of positive impact." [PPC Land weekly](https://ppc.land/bots-overtake-humans/) · July 29–30

### AI slop grades premium

TAG/ANA/Fiducia: slop is 1.3–2.4% of open-web programmatic spend and beats clean supply on the metrics buyers use: 0.05% IVT, 77.2% viewability, premium grades 70% of the time, and a higher price ($7.08 vs $6.15 TrueCPM). MFA suppression catches 88% of it. Known publishers showed effectively zero. [PPC Land weekly](https://ppc.land/bots-overtake-humans/) · July 28

### Bots hold 57.5% of the web; 2.6% of AI crawls serve a person

IAB Australia's crawler guidance (Cloudflare data): most crawling is AI now, half of it feeds training. Cloudflare default-blocks training/agent crawlers on ad pages for new domains from September 15 and moved to paying publishers per answer. Time now serves ads to AI agents via TollBit. llms.txt reality check: 97% of files got zero AI requests. [PPC Land weekly](https://ppc.land/bots-overtake-humans/) · July 28–30

### Article 50 is live; Meta signed the code it refused in 2025

EU AI Act transparency obligations became applicable August 2 (fines up to 3% of global turnover). Meta signed the marking code July 28. Google's AI labels are operational across Ads, Merchant Center, and Editor. Brazil banned paid deepfake ads in the 72 hours before elections after a R$20M Gisele Bündchen deepfake fraud. Still unresolved: who carries the visible label duty per ad. [PPC Land weekly](https://ppc.land/bots-overtake-humans/) · July 28–August 2

### Click-to-agent ads, and ChatGPT inside Google's auction insights

OpenAI is testing a ChatGPT Ads campaign type where the click opens a Business Agent conversation instead of a website (spotted July 31); Meta says 1M+ businesses run its Business Agent weekly. And a practitioner found ChatGPT listed in Google Ads auction insights since May, first receipts that the two platforms bid in overlapping auctions. [Paid Media Collective 224](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/paid-media-news-week-31-yoann-ferrand-zujec)

### GPT-5.6 prices fell up to 80%

Luna -80%, Terra -20% (July 29); frontier-class-last-year intelligence now ~6 cents on the dollar at ~9x speed. Re-run the math on every automation you shelved in spring. [Paid Media Collective 224](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/paid-media-news-week-31-yoann-ferrand-zujec)

## Common Threads

### Every ad dollar routes through an automated buyer
Advantage+, Ads Agent, AI Max, Reddit Max. The human job moves to inputs and verification.

### The quality stack graded fabrication premium
Slop beat clean supply on IVT, viewability, and price. Fraud checks became adversarial signals; verified human context became the scarce asset.

### The toll booths are going up
Majority-machine traffic, Cloudflare pay-per-answer, Time's agent ads. The question moved from "block crawlers?" to "what do we charge them?"

### Disclosure arrived on schedule
Article 50 live, Meta's holdout over, label tooling operational, Brazil's 72-hour rule. Compliance is now the operating environment.
