# Industry track — week of September 21–27, 2026

## Agentic commerce picks sides: Muse, Amazon, Shopify, PayPal, the banks

**Timing note:** Amazon's block began Sunday night, Sept 20 (last evening of the prior window); GeekWire published at 06:05 UTC Sept 21 (11:05 pm PT Sept 20). Straddle convention: the edition states the start date plainly and covers the in-window fallout (Sept 21–27).

- **Amazon blocks Muse (Sun Sept 20, night):** popup: "Continued access by an unauthorized AI agent violates Amazon's Conditions of Use, to which our customers have agreed." Spokesperson: "We think it's fairly straightforward that third-party applications that offer to make purchases on behalf of customers from other businesses should operate openly and respect service provider decisions about whether or not to participate." And: "Agentic third-party applications such as Muse have the same obligations, and we've requested that Meta remove Amazon from the experience." Amazon's complaints: no advance notice, the agent doesn't identify itself, appears to capture and store credentials, can reach account pages and order history; bypasses personalization (Adweek). Declined to comment on legal action. Meta: Muse "has no visibility into people's passwords or payment methods." Context: Amazon won a preliminary injunction against Perplexity in March, lost it Aug 4 at the Ninth Circuit (user, not the AI company, accesses the computers under the CFAA), rehearing denied Sept 10; contract/terms claims remain open, and the popup cites Conditions of Use, not hacking. Amazon's own Buy for Me identifies itself and lets brands opt out. Amazon ads: $68B+ last year. Origins: [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/amazon-blocks-metas-muse-ai-assistant-in-new-standoff-over-agentic-shopping/) (first to report, per CNBC) · [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/21/amazon-meta-muse-ai-agentic-shopping) · [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/commerce/amazon-locks-out-metas-muse-in-agentic-shopping-standoff/) · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/998078/amazon-blocks-meta-muse-ai-agent-shopping) · [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/amazon-blocks-meta-muse-ai-agent-shopping-site-2026-9)

## Courts, regulators, policy

- **UK CMA (Wed Sept 23):** strengthened user-choice proposals: choice screen at first use on Android and Chrome, then a yearly prompt; AI assistants eligible if they meet technical and security criteria; providers on the screen must "fairly attribute publisher content when used." Cites "the growing use of new services like ChatGPT and Perplexity." Consultation closes Oct 9; decision expected by year-end. Builds on publisher controls imposed June 3 and fair ranking/data portability June 17. Sarah Cardell quote (verbatim on GOV.UK). [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/cma-strengthens-proposals-allowing-people-choice-over-their-search-service) · [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2266226/uk-competition-watchdog-wants-to-give-android-and-chrome-users-more-search-engine-choices/)
- **Irish DPC fines Google €403M (Mon Sept 21):** location data in Web & App Activity, Location History, Location Accuracy (May 25, 2018 – Feb 4, 2020); lawfulness, fairness, transparency, retention; compliance order within six months; inquiry opened Feb 2020 after BEUC-led complaints. DPC's Graham Doyle: users could have been influenced "with ads." Irish Times: fines must be court-confirmed; of ~€4.5B DPC fines, ~€20M paid. [EDPB](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/news/the-irish-data-protection-commission-fines-google-403-000-000-eur-following-inquiry_en) · [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/sep/21/google-is-fined-more-than-400m-by-irish-regulator-over-its-use-of-location-data) · [Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/business/2026/09/22/explainer-why-it-may-take-years-for-google-to-pay-its-latest-irish-fine/)
