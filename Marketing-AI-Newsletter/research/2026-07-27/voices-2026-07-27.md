# Voices Research — Week of July 20–26, 2026

Attributable commentary from named individuals. Attribution policy: person, not publication/show. Compiled 2026-07-27.

## Philipp Schindler — SVP & Chief Business Officer, Google (July 22, Q2 earnings call)
- "Those who adopt our AI-powered campaigns like AI Max or PMax see an average of 15% more conversions or value on Search at a similar ROAS." (Company-reported figure; independent Nov 2025 tests disagreed on cost per conversion.)
- Described Highlighted Answers as "our latest experience placing clearly marked sponsored links inside list responses," showing early user traction.
- Attributed 17% Search growth to "many parts of our business working well together and very, very deep Gemini integration."
- Use: primary voice for the lead story. Everything he said is a vendor claim by definition — label as such.

## Sundar Pichai — CEO, Google/Alphabet (July 22, Q2 earnings call)
- Google is "sending billions of clicks to websites every week through AI features in Search."
- Runs directly against independent CTR data (Ahrefs Feb 2026: -58% CTR for top-ranked pages where AI Overviews appear) and publisher case data. The tension IS the story — quote with the counter-evidence.

## Andy Yang — Global Head of Creative & Brand Ads, TikTok (foreword to WARC's "The New Creative Advantage," covered July 22)
- The gap opening in AI-assisted creativity is "not a technology gap, it is an intelligence gap" — brands are briefing powerful tools with static demographics and legacy assumptions.
- Disclosure: TikTok co-commissioned the study and sells community/cultural signal. Still the sharpest frame of the week for the creative-quality problem.

## Mark Howard — COO, Time (coverage during the week)
- On producing content/ad pages that human readers never see, built for AI crawlers: the job is now serving two audiences — humans when they arrive, and machines as a secondary audience. Time routes AI crawlers to a stripped-down copy of its site.
- Context: Cloudflare data — bots are >50% of web traffic; crawl-to-referral ratios run 118:1 to ~50,000:1. "The machine audience" is now an operational reality at a major publisher.

## Sam Clarke — Managing Director, Head of Search, Crossmedia (Digiday coverage, week of July 20)
- Called Google's test letting buyers exclude search partner and Display Network inventory from Performance Max "fairly significant" — perceived lack of control was among the biggest pain points when the format launched.
- Supporting: David Dweck (Go Fish Digital) described both inventory sources as remnant supply advertisers were forced to opt into; Kaitlin McGrew (PMG) said control/reporting features had already lifted PMax spend among their advertisers.
- Use: practitioner counterweight — while platforms automate, buyers are winning back manual controls.

## Mark Abraham — Managing Director & Senior Partner, BCG (June 15 report, context)
- "Ninety percent of the CMOs in our survey agreed that GenAI is already reshaping how consumers discover and evaluate brands. But most marketing organizations are not yet built to compete in that environment."
- Older than the window (June 15) — use only as context, dated, if needed.
