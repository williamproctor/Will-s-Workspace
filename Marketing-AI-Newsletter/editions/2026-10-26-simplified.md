# Monday Signal — Week of October 26, 2026

## The Conversation

### Three studies found little AI-assistant traffic, and "GEO is a distraction" drew rebuttals

Marie Haynes put ChatGPT at 0.62% of Google organic's traffic across 100 sites (Oct 21) and posted, "I think GEO is a distraction." Mike King called judging AI search as a performance channel "the category error," and SparkToro, crediting him, called AI "more like a brand channel". The study authors (Haynes, Kevin Indig and The SEO Works) all sell search services or subscriptions. With 45 of Haynes's 100 sites tracking no GA4 key events, instrument conversions before setting 2027 budgets. [Haynes](https://www.mariehaynes.com/geo-is-a-distraction/) · [King](https://ipullrank.com/ai-search-is-a-branding-channel) · [SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com/blog/is-ai-visibility-a-marketing-boondoggle/)

### ChatGPT's ad rewrites switch on by default, and Meta's terms make advertisers "solely responsible"

OpenAI's advertiser email (via SER, Oct 20) says, "Starting Friday, October 30, 2026, we'll enable creative enhancements for your new and existing self-serve campaigns," and the ads "can run automatically" without review; PPC Land found the help page giving Oct 29. Meta's revised terms make customers "solely responsible" for its AI's risks (CIO.com, Oct 19), and Adweek found advertisers pulling AI-altered Meta ads. Opt out before Oct 29, check each campaign, and screenshot what you switch off. *Editor's note (Oct 26): the help page now says Oct 31, a Saturday, and calls it a Thursday.* [OpenAI's email, via SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/openai-chatgpt-ads-enabling-creative-enhancements-42344.html) · [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/openai-to-rewrite-chatgpt-ads-by-default-as-advertisers-get-days-to-opt-out/) · [CIO.com](https://www.cio.com/article/4237395/metas-new-enterprise-contract-says-if-its-ai-makes-a-mistake-it-will-legally-be-the-customers-fault.html) · [OpenAI Help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001614-creative-enhancements-for-chatgpt-ads)

### Just Eat paused its AI menu photos after one turned soda bread into a soda

Diners posted Just Eat's AI menu images on Reddit from Sept 21 ("AI vs Soda Bread"), and The Independent first reported the apology on Oct 19: "We're sorry for any inaccurate images that have been posted." Just Eat is removing them and paused the rollout; every image had an "AI-generated" label. Have a person review platform-generated creative before it goes live. [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/JustEatUK/comments/1wmakng/ai_vs_soda_bread/) · [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/uk/home-news/just-eat-ai-menus-takeaway-apology-b3069088.html)

## This Week in Marketing AI

### Meta's Q3 ad revenue rose 26%; Zuckerberg expects businesses to pay a fee on Muse purchases

Meta's Q3 ad revenue was $63.06 billion (Oct 21). Zuckerberg said more than 10 million people in the US and Canada set up Muse in "a month and a half," and that Meta will build commerce tools "so we get paid a fee based on the transactions that we help facilitate. We expect this will be paid for by businesses." Meta hasn't published a fee. [Meta](https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2026/Meta-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx) · [Transcript](https://www.medianama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/META-Q3-2026-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf)

### Meta pushed its new business terms to Nov 20, with no other changes

After publisher letters, Meta said Friday, Oct 23, that its revised terms take effect Nov 20, not Oct 30 (covered here Oct 19). Nieman Lab found "no other changes to the terms aside from the implementation date" and no version that mentions robots.txt, though a spokesperson said content owners "will still be able to use robots.txt to signal preferences." [Meta](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2481113349080785) · [Nieman Lab](https://www.niemanlab.org/2026/10/meta-delays-rollout-of-terms-that-would-allow-broad-scraping-of-news-sites-legal-experts-are-still-skeptical/)

### Apple says Safari 27 blocks identity-resolution companies at the domain level

WebKit's blog said Monday, Oct 19, that in Apple's 27 releases "Safari and WebKit block network access to identity resolution infrastructure to prevent cross-site tracking," by domain. Apple names no vendors and publishes no list. It's Apple's first formal statement on the block (covered here Oct 5 and Oct 12). [WebKit](https://webkit.org/blog/18438/blocking-identity-resolution-infrastructure/)

### ChatGPT Ads reached 89 countries; OpenAI counts over 100,000 advertisers since launch

OpenAI added 26 countries on Oct 21 and said "over 100,000 advertisers have run campaigns on ChatGPT," a count of every advertiser since launch, active or not. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-ads-expands-26-new-countries/)

### Salesforce agreed to buy Braze at about $5.66 billion in enterprise value

Salesforce will pay $47.50 a share in cash for Braze, the customer-engagement platform (Oct 20). Its release says marketers "now need agentic solutions that can understand customers, make decisions, and act in real time." [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/10/20/salesforce-signs-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-braze/)

### Netflix cut its 2026 ad revenue forecast to about $2.7 billion

Netflix's Q3 letter (Oct 20) put 2026 ad revenue at "~$2.7B vs. approximately $3B previously (due to slightly slower than expected rollout of some ad product capabilities)". Greg Peters said, "We are still growing our ads business over 80% this year." [Q3 letter](http://archive.fast-edgar.com/20261020/AML2B22CZ222S2B2222L2MZ2AOMGAV222282/ex991_q326.htm) · [Call transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/20/netflix-nflx-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)

## Angles for the Week

- **Opt out of ChatGPT's ad rewrites before October 29, campaign by campaign.** Campaign settings "take priority over account defaults," so check each one. Window: before Oct 29.
- **Meta's terms start November 20; read Section 5 before then.** It makes you "solely responsible" for AI output, and no version of the terms mentions robots.txt. Window: before Nov 20.
- **Before you fund GEO, find out what your site can measure.** Instrument conversions and ask signups how they heard about you. Window: 2027 planning, now.
- **Safari 27 blocks identity-resolution domains; inventory the ID vendors in your tags.** Test UID2, ID5 and RampID calls in Safari 27. Window: before holiday measurement reads.
- **Patch PHP sites now, and serve a 503 if one has to go offline.** Google's docs say 5xx URLs are "eventually dropped." Window: until every site is patched.
