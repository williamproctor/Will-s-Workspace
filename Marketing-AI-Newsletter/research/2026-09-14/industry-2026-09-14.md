# Industry track — week of September 7–13, 2026

## Studies and data

- **Orbit Media annual blogging survey:** 14% of marketers say their blog delivers "strong results," six points below the prior twelve-year low and roughly half the 26% of 2022. AI adoption in blogging reached 92.4% with no relationship to results in either direction. What correlated with decline: abandoning influencer collaboration, original research, keyword research, paid promotion, formal human editing, and consistent analytics use. Average post now takes 3h20m (from 4h+ in 2022), ~50 hours a year saved per marketer while reported performance slid. Ann Handley (MarketingProfs) called it a wake-up call about being "more discerning about which efforts they remove and which they keep." Source: Orbit Media survey, via [Anicca's search roundup](https://anicca.co.uk/blog/weekly-update-search-marketing-11-09-2026/) (primary report URL not surfaced this pass).
- **Exploding Topics consumer survey** (2,300+ US respondents): 57.5% of AI users decided not to buy something based on what a chatbot told them; almost two-thirds have at least partially replaced product-related Google searches with AI conversations; more than four in ten actively dislike AI chatbot ads. Via Anicca (primary URL not surfaced).
- **SE Ranking ChatGPT Ads test** (June; US/CA/AU/NZ): 97,131 impressions, 1,263 clicks, 1.30% CTR across 48 ads. Via Anicca.
- **Sensor Tower** (via Adweek): ads served per ChatGPT user per hour in the US up 163% in August vs April. [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/commerce/amazon-and-openai-team-up-to-target-chatgpts-us-users/)
- **AlsoAsked / Allintitle**: PAA 97% / 100% AI (see platforms).
- **Smarter Ecommerce / Optmyzr**: Shopping CTR up, impressions down (see platforms).
- **IAB Europe pan-European AI adoption survey** (~95 responses, five languages): 85% use AI tools for marketing; targeting 64%, content generation 61%; ad tech vendors 60% report KPI improvement vs agencies 48%; under a third of publishers report higher CPMs. Via [BizStack](https://bizstack.tech/ai-marketing-in-2026-three-layers-one-messy-reality/) / PPC Land explainer (https://ppc.land/ai-marketing/).
- **Gartner:** 93% of audit functions use AI, 60% lack a formal strategy (Anicca AI roundup).
- **TikTok Shop** cited by 34.7% of US shoppers as the marketplace most associated with fraud (Anicca).
