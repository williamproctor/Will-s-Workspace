## The Conversation

### Meta vs Anthropic: the AI ad wars

Meta launched "The Future Is for Everyone" July 23. Optimism, butterflies, "call us whatever the hell you want… we're betting on people." The internet noticed the soundtrack is Bowie's "Five Years" (a song about extinction, verses trimmed) and that the ad shows almost no actual AI, six days before Meta's earnings. It reads as the answer to Anthropic's "hard questions" ad (July 9, roughly 3.3M X views), which Sam Altman mocked as looking like satire. Sharpest detail: the same Anthropic ad was praised on LinkedIn and shredded on X. Same creative, opposite receptions by network. Comment window: open through Wednesday's Meta earnings. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/23/meta-launched-a-new-ai-optimism-ad-set-to-a-song-about-human-extinction/) · [Reception split](https://bretkerr.substack.com/p/the-messenger-paradox-how-anthropics)

### X deletes 42,000 AI reply bots

Nikita Bier, July 24: AI engaging users "without a human in the loop runs counter to our mission." Part of a purge of 1.7 million bots since early 2026, and this wave hit tools real growth teams use. LinkedIn is reportedly suppressing generic AI comments too. Automated engagement is being reclassified from tactic to violation. Comment window: open now; a grounded "what survives (draft-with-review) versus what dies (autopilot)" post lands all week. [Bier's post](https://digg.com/tech/1ygslyhm) · [Analysis](https://startupfortune.com/x-removes-42000-ai-reply-bot-accounts-and-the-warning-to-growth-marketers-is-impossible-to-ignore/)

### Google's AI invented a bakery closure

An AI Overview told customers Anna Mae's Bakery closes July 31. It doesn't. Google blamed thin web data. A perfect miniature of the AI-visibility problem: the machine speaks for your brand whether or not you publish. [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-ai-overview-forces-anna-maes-bakery-to-deny-fake-july-31-closing/) · July 26

## This Week in Marketing AI

### Alphabet Q2: AI-search ads are revenue now, and paid doesn't buy cited

Search up 17% to $63.3B (July 22). AI Mode, past 1B users, carries ads: contextual sitelinks, Direct Offers, Highlighted Answers. AI Max is out of beta with 500K+ advertisers and a claimed 15% lift (Google-measured; independent tests disagreed on cost). SE Ranking: on keywords showing AI Mode ads, the advertiser's domain was among cited sources 11% of the time. Paid placement, citations, and rankings are three separate surfaces. [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-search-ads-gain-17-to-63-3-billion-while-network-drops-1/) · [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-ai-mode-shows-ads-on-1-in-3-commercial-keywords/582976/)

### August 2: AI-content labels get fines up to 3% of turnover

EU AI Act Article 50 binds August 2 (guidelines published July 20; Google signed the Code July 24). Advertising is excluded from the artistic carve-out, so synthetic spokespeople need the full label; assistive editing is exempt. New York has been live since June 9, and California syncs to August 2. Liability sits with the advertiser everywhere. [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-signs-eu-ai-code-as-advertisers-face-3-turnover-fines-august-2/)

### ChatGPT Ads: conversion bidding on a dark auction

New this week: an oCPC conversions objective, geo exclusions, AppsFlyer/Adjust, advanced matching, a bulk API, product cards. Adthena tracks 7,378 ChatGPT advertisers (up 97x from a small base), but the Ads Manager shows only your own numbers, and UK costs swung 39–278% unexplained. Modern-scale spend, two-decades-ago reporting. [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/chatgpt-ads-budget-api-ad-formats-more-41756.html) · [PPC Land weekly](https://ppc.land/marketers-brief-ai-with-the-demographic-data-they-say-no-longer-works/)

### Similarweb: the assistant market split three ways

Gen-AI platforms drew 9.5B monthly visits, up 70% in a year. ChatGPT fell from above 80% share to about half; June snapshots put Gemini around 27% and Claude around 9%. Citations rose to 6.8% of ChatGPT answers. Cited pages are deep pages while referral traffic lands on homepages. AI referrals convert at 7.1%, second only to paid search. Ads appear in 26% of US desktop chats, which leaves roughly 74% of exposure earned. Caveat: Similarweb and Adthena publish incompatible numbers; no standard exists. [Similarweb report](https://www.similarweb.com/corp/reports/2026-generative-ai-landscape/)

### WARC: the brief is the bottleneck

88% of marketers report more creative volume with AI; only 45% report better quality. 67% brief models with demographics while 59% of the same marketers say demographics don't work, and just 17% add community insight. TikTok's Andy Yang: "not a technology gap, it is an intelligence gap." (TikTok co-commissioned.) [PPC Land weekly](https://ppc.land/marketers-brief-ai-with-the-demographic-data-they-say-no-longer-works/)

### Fractl: buyers still say "SEO"

81% call AI-search work "SEO" internally; "AEO" gets 3% of help-seeking searches. Buzzword pitches are the top vendor red flag at 36%, and case studies beat acronyms four to one. Budgets moved anyway: 24% of search/content spend now goes to AI visibility. [Fractl](https://www.frac.tl/ai-search-optimization-language/) · July 21

## Common Threads

### Authenticity is becoming platform policy
X purges AI replies, LinkedIn demotes AI comments, Meta ships human-verification badges. Genuinely human commentary appreciates as fake commentary gets purged.

### Paid doesn't buy cited
AI search now sells placements; citations stay earned (11% overlap, and about 74% of chats are ad-free). Two budgets, two disciplines.

### Production outran verification
88% more creative, 45% better. Agents ship time savings with no accuracy rates, and attestation fields go unvalidated.

### Measurement is unsettled
Similarweb vs Adthena: 43% vs 18% AI Overview frequency. Single-vendor panels are hypotheses, not ground truth. Say so in decks.
