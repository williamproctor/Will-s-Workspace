# Industry track — week of August 24–30, 2026

## Meta's teen settlement: up to $18B and a two-hour clock (Aug 26)

- 47 states, DC, and three territories filed a proposed consent judgment (N.D. Cal.) ending the federal trial that began Aug 18 before Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers (Instagram head Adam Mosseri had testified; Zuckerberg was expected). Guaranteed payments of at least $12.1B (ten installments of ~$1.166B plus $459.3M resolving Cambridge Analytica claims); up to ~$5B more in contingency installments that unlock only if competitors adopt matching time limits. Headline totals reported as $17B (AP) to $18B (Reuters/CNN). Florida and New Mexico didn't join; Texas took a separate deal worth $1B+. Meta admits no wrongdoing; expects a **$10B legal charge in Q3** (not in prior guidance). Four states had sought up to $1.4T in statutory penalties.
- **Product terms:** a daily two-hour limit on Instagram and Facebook for users 13–17, nighttime blocks, expanded parental supervision. For media buyers, capped teen time is capped teen inventory.
- Quotes: Colorado AG Phil Weiser: the relief goes "well beyond what any court has ordered or is likely to order." California AG Rob Bonta: Meta "has agreed to make massive transformations… and will do it within months." Tulane's Rob Lalka (MarketWatch): not a "Big Tobacco"-scale financial settlement.

## AI search audited from three sides

- **Brainlabs via Digiday (Aug 25):** 54 clients, 19 sectors, Jan 2025–Apr 2026; aggregate sessions fell 10.5% (140.1M → 125.4M); 46 of 54 clients lost traffic.
- **Bali venue study (arXiv 2608.07069, Aug 7, Norly Research):** 4,776 food venues, 2,208 queries across ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini/Perplexity; at least 85.6% of venues never recommended by any system; owning a website lifts odds 1.92x, review volume 1.64x.
- **Empirank:** of 182 businesses OpenAI systems recommended, 24 incorrect claims (~1 in 9), 479 unconfirmed.
- **Adthena:** retail takes 39% of ChatGPT advertising; ads reach 4.47% of US queries, one item per answer — the benchmark European buyers inherited when EEA ads launched Aug 24 without personalization.
- **IAB (confirmed Aug 24):** building a framework to measure and attribute ads served to AI agents, release targeted Nov 12, 2026, led by VP of AI Caroline Gigerich; UTM/referral data don't survive an agent reading a page and handing back a recommendation.
- Source for all five: PPC Land weekly (Digiday, arXiv, IAB underlying): https://ppc.land/the-week-ai-agents-got-the-ad-account-and-meta-got-a-two-hour-clock/

## Nobody owns the audit: three filings on decentralized content

- **Sony Music v. Kroger** (C.D. Cal., 2:26-cv-09358, filed Aug 21): at least 392 unauthorized uses of Sony recordings across Kroger-family social accounts; fourteen prior Sony licenses 2017–2025; first notice June 30, 2025; statutory exposure ceiling ~$58.8M.
- **Pandiscia v. Twitch/Amazon** (N.D. Cal., 3:26-cv-08721, filed Aug 20): class action alleging ~two years of streams, VODs, clips, chat logs fed into Amazon's Nova Reel video model without consent; ToS and privacy notice both amended Aug 12, 2026 to add generative-AI language; benchmarks against Google–Reddit (~$60M/yr) licensing.
- **Williamson v. Supplying Demand** (Liquid Death; N.D. Cal., 3:26-cv-08839, Aug 24): "zero sugar" cans listing allulose second by weight.
- **eHarmony** found liable on five counts under Australian Consumer Law (ACCC release 99/26).
