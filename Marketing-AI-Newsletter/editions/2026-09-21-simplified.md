## This Week in Marketing AI

### The ad-tech opinion: AdX into Prebid, AdWords out of DFP, six years of monitoring

106 pages, unsealed Tuesday. No breakup ("neither realistic nor needed"). AdX and DFP must integrate with Prebid on "functionally equivalent" terms; AdX bids into rival ad servers on DFP's terms; DFP's winner selection gets documented; real-time bid data goes to publishers; no First/Last Look. Buy side: remedies apply to AdWords, "not as to DV360"; AdWords can't bid directly into DFP. Six-year term (the government wanted 15); monitor with source-code access; effective 60 days after the Final Judgment, draft due early October. [The opinion](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.vaed.533508/gov.uscourts.vaed.533508.1858.0.pdf) · [AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/platforms/the-court-just-unsealed-judge-brinkemas-remedies-decision-in-the-google-ad-tech-antitrust-case-heres-your-tldr/)

### Google pays some publishers for shaping AI answers; links don't count

Invite-only "AI contribution pilot" in Search Console (Digiday, Sept 14): pays when content "contributes significantly" to answers in Gemini, AI Overviews, and AI Mode, during generation only. Monthly earnings widget, no method disclosed, opt out anytime; dozens of publishers approached. Critics, per Digiday: "less like a meaningful payout and more like a legal fig leaf." For owned content: factual density and groundable pages now have a price. [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/google-rolls-out-pay-per-use-ai-licensing-program-to-publishers/) · [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-al-contribution-pilot-42076.html)

### Ads moved inside the answer

OpenAI (Sept 16): Sponsored Agents let users chat with a business's agent after an ad, "distinct from ChatGPT's independent answers"; ads built from prompts in ChatGPT Work; HubSpot (first CRM partner) and Shopify (first ecommerce partner) integrations. Google tested sponsored anchor-text links inside AI Mode answers. Ads dressed as citations may win placement and lose the click; disclosure design is now on the media plan. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/reimagining-advertising-with-ai/) · [SER on AI Mode](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ai-mode-text-link-ads-42082.html)

### Google's holiday playbook

Sept 16: AI performance insights (share of voice across AI Mode and AIOs, no click metric) GA in the US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, India; Business Agent beta inside YouTube ads; UCP cart transfer lets shoppers finish on the merchant's own site (US now; AU/CA next year). lululemon's conversational attributes appeared in 50% of relevant AI Mode recommendations. The feed writes part of the answer. [Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/google-shopping-updates-holiday-shopping/) · [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-ucp-update-lets-merchants-enable-cart-transfer-to-site/589713/)

### Europe's remedy reached the shopping aisle

By Sept 18, free product listings and "popular products" carousels were gone across the EEA, down ~90–100% (Productrise), replaced by "Comparison Sites" units; Ginny Marvin confirmed a DMA requirement. Local business queries joined the aggregator/supplier units. For EU clients: a channel change, with CSS and paid Shopping as the levers. [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-drops-free-product-listings-eea-42113.html) · [SEJ on local](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-adds-local-businesses-to-eea-search-result-units/589955/)

### Microsoft's AI-ad rules

Disclose when required, close to the content and ideally inside the asset; keep watermarks, metadata, and provenance intact (interfering is grounds for rejection); a label doesn't save a deepfake or unauthorized likeness. Audit export steps that strip metadata. [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/microsoft-advertising-sets-rules-for-ai-generated-ads-488429)

## Common Threads

### The answer became the ad slot
Sponsored Agents, sponsored links inside AI Mode answers, Business Agent inside YouTube ads. None came with a click-through benchmark.

### Money is moving toward whoever shapes the answer
Google pays for contribution, Profound valued at $1.8B, Europe's shelf space handed to comparison services. The price lists are private.

### Supervision instead of surgery
A six-year monitor over divestiture, TikTok's decree kept in force, a European remedy under watch.

### Exposure gets measured; effect waits
Novig's 911% spike with 4% about the app; a full August 17 read in mid-October; earnings with no method; AI insights with no clicks.
