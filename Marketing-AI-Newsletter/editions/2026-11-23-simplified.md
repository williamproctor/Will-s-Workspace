## The Conversation

### Think with Google said content, not AI, made brands invisible in Search, and SEOs pushed back

On Nov 19 Think with Google paired "Stop the 'Top 10' listicle" with "start leading with first-hand expertise" in advice to CMOs, linking an ads-side article in which a running store could make a video on "exactly why a specific customer's running shoes collapsed"; it's a marketing post, not a ranking change. Eli Schwartz's version reads "AEO VISIBILITY IS NOT REAL." (both via SEJ), and Daniel Foley Carter called AI Overviews "a nightmare for SEOs and digital marketers" (via SEL). [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-told-cmos-to-stop-top-10-listicles/598310/) · [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-ai-search-advice-seo-backlash-502610)

### Malte Landwehr said two domains that publicized scaled AI content lost rankings in the core update

Landwehr, of Peec AI, which sells AI-search visibility tracking, posted on Nov 16 that "Two domains that publicly spoke about the scaled rollout of AI content in the past lost Google rankings"; he didn't name them. Glenn Gabe and SISTRIX date the impact to Nov 13, SISTRIX sees "no particular sector that has been affected more than others," and Google hasn't tied the update to AI content. Lily Ray wrote that content made "for search engines at scale, with no human reviewing it" eventually "becomes a liability." *Editor's note (Nov 23): When we checked, the Search Status Dashboard still showed no completion for the update.* [Landwehr on X](https://x.com/MalteLandwehr/status/2122235819385598039) · [SISTRIX](https://www.sistrix.com/blog/november-2026-core-update-radar-visibility-analysis/) · [Ray's essay](https://lilyraynyc.substack.com/p/why-building-trust-through-real-people)

### ChatGPT answers started carrying Black Friday coupon codes with Copy buttons

On Nov 19 Metehan Yeşilyurt posted, "ChatGPT just turned Black Friday coupons into a widget"; by his tracking the deal cards went "from about 3% to 16% of ChatGPT answers that show products, in just four weeks." He isn't sure "if this is a test or a rollout," and we found no OpenAI statement. [Yeşilyurt's post](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:ugcPost:7529194932222185472/) · [Yeşilyurt's Substack](https://metehanai.substack.com/p/chatgpt-is-giving-out-black-friday)

## This Week in Marketing AI

### Muse's shopping skill now says "Do not search Amazon," and Jassy wants "a fair exchange of value" from outside agents

Muse launched in Australia on Nov 17 and added Loblaw, its first Canadian retailer, on Nov 18. Jassy said outside agents also need "a good customer experience, which it hasn't been to date" (via Business Insider), and Salesforce, which sells commerce software, projects that "Twenty percent of holiday shopping traffic will be nonhuman" without saying what counts. [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/do-not-search-amazon-metas-muse-now-shops-elsewhere-by-default/) · [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/amazon-ai-shopping-agents-muse-instinct-dots-2026-11) · [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/cyber-week-ai-sales-2026/)

### Microsoft launched ads in Bing's AI Overviews and rebuilt its bidding models

Microsoft said Nov 19 that "Ads in AI Overviews are now available in all markets except China," with "no separate campaign set-up required" for eligible campaigns, including Performance Max and AI Max. On Nov 18 it announced "its biggest upgrade to bidding models in a decade"; its reported lifts compare strategy switches, not the new model alone. [Microsoft Advertising, Nov 19](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/november-2026/put-ai-to-work-engaging-customers-in-more-places-whats-new-in-microsoft-advertising-this-november) · [Microsoft Advertising, Nov 18](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/november-2026/building-the-next-generation-of-automated-bidding-at-microsoft-advertising)

### Anthropic priced its IPO at $83, closed its first day at $97.17, and described no ad product

Anthropic priced 980 million shares at $83 on Nov 18, above its $70–$78 range, and closed its first Nasdaq day on Nov 19 at $97.17. Its prospectus plans to "invest in brand and marketing" and, as we read it, describes no advertising product. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering) · [Prospectus (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074944/anthropicpbc-424b4.htm) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/19/anthropic-ipo-ant-live-updates.html)

### Google's homepage added a sidebar for recent AI searches, and a site's AI opt-out barely moved its clicks

On Nov 19 Robby Stein announced a desktop-homepage sidebar to "jump back into your recent AI searches" (via Search Engine Land). On Nov 22 Brodie Clark posted that a site that left AI Overviews and AI Mode saw an effect on clicks that was "Barely even noticeable"; he didn't name it or publish figures, per PPC Land. [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-rolling-out-a-collapsible-sidebar-for-google-search-502587) · [Clark's post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/brodieclark_this-has-been-pretty-eye-opening-to-witness-activity-7530248034346262528-lA5R) · [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/site-exits-google-ai-overviews-and-ai-mode-clicks-barely-move/)

### Dentsu cut its organic growth guidance to "c. (1)%" and Bloomreach bought AthenaHQ

Dentsu reported a 3.7% organic decline for the third quarter on Nov 16 and changed its organic growth guidance "from '0-1%' to 'c. (1)%.'" The same day Bloomreach bought AthenaHQ, a Y Combinator-backed AEO platform, for an undisclosed price. [Dentsu's results deck (PDF)](https://www.nikkei.com/markets/ir/irftp/data/tdnr/tdnetg3/20261116/g8gtrc/140120261116580808.pdf) · [Bloomreach](https://www.bloomreach.com/en/news/2026/as-ai-reshapes-how-consumers-discover-brands-bloomreach-acquires-aeo-leader-athenahq-to-turn-ai-visibility-into-measurable-revenue/)

### Coca-Cola's US holiday ad switched from AI to live action, and the company says AI remains "a tool"

Coca-Cola's US "Enjoy Joy" campaign launched Nov 19 with a live-action film by Ogilvy, as Marketing Dive reported first, after the brand's divisive AI-generated holiday ads. A spokesperson told MediaPost, "This does not signal a broader shift," and that human storytellers "remain at the center of the creative process, with AI serving as a tool." [Marketing Dive](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/coca-cola-pivots-from-divisive-ai-generated-ads-for-holiday-campaign/875215/) · [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/419592/coke-drops-enjoy-joy-with-key-ingredient-humans.html)

## Common Threads

### The checkout moved into the answer
Muse checks out Loblaw baskets in the chat, Bing sells ads inside AI Overviews, and ChatGPT's deal cards carry the code.

### Access turned into terms
Jassy wants "a fair exchange of value" from outside agents, Muse now says "Do not search Amazon," and OpenAI and Meta are reworking their business terms.

### The content arguments turned on human review
Landwehr's two domains had publicized scaled AI content, Ray drew her line at "no human reviewing it," and Coca-Cola keeps human storytellers "at the center."

### Impressions and outcomes came apart
Clark's site left AI Overviews and AI Mode and kept its clicks roughly level, and a code copied from a ChatGPT card leaves no referral.
