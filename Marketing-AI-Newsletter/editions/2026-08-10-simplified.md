## This Week in Marketing AI

### Exact match expands 29% of the time, and the migration is now mandatory

smec's Mike Ryan, across 383M ecommerce impressions: more than 25% of exact-match impressions were expanded by AI Max search-term matching, rising to ~29% by July; keyword-row reporting can't split expanded from literal. Google claims a 15% conversion lift (its own measurement); independent tests keep finding lower ROAS. September 1 extends AI Max to accounts that didn't volunteer. [smec analysis](https://ppc.land/exact-match-loses-1-in-4-impressions-to-ai-max-smec-finds/) · [Migration](https://ppc.land/google-ads-ai-max-auto-upgrade-lands-september-1-will-ads-all-look-alike/)

### ChatGPT Ads goes mass-market

$25 campaign minimums; product feed carousels live; official expansion to Brazil and Mexico; a descriptive heading test above ads. Density logging: 73.6% of shopping questions already return an ad (54,899 placements, 1,844 advertisers). August 17: automatic advanced matching becomes the pixel default unless reviewed. First academic study of the channel is out. [PMC 225](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/paid-media-news-week-32-yoann-ferrand-uitsc)

### Nielsen buys DoubleVerify ($2.15B)

The largest independent verification vendor merges into the audience-measurement incumbent, two weeks after research showed verification signals grading AI slop as premium. Neutrality and pricing questions get sharper. [PMC 225, citing AdExchanger](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/paid-media-news-week-32-yoann-ferrand-uitsc) · August 5

### Holdcos are reselling AI compute at a markup

Token costs bundled into principal-media commitments, bought wholesale, resold as a "discount against list," with margins unverifiable from outside. Meanwhile a Dutch agency owner's diagram made the rounds: agentic buying automated the old org chart (twelve platform endpoints, each its own MCP call) instead of redesigning it. [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/agencies-turn-ai-tokens-into-a-margin-business-as-agentic-spend-stalls/) · August 2–3

### Amazon Sponsored Products left Amazon

Placements extended to premium third-party sites, apps, and select social platforms (creators included), using existing bids and budgets, default-on in the US and Canada. Check placement reporting before your retail budget quietly buys the open web. [PMC 225](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/paid-media-news-week-32-yoann-ferrand-uitsc) · August 4

### A publisher's before-and-after: Google fell to 21% of traffic

People Inc. told investors Google Search is 21% of its traffic, down from nearly two-thirds; it's holding ad revenue flat by packaging offsite content. Cleanest major-publisher datapoint yet on the AI-answers traffic shift. [Via PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-ads-ai-max-auto-upgrade-lands-september-1-will-ads-all-look-alike/) · August 5
