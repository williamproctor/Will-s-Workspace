- **How LinkedIn detects AI slop, with a number (Thu Oct 8; follow-up to LinkedIn's Sept 15 AI-slop post, which the 2026-10-05 research treats as pre-window context):** VP of Engineering Tim Jurka, in a LinkedIn article: "We’ve heard you: AI slop ain’t it."
