# Monday Signal — Week of November 2, 2026

> **Google expanded ads in AI Mode to 10 more countries on Wednesday, Oct 28, and that evening Alphabet's Philipp Schindler said the strong growth in AI Mode queries "had an impact because of the lower monetization we were seeing in AI Mode."** Andy Jassy said outside shopping agents aren't meaningfully affecting Amazon's ads; Meta said it will consult before changing its terms, an Oslo court barred the change, and the CMA opened a case; four companies backed one AI-training token; and Anthropic filed to go public. ## The Conversation

### Reddit's CEO said "AI Mode does not drive traffic," and Reddit's stock fell about 30% the next day

On Reddit's Oct 29 call, Steve Huffman said search traffic dropped "by more than 1 million referrals on average per week" in October and that "the AI Mode does not drive traffic, not for us and not to anybody as far as I know." Reddit's 10-Q plans login tests abroad this quarter. Track Reddit-sourced citations in AI answers as their own line. [Motley Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/30/reddit-rddt-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/) · [Reddit's 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1713445/000171344526000116/rddt-20260930.htm) · [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/382276/reddit-stock-plunges-30-googles-ai-search)

### Cyrus Shepard opted a site out of Google's AI answers, and its regular organic clicks fell too

Shepard posted (Oct 28) that AI impressions went "immediately to 0" and "regular organic clicks ALSO DROPPED when AI features were disabled." SER says he used Google's Search generative AI opt-out controls; Search Engine Land says he "didn't disclose the site's traffic volume, test dates, size of the organic click decline, or opt-out method." Test on one section before opting out sitewide. [Shepard's post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cyrusshepard_case-study-what-happens-when-you-opt-your-activity-7521340041206407169-Dhue) · [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ai-overviews-mode-ctr-42421.html) · [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-ai-opt-out-test-organic-clicks-drop-499898)

### Meta turned on product browsing in existing ads Oct 28, and ChatGPT's ad rewrites switched on Oct 30

These continue our Oct 19 (Meta) and Oct 26 (ChatGPT) stories. Meta cites "3% more conversions" from its own test, and OpenAI's help page, which now gives Oct 30 (last week's editor's note said Oct 31), says changes over time "do not, on their own, establish the effect of creative enhancements." Keep one opted-out campaign per platform as a holdout. [Meta's notice, via PPC Land](https://ppc.land/meta-will-turn-on-product-browsing-in-existing-ads-on-october-28/) · [OpenAI Help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001614-creative-enhancements-for-chatgpt-ads)

## This Week in Marketing AI

### Google took ads in AI Mode to 10 more countries as Alphabet cited "lower monetization"

Brazil, Mexico and the UK follow in Q4. Existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns are "automatically eligible," and advertisers "can't manually write, edit, approve, or opt out of" the AI explainers next to ads. [Google](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/ads-in-ai-mode-global/) · [Google Ads Help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/18826097?hl=en-GB) · [Motley Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/29/alphabet-googl-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)

### Amazon's ad revenue rose 10.9%, and Jassy says outside shopping agents aren't "meaningfully impacting Amazon Ads"

Ad revenue was $19.64 billion (Oct 29), with Q4 growth expected "in the high teens." Jassy said "Few customers are doing much shopping this way yet." [Amazon](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/news-release/news-release-details/2026/Amazon-com-Announces-Third-Quarter-Results/default.aspx) · [Call transcript](https://openfilings.org/companies/us/AMZN/transcripts/b9f4c15d-91f5-40b4-8c5b-c630094ce021)

### Meta said it will consult before changing its terms, an Oslo court barred the change, and the CMA opened a case

This continues our Oct 26 story. Meta's Andy Stone said (as quoted by Nieman Lab) the consultation "could extend beyond November 20, in which case we'd delay implementation." The Oslo order (Oct 28) holds until the change's legality is decided, and its text isn't published; the CMA's Competition Act case opened Oct 30. [Nieman Lab](https://www.niemanlab.org/2026/10/meta-pledges-to-consult-publishers-before-finalizing-its-new-terms/) · [MBL](https://www.mediebedriftene.no/artikler/2026/norwegian-media-companies-prevail-in-court-against-meta/) · [CMA](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/cma-opens-investigation-into-metas-commercial-terms)

### Google, Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthropic backed one robots.txt token for AI training

Anthropic's K. Kelley updated the Internet-Draft on Oct 30; it has "no formal standing in the IETF standards process." No company honors the token yet. Starting "in 2027," Google plans to read the token as a Google-Extended disallow unless Google-Extended is named; Microsoft honors it from Feb 1, 2027, and OpenAI says "in the coming months." [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-kelley-aipref-training-preference-signal/) · [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/10/ai-training-control) · [Bing blog](https://blogs.bing.com/search/2026/10/your-content-your-choice-clearer-preferences-for-training-and-grounding/)

## Common Threads

### AI answers cost clicks, and each company counts the loss differently
Reddit counts referrals, Google cites "lower monetization," and Shepard estimates CTR on impressions; label the metric before comparing.

### The defaults switched on, and the responsibility stayed with advertisers
Meta's product browsing and ChatGPT's rewrites run unless you opt out, and Pennsylvania's disclosure rule starts Dec 21.

### Opting out of AI now costs visibility, and the cost differs by platform
Bing warns of "reduced brand visibility in AI experiences," and Shepard lost organic clicks when he opted out.

### Amazon backs its own shopping agent, and Pinterest and Reddit argue for the destination
Jassy backs Alexa for Shopping, Ready says "shopping is not a task to be automated," and Huffman wants Reddit to be "a direct destination."
