## Novel Ideas

### Idea 1: Vidu Q3 — AI Pipelines Built for Serialized Content
- **Source:** X: Vidu Q3 at SXSW (~Mar 10)
- **What's new:** While every other AI video tool focuses on one-off generation, Vidu Q3 is the first pipeline specifically designed for animated series production. Character consistency across episodes, automated lip-sync, and asset reuse solve the biggest pain points that have kept AI video out of serialized content.
- **AV Implication:** This is the missing piece for AV teams exploring AI video for recurring content (training series, internal communications, branded content). Watch this space closely.

### Idea 2: Fish Audio S2's Word-Level Emotion Tags
- **Source:** X: Fish Audio S2 release (~Mar 10)
- **What's new:** Instead of choosing a single "voice style" for an entire narration, S2 lets you tag individual words or phrases with emotions like [excited], [somber], [whispered]. This is granular creative control that no commercial TTS has offered before — and it's open source.
- **AV Implication:** This changes the TTS workflow from "pick a voice and hope it sounds right" to "direct the performance word by word." Audio teams should evaluate this immediately.

### Idea 3: Claude Cowork's "Skills" System — Reusable Agent Instructions
- **Source:** Podcast: Latent Space — Felix Rieseberg / Anthropic (Mar 17)
- **What's new:** Claude Cowork introduced "skills" — lightweight markdown-based instruction sets that teach the agent new capabilities without code. Instead of building MCP integrations, you write a skill file that tells the agent what to do, and it figures out the execution. Felix Rieseberg described this as more practical than rigid tool schemas for knowledge work.
- **AV Implication:** This pattern is directly applicable to AV department automation. Imagine writing a skill file that says "when I drop a video in this folder, generate a transcript, create timestamps, and draft social media clips" — and the agent handles the rest.
