## The Conversation

### John Lewis's Christmas ad drew scorn on X and 5.6 stars from System1, and Dior explained its "all hand-drawn" film

Carma put social sentiment on "Gloria and Pickles" (Nov 3) at 77% negative; System1's representative panel gave it 5.6 stars, and Andrew Tindall posted that "only 6% felt real contempt. But 69% loved it." Dior billed its Nov 4 film as "all hand-drawn," and on Nov 7 the film's producer told Variety they "used an automated interpolation system for a very small part of the process". Very disclosed "AI-assisted" production on day one. Say in the press kit what was drawn, shot, generated or automated. [Tindall's post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/andrew-tindall_advertising-marketing-activity-7523767013614194688-emRP) · [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/global/sylvain-chomet-denies-ai-accusations-dior-robert-pattinson-1236941945/) · [The Very Group](https://www.theverygroup.com/media/news/2026/new-flamingo-family-leads-verys-christmas-creative/)

### A working paper says "ChatGPT's ads don't pay," and OpenAI swaps bid caps for targets Nov 9

Christian Schulze and Maximilian Kaiser's paper (Grips Intelligence data; Kaiser advises Grips) finds a paid ChatGPT visit earns $0.89 per session against $2.45 for paid Google Search, so a ChatGPT click would have to cost $0.26 to match; the largest known account paid $1.72. From Nov 9, new campaigns get Target CPC, which lets OpenAI's system "bid above or below your target in individual auctions," and Max CPC retires Jan 1, 2027. Set the target from your own revenue per session. *Editor's note (Nov 9): OpenAI's VistaPrint case study reports "5.9x return on ad spend" for one US campaign, based on VistaPrint-reported, platform-attributed sales.* [The paper (PDF)](https://ecommerceresearch.org/chatgpt-ads/Kaiser-Schulze-2026-Can-Advertising-Fund-Free-AI.pdf) · [OpenAI Help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001634-bidding-strategies-and-how-pricing-works-in-chatgpt-ads) · [OpenAI's case study](https://openai.com/ads/customers/vistaprint/)

### More than 6 million of Facebook's AI-written "Answers" pages are in Google's index

Saul Westbrook (Nov 4) estimated 40.6 million October visits to facebook.com/fb-answers/ from Semrush data and found that "LLMs are blocked from crawling these pages"; Glenn Gabe counted "6M+ urls indexed there" (on X, via SER). Google and Meta haven't commented. Search the directory for your brand and log what the pages say. [Peripheral Creative](https://www.peripheralcreative.com/news/facebook-answers-ai-generated-pages) · [Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/facebook-answers-google-42467.html)

## This Week in Marketing AI

### Midterm political ads set a record $11.87 billion, and most AI-made ads ran without a disclaimer

Wesleyan Media Project counted 289 AI-generated political ads, and only 27% carried disclaimers through mid-October (via USA Today). Voters weighed 41 data-center ballot measures, AI stocks "sold off sharply" on Nov 5 (CNBC), and OpenAI pledged to "pay the full cost of the electricity we use." [The Current](https://www.thecurrent.com/media/political-ad-spending-record-2026-ctv) · [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/elections/2026/10/30/slopaganda-ai-campaign-ads-skyrocket-2026/92368828007/) · [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/setting-new-standard-responsible-ai-infrastructure)

### Meta paused its new terms, and a São Paulo court barred it from using Brazilian journalism without authorization

Meta said Nov 5 that its updated terms "are now paused," with no new date. On Nov 3 a São Paulo judge barred Meta from using journalistic content without authorization in a preliminary order issued without hearing Meta. [Nieman Lab](https://www.niemanlab.org/2026/11/meta-pulls-down-controversial-terms-of-service/) · [ANJ, ABERT and ANER (PDF)](https://aner.org.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/ANJ-ABERT-e-ANER-liminar-versao-final.pdf)

### AI Mode added home-services booking, a fourth ad was sighted, and Performance Max may hand brand queries back

Since Nov 4, AI Mode links to booking through Angi, Thumbtack, Housecall Pro and Jobber in the US, as partner integrations, not ads. SER reported four sponsored results under one AI Mode answer, which Google hasn't confirmed, and Google's help text now says Performance Max "may also filter out navigational or brand-specific search queries." [Stein on X](https://x.com/rmstein/status/2118027605018632408) · [SER on the fourth ad](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ai-mode-four-ads-42443.html) · [SER on Performance Max](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ads-pmax-brand-queries-42438.html)

### Shopify's AI-attributed orders grew 3.3x, HubSpot said AI is changing discovery, and Snap's ad revenue grew 4%

Harley Finkelstein said, "AI traffic is up 2.6x year-over-year. Orders are growing even faster at 3.3x," while noting "the volume is still small here." Snap reported "Advertising Revenue grew 4%" as upper-funnel brand advertising declined. [Shopify transcript](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/shop/transcripts/725032-q3-2026/) · [HubSpot transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/11/05/hubspot-hubs-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/) · [Snap's prepared remarks](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1564408/000156440826000063/snapincq32026preparedrem.htm)

### AppLovin's $2.18 billion quarter topped five ad tech rivals combined

AppLovin's Q3 revenue rose 55% (reported Nov 4), while Teads fell 26%, Criteo fell 11%, and Magnite's CTV rose 39%; Magnite's Michael Barrett said, "The numbers would be much stronger if it were more of a search generated traffic world as opposed to answer engines." ### The New York Times and three Tribune papers sued Brave, alleging it swaps publishers' ads and supplies AI companies

The Nov 2 complaints in New York federal court allege that Brave's browser removes publishers' ads to show users "Brave's own ads instead" and that Brave supplies "many of the most prominent GenAI companies." We found no response from Brave.
