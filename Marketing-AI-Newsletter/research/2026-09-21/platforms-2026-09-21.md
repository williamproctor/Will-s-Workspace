## Google Search / AI Mode
- **AI contribution pilot** (details reported Mon Sept 14): invite-only Search Console program that pays when content "contributes significantly to the creation of AI-generated responses" in the Gemini app, AI Overviews, and AI Mode. Help text: "AI contribution focuses on the phase of generation where web content can significantly influence what response is generated. Web content may also confirm facts or be linked to within Google's [AI] after a response is generated, but those instances don't qualify." Earnings widget shows a monthly number with some history, no calculation method; payments usage/value-based, no upfront fee; opt out anytime; dozens of publishers approached, beyond news. Google called it an early-stage learning pilot and pointed to a June post on rewarding content that grounds responses. SER first spotted the pilot in April. Digiday: critics say it "looks less like a meaningful payout and more like a legal fig leaf." Origins: [Digiday, Sept 14](https://digiday.com/media/google-rolls-out-pay-per-use-ai-licensing-program-to-publishers/) · [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-al-contribution-pilot-42076.html) · [MediaNama, Sept 17](https://www.medianama.com/2026/09/223-google-ai-pilot-publishers-responses/).
- **Sponsored text-link ads inside AI Mode answers (test, ~Sept 15):** ads rendered as anchor-text links in the answer with a "Sponsored" label above; spotted by Sachin Patel; follows April's organic anchor-text links. Barry Schwartz doubts CTR vs richer units. Origins: [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ai-mode-text-link-ads-42082.html) (URL per Schwartz's own post) · [NetContent analysis](https://netcontentseo.net/article/google-is-testing-sponsored-links-that-look-like-native-citations-inside-ai-mode-answers-815).
- **EEA remedy spreads (Sept 18):** free product listings and "popular products" carousels removed across the EEA, replaced by "Comparison Sites" aggregator units; Hugo Huijer (Productrise) measured ~90–100% drops (Germany, France, Belgium, Sweden, Netherlands); Adriaan Dekker: "The space is going to price comparison services (CSS) instead"; Ginny Marvin confirmed removal was due to the DMA. Local business queries added to aggregator/supplier units the same day (Search Central docs updated Sept 18); directories need approval + a POI feed; direct businesses appear in the supplier unit without extra data. Origins: [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-drops-free-product-listings-eea-42113.html) · [Productrise](https://productrise.app/blog/product-carousels-disappearing-from-google-europe-eea) · [SEJ on local](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-adds-local-businesses-to-eea-search-result-units/589955/) · [aggregator unit docs](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/aggregator-unit).
- **Scraper/tracker blocking + volatility:** Google "may be more successful in blocking AI scrapers, search scrapers and third-party tracking tools since around September 13"; Glenn Gabe (X, Sept 18) on rank-tool data diverging from reality for a site that dropped ~100% and recovered. Ranking volatility "unusual" since Sept 4, spiking again this week (no confirmed update). [SER](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-blocking-scrapers-and-tracking-tools-42118.html)
- Other (SER Sept 18 recap + Friday video text): Search Live powered by Gemini 3.8 Live; Search profiles redesign (account management, badges); local knowledge panels becoming AI Overviews; AI Mode button in the SERP search bar (test); Mueller: Search Console position reporting will "evolve over time"; Discover missing on desktop; adult products must be labeled in feeds; Merchant Center API supports product reviews.

## Microsoft
- **AI-generated ad rules (SEL, Mon Sept 14):** disclose AI-generated/materially altered synthetic content when law or policy requires, clearly and close to the content (ideally embedded in the image/video); preserve watermarks, metadata, C2PA/Content Credentials; ads can be rejected/removed for prohibited deepfakes, impersonation, unauthorized likeness/voice, missing disclosures, or interfering with provenance data; Microsoft's own AI tools embed imperceptible watermarks that don't satisfy consumer-facing disclosure. Origins: [SEL](https://searchengineland.com/microsoft-advertising-sets-rules-for-ai-generated-ads-488429) · [TechWyse](https://www.techwyse.com/news/platform-updates/microsoft-ai-ad-guidance-disclosures-advertiser-responsibility).
